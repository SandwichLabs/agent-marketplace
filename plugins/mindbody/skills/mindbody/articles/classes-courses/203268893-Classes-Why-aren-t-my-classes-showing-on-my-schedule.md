# Reasons your classes may not show on your schedule

Troubleshooting checklist for a class that is missing from the business or consumer schedule. Work through the causes in order.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true (Class Schedule)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_tg_tabs_v2.asp (Client View Settings)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/settings/closed-days (Closed days)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_tg.asp (Service categories)
- Menu path: **Classes**; **Settings > General > Client View Settings**; **Settings > General > Schedule a Closed Business Day**; **Settings > Classic Setup > Service Categories**

## Steps
### 1. Class has expired (dates)
1. **Classes**, click the class name (Edit Scheduled Class/Event).
2. If a room is assigned and End date is grayed out, click **Clear all Rooms** under Regularly assigned room.
3. Extend **End date**, **Save**; optionally reassign the room and **Save** again.

### 2. Hidden cancellations
1. On Classes click **More > View Hidden Class Cancellations**.
2. For a missing class, three-dot menu in Edit column > **Undo class cancellation**.
3. Open the class, scroll to bottom and click **Delete class cancellations**, then **OK**.

### 3. "Show to public" off
1. Open the class, tick **Show to public** (top right), **Save**.

### 4. Consumer-site screen assignment
1. **Settings > General > Client View Settings**, scroll to Custom Views, open **Service Categories** dropdown and ensure the category is ticked for the right screen, **Save**.

### 5. Business-site screen assignment
- Assign the service category via the navigation menu **Edit** option.

### 6. Closed day with no description
1. **Settings > General > Schedule a Closed Business Day**.
2. Trash-can any closed day lacking a description, re-add it with a description.

### 7. Schedule filters
- Check the right-hand filters on Class Schedule: all service categories, class types and instructors should be selected.

### 8. Service category type changed
1. **Settings > Classic Setup > Service Categories**.
2. Set the correct **Service category type**, **Save** (e.g. a class category switched to course type shows on the wrong screen).

## Gotchas
- A closed day without a description renders the whole schedule blank.
- Check date range and weekday match (a Monday class starting on a non-Monday date first appears on the next Monday).
- Classes hidden from public only show when logged in (as owner).
- Visible on web/app but not in the Mindbody Business app: class may be assigned to a deactivated (e.g. duplicate) staff profile; reassign the teacher.
- Livestream classes, and classes with "Live", "Online", "Virtual" in the name, do not appear in the Mindbody app.
