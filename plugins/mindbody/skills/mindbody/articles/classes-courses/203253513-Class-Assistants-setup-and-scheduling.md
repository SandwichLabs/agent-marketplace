# How to set up and schedule assistants for classes and courses

Lets a class or course have up to two assistant staff in addition to the teacher, with their own pay. Follow the steps in order: enable, mark eligible staff, set pay, assign.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_res.asp (Class and Course Options)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff (Staff)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true (Class Schedule)
- Menu path: **Settings > Services > Class and Course Options**; **Staff**; **Classes > Sign In**

## Steps
### 1. Enable the feature
1. Open Class and Course Options.
2. In **Booking and Sign-in Policies** tick **Assistant** (and **Assistant2** for a second).
3. Optional display: in **Class Schedule Appearance & Options** tick **Consumer Mode - Show Assistant** / **Show Assistant 2**; in **Course Schedule Appearance & Options** tick **Consumer Mode - Show Assistant 1** / **Show Assistant 2**.
4. Click **Update**.

### 2. Mark staff eligible
1. **Staff** > click the person.
2. On Staff Profile tick **Assistant** (and **Assistant2** if they serve as second assistant) under Settings, **Save**.

### 3. Set default pay
- In Class and course pay rates, set **Rate 1 - Assistant Rate** under per-class pay rates and make it the assistant's default rate.

### 4a. Assign for a single day
1. **Classes > Sign In** on the class.
2. Pick from the **Assistant** dropdown (optionally **Assistant2**), click **Update**.

### 4b. Assign across multiple days
1. Open Edit Scheduled Class/Event for the class/course, click **Make Schedule Change** at the bottom.
2. Choose the type of change and the start/end dates.
3. Tick **Assistant change**, pick **New assistant** (optionally **Assistant 2 change** / **New assistant 2**).
4. Click **Submit**.

### Make assistant pay come out of the teacher's pay
1. Open the class or course from **Classes** or **Courses**.
2. Tick **Paid by teacher**, click **Save**.

## Gotchas
- Assistant default rate applies even to other pay structures; if an assistant also teaches, manually assign them a different rate for those classes.
- Assistants get no per-head bonus and are ineligible for the No Registrations Rate on Rate 1 - Assistant Rate.
- With **Paid by teacher**, the Assistants report is unchanged but the Payroll report shows the deduction in the teacher's Assistant Pay column and the assistant disappears from payroll results (example: teacher $25, two assistants $10 each, becomes $5 net).
- Assistants stay bookable for appointments; manually add unavailable time.
- Assistants do not receive automated notifications; only the teacher does.
- Class assistants show on consumer site and branded app; course assistants show only on the consumer site.
