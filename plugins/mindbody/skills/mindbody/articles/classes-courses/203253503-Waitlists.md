# How to use waitlists for classes

Waitlists let clients queue for a full class and get moved onto the roster when someone cancels. Use this to turn waitlists on, size them per class, and manage who gets promoted.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_res.asp (Class and course options)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true (Class Schedule)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/servicesandpricing/Classes
- Menu path: **Settings > Services > Class and Course Options > Booking & Sign-in Policies**

## Steps
### Enable waitlists (site-wide)
1. Open **Class and Course Options** (search in Settings or Services menu).
2. Expand **Booking & Sign-in Policies**.
3. Tick **Enable Waitlists**, review the extra waitlist settings below.
4. Click **Update**.

### Give an existing class a waitlist (option 1: Class Schedule)
1. Click **Classes** in the left nav.
2. Click the three-dot menu on the class > **Edit class setup**.
3. Fill **How many can waitlist?**, click **Save**.

### Option 2: Services & Products
1. **Services & Products > Classes**, click the "N scheduled" link next to the class.
2. Click the date range of the schedule to open Edit Scheduled Class/Event.
3. Fill **How many can waitlist?**, click **Save**.

### Waitlist a client from the roster
1. Open the class's Class Sign In screen.
2. Search/add the client in **Search for client**.
3. When the class-is-full pop-up appears, choose **No, waitlist instead**.

### Manually promote a client
- On the Class Sign In screen waitlist section, click **Add to class** beside the client.

## Settings & fields
(all in Booking & Sign-in Policies)
- **Cancellation Windows**: minutes before start after which clients cannot early cancel; gives waitlisters time to arrive. Cancelling a waitlisted booking does not show as a cancellation in visits or the Cancellations report.
- **Late Cancellation Window Automation**: choose **Start "First to Claim" Automation** (SMS blast to everyone on the waitlist; first to reply gets the spot) or **Continue Auto-add Automation** (auto-add in waitlist order). Operates from the cancellation window until class start or the lock window.
- **Waitlist Lock Window**: cut-off before class when new waitlist joins stop; clients must call, and staff booking gets an override-capacity prompt. Does not auto-move people already waitlisted.
- **Number of Overlapping Waitlists Allowed**: how many same-time waitlists one client may join (cannot cap total waitlists).
- **Allow Clients to Waitlist Classes/Courses that overlap existing reservations**: lets clients waitlist a class while booked in another at the same time; can cause attendance churn, only for sites with many simultaneous classes.
- **Enforce settings of "Client Unpaid Scheduling Restrictions" for the Waitlist**: if disabled, clients may be promoted as unpaid; if enabled, promotion requires a valid pricing option (subject to documented exceptions).

## Gotchas
- Staff need the **Make reservations** permission to waitlist or promote; promoting a client with no valid pricing option needs **Make unpaid reservations**.
- Auto-promotion only happens when the class is outside the cancellation and waitlist lock windows and the client has a valid pricing option; otherwise they stay and (unless opted out) get a text.
- When online capacity is reached, online signups go to the waitlist even if total capacity has free spots; staff can fill those manually.
- Two-way SMS requires meeting SMS country requirements.
- Promoted clients show in gray on the roster until the **Waitlist Notified** checkbox is marked (SMS delivered); email-only clients must be confirmed manually. A red asterisk next to **Sign In** on the schedule means a roster client is unconfirmed.
- A session is deducted when a client is promoted.
- Disabling **Enable Waitlists** hides existing waitlists and moves current waitlisters onto the roster as spots open; it does not delete them.
- Duplicate waitlist signup for the same class is blocked.
- Members can be given priority waitlisting in membership setup.
