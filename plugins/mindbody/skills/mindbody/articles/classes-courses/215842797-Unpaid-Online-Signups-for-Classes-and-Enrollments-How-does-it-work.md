# Unpaid online signups for classes and courses

Lets clients reserve a class/course online without paying at booking (pay in person, or let a membership/autopay cover it). By default payment is required online; this article shows the three switches that must be set.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_res.asp (Class and Course Options)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass (Class schedule)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_enroll.asp (Course schedule)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_membership.asp (Membership Settings)
- Menu path: **Settings > Services > Class and Course Options > Booking & Sign-in Policies**; **Settings > Clients > Membership Settings**

## Steps
### 1. Choose who may book unpaid (site-wide)
1. Open Class and Course Options, expand **Booking & Sign-in Policies**.
2. Set **Client Unpaid Scheduling Restrictions**: Allow All Clients / Allow Members Only / Allow Clients with AutoPays Only / Allow Members or Clients with AutoPays Only.
3. Click **Update**.

### 2. Enable per class or course
1. **Classes** or **Courses** > click the name.
2. On Edit Scheduled Class/Event tick **Unpaid online signups**, **Save**. Repeat per class/course.

### 3. Allow specific memberships (member options only)
1. **Settings > Clients > Membership Settings**, pick a tier in **Select Membership Tier**.
2. In Members Scheduling tick **Allow Clients in this Membership to Schedule Unpaid**, **Save**.
(Option only appears if "Allow Members Only" or "Allow Members or Clients with AutoPays Only" is chosen.)

## Settings & fields
- Member option: members can book unpaid even outside their membership's service category.
- AutoPay option: class must be in the same service category as the pricing option on their autopay. Autopay options add a checkbox "Limit unpaid bookings to client's contract allowance."
- Consumer site shows **Make a single reservation** (buy pricing option) and **Register as unpaid** (no later purchase prompt); button labels renamable on Words and Phrases.
- Mindbody app, branded app and branded web tools show only **Book**; with unpaid signups enabled the client is booked unpaid automatically.

## Gotchas
- If **Hide Sign Up buttons from clients without a current series** is on, clients with no pricing option cannot sign up unpaid at all.
- Clients with a future scheduled suspension cannot book beyond its start date.
- Unpaid clients on a waitlist may be added to the class.
- Enable **Automatically Reconcile Unpaids** (General Setup & Options) to auto-clear unpaid visits when payment runs.
- Cross-regional sites: unpaid signups not supported with memberships bought at another location.
- Mindbody app booking needs at least one online pricing option for the class, even if payment is not required.
- Not supported on the Mindbody web app.
- Unpaid visits must be reconciled or charged later (Unpaid Visits report / Point of Sale).
