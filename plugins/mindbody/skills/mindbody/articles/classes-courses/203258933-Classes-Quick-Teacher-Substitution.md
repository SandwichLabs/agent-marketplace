# Quick Teacher Substitution

Swap the instructor for a single class occurrence directly from the Class Schedule when the regular teacher is out. The next day the normal teacher returns automatically.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/appshell/shuttle/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_res.asp (Class and course Options)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true (Class Schedule)
- Menu path: **Classes > More > Class and course Options > Class Schedule Appearance & Options**

## Steps
### Enable (one time)
1. **Classes > More > Class and course Options**.
2. Expand **Class Schedule Appearance & Options**.
3. Tick **Enable Quick Teacher Substitution**, click **Update**.

### Substitute a teacher
1. Open **Classes** and find the class.
2. Tick the checkbox in the **Instructor** column.
3. Choose the substitute from the dropdown.
The change shows on the substitute's schedule and covers only that one session.

## Gotchas
- Substitute is paid at their default pay rate. To use a different rate, make a temporary change on the Make Schedule Change screen instead.
- Needs the **Manage class/events schedules** permission.
- Enabling it also affects substitute-footnote display on the business schedule (footnotes require it off).
- Only affects one date; multi-date substitutions use Make Schedule Change.
