# Prerequisites for classes and courses

Prerequisites restrict who can sign up for a class or course (e.g. require an orientation first). Three kinds: client-type prerequisites, auto-assigned client types, and age limits.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/servicesandpricing/Classes
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/servicesandpricing/enrollments
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true
- Menu path: **Services & Products > Classes** or **Courses** > class name > **Additional options**

## Steps
### Add/edit on an existing service
1. **Services & Products > Classes** (or **Courses**), click the class/course name.
2. Click **Additional options**, edit prerequisites, **Save**.
(Alternative from the schedule: click name, then **Edit this Class/Event Description**, then **Additional options**.)

### Remove a client type prerequisite from the class schedule
1. **Classes**, three-dot menu > **View client sign-up restrictions**.
2. Click **Client Types Prerequisites**, **Remove x** beside the one to drop, **Save**.

### New services
- Under **Additional options** while creating: Prerequisite notes, Prerequisite client types, Auto-assigned client types, Age prerequisite (min and max).

## Settings & fields
- **Prerequisite client types**: only clients with the type can book online. If several are set, **Require All Prerequisite Client Types** (Class and course Options) decides all-vs-any (off = any one suffices).
- **Auto-assigned client types**: given when a client is marked **Signed in** on the class; booking alone does not assign it; early-cancelling after sign-in does not remove it.
- **Age prerequisite**: min/max age; clients must have a birthdate on file. Requires **Children's Program Features** on in General Setup & Options.
- **Prerequisite notes**: informational text.

## Gotchas
- Client types must be created first.
- Staff can still register clients who do not meet the prerequisite; only online booking is blocked.
- Applies to advance courses containing prerequisite courses.
- When one client books for another, the logged-in booker's account must meet the prerequisites (e.g. parent for child).
- Changes affect new reservations only.
- Works in the software, Mindbody app, branded web tools, and branded apps v4.0+.
- Auto-assigned types removed manually if a client is cancelled after sign-in.
