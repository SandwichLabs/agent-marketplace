# How to allow staff members to sign clients up for classes as unpaid reservations

Short how-to for enabling the **Make unpaid reservations** permission so a staff group can register clients into classes without prepayment. Use it when staff get blocked from booking a client who has no valid pricing option.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_staff.asp
- Menu path: **Settings > Staff > Staff Permissions** (or search "Staff Permissions")

## Steps
1. Open Staff Permissions.
2. Pick the permission group from the dropdown.
3. Expand **Classes & Courses**.
4. Tick **Make unpaid reservations**.
5. Click **Update**.

## Gotchas
- Applies per permission group; members need to log out and back in for changes to take effect (general permissions behavior).
- Package-dependent; permission editing is not available on every software package.
- Unpaid reservations leave unpaid visits that must later be reconciled.
