# Automated lead distribution in the sales pipeline (Lead Management)

Round-robin assignment of new leads to sales staff the moment they enter the Sales Pipeline. Use it to configure who receives leads.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: Sales Pipeline > **Settings** (top right) > **Lead Distribution**

## Steps
### Turn on/off
1. In the Sales Pipeline click **Settings**, then **Lead Distribution**.
2. Use the toggle next to **Enabled** (on by default).

### Include or exclude staff from assignment
1. Tick the staff in the Assigned Sales Staff Members list and click **Move to Available Staff** to exclude them (a "Member(s) successfully moved" message appears); or tick them in the Available list and click **Move to Assigned Staff** to include them.
2. In the Assigned list, drag the six-dot handle to change order. Use the search bar to find someone across the lists.

## Settings & fields
- **Assigned Sales Staff Members** list: staff who get new leads in sequence. All active sales staff start here.
- **Available Sales Staff Members** list: eligible but not currently receiving leads.
- **Deactivated Sales Staff** list: deactivated staff.

## Gotchas
- Feature depends on software package. Editing needs the staff permissions "Ability to work the leads from Sales Pipeline" and "Sales Pipeline setting".
- Assignment cycles one by one down the Assigned list and loops back to the top; new sales staff are added at the top.
- At least one staff member must remain in Assigned; removing all triggers a "Keep at least one assigned sales staff member..." warning.
- Deactivating a staff member redistributes their leads equally among the Assigned list and moves them (greyed) to Deactivated; reactivated staff return to the top of Assigned.
- Enabling the **Rep 1** checkbox on a Staff Profile puts them atop the Assigned list; disabling Rep 1 removes them from all lists.
- Name/detail edits on staff propagate to the lists.
- The Utility Messages add-on mentioned in the source (automated SMS, only France, Germany, Netherlands, Spain, Switzerland) is tangential to this feature.
