# Performance reports (Marketing Suite)

How to see send, open, click, bounce and unsubscribe stats for Marketing Suite automations and campaigns. Use when judging which messages perform or draw spam complaints.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link (Automations): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/marketingsuite/automations
- Direct link (Campaigns): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/marketingsuite/campaigns
- Menu path: **Marketing > Marketing Suite** (Booker: **Marketing > Automations**)

## Steps
### Automation performance
1. **Marketing > Marketing Suite**, click **Manage** beside Automations.
2. Click **Performance** under the automation.
3. Summary is at top; per-email/text Breakdown below. Dropdown top right chooses 7, 30 or 60 days.

### Campaign performance
1. **Marketing > Marketing Suite**, click **Manage** beside Campaigns.
2. Beside the campaign click **Actions > Performance**. Summary on top, opened links at bottom.

## Settings & fields
Automation metrics:
- **Sent**: emails plus texts combined.
- **Opened**: email open rate only; shows N/A for texts.
- **Clicked**: link clicked in email or text.
- **Bounced**: failed deliveries.
- **Unsubscribed**: unsubscribes after opening.

Campaign metrics:
- **Total Eligible** (contacts matching criteria), **Sent**, **Excluded** (skipped, e.g. already emailed in last 24 hours), **Opened**, **Clicked**, **Bounced**, **Unsubscribed**.

## Gotchas
- Feature depends on package/add-on. Mindbody staff need the "View and manage the Marketing tab" permission; Booker has its own optional Marketing permissions.
- Data can lag up to 24 hours after the Marketing Suite daily sync.
- A spam-complaint alert icon appears in the Breakdown beside the offending message.
