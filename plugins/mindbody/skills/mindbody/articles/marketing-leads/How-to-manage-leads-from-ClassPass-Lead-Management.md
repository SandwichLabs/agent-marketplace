# How to manage leads from ClassPass (Lead Management)

Turn the ClassPass lead channel on or off so ClassPass users flow (or stop flowing) into the Sales Pipeline.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Marketing > Lead Management** > pipeline **Settings** > **Lead Channels** section (path inferred from other Lead Management articles; this article says only "Sales Pipeline Settings")

## Steps
### Enable
1. Open Sales Pipeline Settings and find the **ClassPass** channel in Lead Channels (disabled by default).
2. Click the toggle beside ClassPass, then **Activate**.

### Disable
1. In **Settings**, click the toggle beside ClassPass, then **Deactivate**.

## Gotchas
- Off by default, so no ClassPass leads arrive until activated.
- Deactivating only stops new ClassPass leads from entering; the source says nothing about existing cards.
