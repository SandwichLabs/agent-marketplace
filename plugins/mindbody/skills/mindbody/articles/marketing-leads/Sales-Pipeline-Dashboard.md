# Sales pipeline dashboard overview (Lead Management)

Tour of the Lead Management board: stage columns, lead cards, sorting, filters, bulk moves and reassignments, daily digest, and automation features. Use when working leads through a sales funnel.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/lead-management
- Menu path: **Marketing > Lead Management**

## Steps
### Quick-Start Guide
1. Click **Quick-Start Guide** (upper right). **Start Tour** runs a 60-second walkthrough; chapters expand into setup steps with status badges (Connected / Not Connected).
2. Per step, **Watch Demo** previews without changing anything (close with X); **Set up** jumps to the relevant setting. Progress is saved.

### Daily digest
1. Click **View Now** to see recommended leads and pipeline updates; click a lead to open it, X to return; **More Leads** / **View less** to expand/collapse; **Won** shows recently won leads.
2. Enable/disable: click **Go to Settings** in the digest, or the **Settings** gear on the dashboard, toggle **Daily Digest**, click **Save**.

### Sort
- Click **Sort by**: Pipeline: Date Added, Stage: Date Added, Goal, Assigned To, Follow-up Overdue, Lead Score. Then **Order** Ascending/Descending.

### Move cards
- Drag and drop a card between columns.
- Bulk move: dot menu next to pipeline name > **Move Leads** > choose Source Pipeline and Source Stage > **Select Destination Stage** > Destination Pipeline and Stage > **Move Leads** > verify counts > **Yes, Confirm** > **Go to the destination pipeline**.

### Bulk reassign
1. Dot menu > **Reassign Leads**.
2. Pick staff to take leads from, a **Date created** filter (All Dates or range), and **Pipeline stage**.
3. Pick staff to assign to (distributed equally), check the lead count, click **Assign Leads**, then **Go back to sales pipeline**.

## Settings & fields
- **Default stages**: New Lead, Contact Made, Trial Started, Trial Completed, Won (purchase made), Abandoned. Stage names can be customized.
- **Smart Defaults banner** (individual studios only, not enterprise or template sites): auto-applies a prebuilt pipeline per business type and enables default triggers. **Customize** opens Set Trigger. Editing triggers or won criteria removes the banner. Default triggers: first class/appointment/visit booked, then completed; inactivity moves to Abandoned after 60 days (New Lead, booked) or 45 days (completed).
- **Lead card fields**: Name, Lead score, Goal, Assigned to, Intro Offer, Days left on trial, Total visits, Last visit, Next visit, Next follow-up, Won on, Sold, Abandoned on; tooltips show created, last updated, trial period, contact details.
- **Filters** (saved between sessions): Search for lead (name, email, phone), Assigned to, Type (all/lead/follow-up tasks), Follow-up status, Date Range (default Last 7 days; also hour, today, 30, 90, all, custom), Lead channel, Location (multi-location only).
- **AI/ML lead score**: 0-100%, labeled likely/may/not likely to convert; for Trial Started and Trial Completed leads; refreshed every 24 hours.
- **Automated lead distribution** assigns new leads to staff on entry. **Automated stage progression** triggers are off by default.
- **Closed-won with pays-for**: when one client pays for another, the recipient's lead moves to Won.
- **Cross-regional (enterprise)**: **Don't track as a new lead** checkbox stops visitors from other studios creating leads; leads are per location.

## Gotchas
- Permissions: Sales Team Management > Ability to work the leads from Sales Pipeline and Sales Pipeline setting.
- Digest visibility: managers/owners see all leads; staff only unassigned and their own.
- Columns show up to 50 cards; scroll for more. Newest on top.
- Moving leads (drag or bulk) does not fire the destination stage's trigger.
- Cannot manually move to Won unless lead conversion criteria are set; if forced, a prompt offers adding the sold pricing option to criteria (**Yes, include it in my criteria**).
- Moving out of New Lead may prompt to complete Account Details.
- Bulk reassign excludes Won and Abandoned leads.
- Lead counter includes Won and Abandoned; with filters it shows matches out of total.
- Features depend on package.
