# Messenger overview

Messenger is the real-time SMS and web-chat tool for talking to clients and staff. Use this page to find how to open it and what the main Messages screen contains.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://messenger.mindbodyonline.com/login
- Menu path: **Messenger** icon at the top right of the Mindbody site opens a widget; its expand icon opens the full **Messages** screen.

## Steps
### Log in directly
1. Open https://messenger.mindbodyonline.com/login.
2. Enter the Messenger username and password.
3. Click **Sign in with Messenger**.

### Open from the Mindbody site
1. While logged into the Mindbody site, click the **Messenger** icon (top right). A widget appears at bottom right.
2. Choose **Live Chats** or **My Queue**, or click the expand icon to open the full Messages screen. Close icon dismisses the widget.

## Settings & fields
- **Live Chats**: active staff-client conversations; the bot is off for these.
- **My Queue**: chats flagged as needing a human; the bot keeps replying meanwhile.
- **Inbox sections** on Messages: Live Chats, My Queue, Today's Chats, Archived.
- In-chat tools: emojis, secure credit-card links, contact cards, saved replies, attachments. Chat actions: mark resolved/unread, print transcript, delete messages. Client chats show a profile panel with details and visit history.
- Typical uses: change appointments by text, follow up leads, post-visit check-ins, collect/update cards, broadcast to clients or staff, send files, saved replies.

## Gotchas
- Only available with certain packages or as an add-on.
- Messenger is a different product from Messenger[ai].
- Only managers can change these settings.
- Messenger is auto-activated for the owner login only; other staff need the **Access Messenger** permission.
- Two-way messaging does not work in the widget; use the full Messages screen.
