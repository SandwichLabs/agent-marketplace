# How to set up campaigns (Marketing Suite)

Campaigns are one-time email newsletters (promos, events, news) built from templates and sent now or scheduled. Use this to create, schedule, and review them.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/marketingsuite/index
- Menu path: **Marketing > Marketing Suite**, then **Manage** beside Campaigns.

## Steps
### Create a campaign
1. Click **Manage** next to Campaigns, then **Create Campaign** (top right).
2. Pick a template, or choose **Create template** or **Code your own HTML** to build from scratch.
3. Fill in the Subject Line, or let smart subject lines generate one.
4. Edit the layout: hover a section to use + (add block, top/bottom), arrows (move), x (remove), copy icon (duplicate); Undo/Redo on the right.
5. Block types: text, images, video, text+video, text+image, Button / Link, social link (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter by default, removable), horizontal line, spacer, Offer (adds a pricing option).
6. Images: click an image > **Edit Image** > **Upload Image** (computer, web, or stock library; Unsplash recommended). Optional Image link (makes it clickable) and Image alt text (shown if the image fails).
7. Edit text in place; toolbar offers font size/style, bold, italic, underline, lists, color, hyperlink, remove formatting, merge tag, alignment, background color.
8. Click **Preview and Test** to see it as an inbox would; optionally save as a template.
9. Click **Next** to reach the Schedule screen and set **Audience** (contact lists, or "All marketing email subscribed contacts"), **Campaign Name** (internal only), **Date**, and **Time**.
10. Click **Schedule**, or **Save as Draft and Exit**.

### Manage campaigns
- Click **Actions** beside a campaign. Drafts: Performance, Preview and Test, Duplicate, Edit, Schedule, Delete (permanent). Scheduled: Performance, Preview and Test, Duplicate, Change Schedule, Unschedule.
- List columns after sending: Lists, Sent, Opens, Clicks. **Actions > Performance** gives full stats (sent, bounced, opened, clicked, unsubscribed).

## Gotchas
- Needs permission **View and manage the Marketing tab**; features depend on package.
- Email only; campaigns cannot go by SMS.
- Cap: 200,000 campaign emails per day per location, resetting at midnight.
- Button size is automatic from its text.
- Tip: include your own address in the audience to verify delivery. Review or create smart lists first so the audience is right.
- Templates are mobile-optimized; gray side padding depends on the email client.
- Deleting a draft is permanent; confirm before sending or scheduling to a large audience.
