# How the Marketing Suite works

Conceptual overview of the Marketing Suite (full version on Ultimate 2.0 / Ultimate Plus): what Smart Marketing tools and automations do, and which package gets which features. Read it to decide which tool fits a marketing goal.

## Where in Mindbody
- Smart Marketing tools: **Open more tools** link inside the Marketing Suite.

## Settings & fields
- **Smart Marketing tools**: AI-driven emails/texts, mainly for appointment businesses. You enable each tool and set send frequency, whether a discount is included, eligible services, and eligible recipients. After enabling, a tool does not fire immediately; it weighs factors (how soon the class/appointment is, whether it needs a boost, last visit, recent offers, membership status, etc.), picks the best tool and targets clients likely to respond. Non-responders wait at least a couple of weeks before another, possibly different, offer. Goal: fill next week's calendar.
- **Automations**: you pick smart contact lists (e.g. prospects, new clients, active members), write a message series in the built-in editor, and set the interval between messages. Clients auto-enroll and move between lists as their status changes. Sent to every eligible client (no AI selection). Suited to birthdays, anniversaries, visit milestones. Goal: long-term engagement.
- **What the suite includes**: 2-way SMS for Smart Marketing tools (except package/membership promotions), custom email builder, segmented contact lists, referral and rewards program, on-demand promos, slow-day/last-minute outreach, package and membership upsells, lost-client recovery, automatic feedback and reviews posted to social channels.
- **Package comparison** (summary):
  - All tiers (Starter/Essential, Accelerate 2.0, Ultimate 2.0/Plus): Mindbody app listing, Affiliate Network listing, Dynamic Pricing, Promoted intro offers.
  - Accelerate 2.0 and up: templated engagement emails, smart lists updated in real time; Accelerate has only basic 2-way appointment confirmation texts.
  - Ultimate 2.0 / Ultimate Plus only: automated personalized emails and texts, customizable engagement emails, custom phone number, last-minute offers, full 2-way confirmation texts, feedback prompts, sign-up through SMS, segmentation (purchase/visit, marketing interaction, satisfaction, referral source), rating/review prompts, actionable feedback, referral program, custom triggered surveys, promoted reviews on other sites.

## Gotchas
- Features depend on software package.
- Best practice: add your own email address to campaigns to confirm they actually send.
