# How to create automations (Marketing Suite)

Automations are drip series of emails and/or texts sent to clients on smart/static lists. Clients are enrolled automatically as they become eligible and drop out when they stop qualifying. Use this to build, edit, duplicate or delete one.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/marketingsuite/automations
- Menu path: **Marketing > Marketing Suite**, **Manage** beside Automations (Booker: **Marketing > Automations**)

## Steps
### Step 1: Who it's sent to
1. Click **Create Automation** (top right); in the "New Automation" panel enter a name and description (internal only) and **Save**.
2. Click **Who it's sent to** and choose smart/static lists to include or exclude.
3. Optional: **Preview List** to verify, then **Save**.

### Step 2: What to send
1. Click **What to send**.
2. Choose email, text, or both (applies to the whole automation; cannot be changed later).
3. Design the email in the content editor (templates, or **Code your own HTML**; Smart subject lines can auto-generate titles). Enter the text message beneath it if applicable.
4. Click **+Add Step** for further messages and **Add Delay** to set the gap (e.g. 7 days).
5. **Save**, review, then click **Looks Good, Turn it On!** to activate.

### Preview
1. On the Automations screen click **Preview & Test**, then pick **Preview & Test** again from the dropdown arrow.

### Edit, delete, duplicate
1. On the Automations screen click **Settings** on the automation, click its name, choose **Edit Name/Description**, **Delete**, or **Duplicate** (multi-location businesses can copy to all or individual locations).
2. Duplicates appear within minutes; edit via **Actions > Edit** and fix location-specific details (phone, offers). Copies are independent.

## Settings & fields
- **Send to**: "Contacts on ALL of the lists below" (must be on every list) vs "Contacts on ANY of the lists below" (any one list).
- **Do not send to**: members of any selected list are excluded.
- **Enroll contacts who already meet these criteria**: off by default, so only newly qualifying contacts enter.
- **Only send this the first time contacts meet the automation criteria**.
- Pricing option links can be dropped into emails via the integrated offer builder (not in Booker).

## Gotchas
- Package/add-on dependent; needs "View and manage the Marketing tab" permission.
- Text automations only in US, Canada, UK, Australia, Hong Kong, Singapore. Max 100 automations per location.
- A contact list can belong to only one automation at a time; the error "These lists are already used by [x]" means copy the smart list.
- Timing: Mindbody sends after each sync (up to 4 a day); first message typically 2-6 hours after eligibility, late-evening eligibles go next morning. Booker sends once daily. For exact send times use campaigns.
- Series stops when the series finishes, the client leaves the list (status change, purchase, visit, accepts offer), or opts out.
- Texts go only to opted-in clients; the NOOFFERS/STOP opt-out footer is mandatory and not editable. Email includes a preferences link.
- Text limit is 136 characters for the editable message; links starting with http:// are shortened to 21 characters. Follow-up replies and Smart Marketing offers over text are not customizable. Emojis can be pasted in (render varies).
- Two-way SMS chat needs Messenger[ai].
