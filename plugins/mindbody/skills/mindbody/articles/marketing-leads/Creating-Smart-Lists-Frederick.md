# How to create smart lists (Marketing Suite)

Smart lists are rule-based client segments that auto-update membership; they feed campaigns and automations. Build one when a send must target a specific group.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/marketingsuite/contactlists
- Menu path: **Marketing > Marketing Suite**, then **Manage** beside Contact Lists

## Steps
1. Open Contact Lists, click the **+** icon (top left), choose **Create Smart List**.
2. Pick a filter kind on the right: **Contact Field Filter**, **Interaction History Filter**, or **Contact List Filter** (combine/extend existing lists).
3. Choose the Filter Type / Field Name (or Interaction Type / List).
4. Choose an Operator (e.g. Is, Is not, Contains; options vary by filter).
5. Select or type the Value (e.g. State or Province / Is / CA).
6. Click **Add**.
7. Optional: **and...** adds a narrowing filter; **or...** adds an alternate criterion that widens the list.
8. Name the list and click **Save**.

### Edit, delete, duplicate
1. Open Contact Lists, click the list name. Pencil icon edits name/filters, trash icon deletes, **More** menu duplicates.

## Settings & fields
- Three parts of a filter: filter type/field name, operator, value.
- Contact Lists screen has an email/text eligibility dropdown: Marketing Emails Eligible, Marketing Texts Eligible, Marketing Emails Not Eligible, Marketing Texts Not Eligible, All. Informational only; does not change filters.
- Marketing Communications column: green Text/Email icon = opted in, gray = opted out, red Invalid Email icon = bad address.

## Gotchas
- Requires the "View and manage the Marketing tab" permission; package-dependent.
- Lists refresh with Marketing Suite syncs: Mindbody up to four times daily (three between 7 AM and 7 PM, one overnight); Booker once daily.
- Filter fields only appear once the underlying feature is in use on the site (e.g. liability release).
- Premade lists exist on first visit.
- Pricing options appear in filters only after being sold at least once. Filter by Product External ID rather than name: name-based filters stop updating if the option is renamed. The ID is in the URL when you click the option under Services & Products, or shown bottom-left when hovering the name on the Pricing Options screen.
- Smart lists are not used by Smart Marketing tools (algorithm managed).
- Clients tagged in other reports cannot form a smart list directly. Workaround: tag clients, generate Mailing Lists report for tagged clients, export to Excel, save as CSV, upload to Marketing Suite.
- Marketing is never sent to unsubscribed contacts or invalid emails.
- New lists become selectable in campaigns and automations.
