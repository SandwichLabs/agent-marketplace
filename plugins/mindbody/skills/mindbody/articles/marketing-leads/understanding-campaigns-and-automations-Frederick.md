# Campaigns and automations (Marketing Suite)

Conceptual guide to the two outreach tools: one-off campaigns versus ongoing automations, with example flows. Read before deciding which to build.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/marketingsuite
- Menu path: **Marketing > Marketing Suite > Manage** beside Automations or Campaigns (Booker: **Marketing** > Automations or Campaigns).

## Settings & fields
- **Campaign**: a one-time email (newsletter style) for a broad audience, built from templates and sent manually when there is news, an event, or a sale. Email only.
- **Automation**: a series of messages sent automatically over time to a defined group ("set it and forget it"). Can be email or text.
- **Example, nurture prospects** (never visited or purchased): offer on joining the list, follow-up after 14 days, final offer 30 days later. It stops for a client who buys, visits, opts out, or otherwise leaves the list.
- **Example, first-time visitor**: welcome message, membership offer two weeks later, reminder two weeks after that. A client who buys a first pass leaves the prospect automation and enters this one; a member could then enter a loyalty automation.
- **Customer journey**: Prospect, New Client, Member, Promoter; build smart lists and automations per stage so clients move between series as their status changes and lists update by themselves.

## Gotchas
- Features depend on package or add-on. Mindbody needs the **View and manage the Marketing tab** permission; Booker uses its Marketing optional permissions.
