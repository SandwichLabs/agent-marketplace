# Settings screen overview

Map of the Settings screen, the hub for configuring a Mindbody site. Use it to find which section holds a given setting and which article documents it.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/settings
- Menu path: **Settings** in the left navigation panel.

## Steps
1. Open Settings.
2. Find a setting by typing its name in the search bar at the top, by choosing a section from the Settings menu, or by scrolling the full list.

## Settings & fields
Sections and what they contain:
- **Communications & Marketing**: News & Events, Notifications (auto emails/texts), Links, Appointment Reminders, Waitlist Notifications, Retention (dashboard), Social Media.
- **Staff**: Staff Permissions, Self Clock-In, Time Clock Tasks.
- **Clients**: Membership Settings, Modify Tagged Clients, Locate/Merge Duplicate Clients, Unmask Merged Clients, Cancel Class and Appointment Bookings (mass cancel), Self Check In, Data Privacy, No-Show/Late Cancel Fees, Required Fields, Prospect Stages, Relationship Types, Client Profile Custom Fields, Client Alerts, Client Indexes and Index Values, Client Types, Referral Types/Subtypes, Contact Log Types/Subtypes, Client Statuses, Class Tests, Gender.
- **Inventory**: Purchase Orders, Inventory Tickets, Product Sizes, Product Colors, Suppliers, Payment Terms.
- **Pricing**: Pricing Options, Organize Pricing, Manage Sales (search by Sale ID), Promo Codes, Account Payments, Gift Cards, Contracts, Organize Contracts, Packages.
- **General**: Locations and Mindbody App Listings, Rooms and Resources, Schedule a Closed Business Day, Client Forms and Liability Waiver, Logo and Colors, Contact Information, Account Language, Tax Rates, General Setup and Options, Words and Phrases, Client View Settings.
- **Services**: Class and Course Options, Appointment Options, Suspension Types, Scheduling Increments, Martial Arts Belts, Progress Tracking, ICD Codes.
- **Retail**: Close Out Data, Payment Methods, Revenue Categories/Subcategories, Product Revenue Categories, Cash Register Options.
- **Mindbody Add-ons**: Partner Store (integrations), Premium Services, API Integrations.
- **Classic Setup**: Service Categories, Appointment types and assignments, Appointment Availability, Class Types, Class & Course Management, Manage Schedules, Schedule a Class/Event, Class/Appointment Levels, Advance Course Management, Semesters, Media Management.

## Gotchas
- Each option depends on staff permissions; missing or grayed-out items mean the owner must grant access.
- Some features depend on software package.
