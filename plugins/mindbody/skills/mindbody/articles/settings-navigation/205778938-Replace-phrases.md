# How to set up and use replace phrases

Replace phrases are placeholders in angle brackets (e.g. <STUDIOURL>) that Mindbody fills with real data (service name, appointment time, etc.) in notifications and contract agreement terms. Needed when editing those texts.

## Where in Mindbody
- Notifications: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/settings/notifications
  - Menu path: **Settings** > **Communications & Marketing** > **Notifications**
- Contracts: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/paginatedcontracts/index
  - Menu path: **Services & Products** > **Contracts**

## Steps
### Find phrases for a notification
1. Open **Notifications** and click the email to edit.
2. Expand **Replace Phrase** to see the list valid for that email.
3. Put the cursor in the text and click a phrase to insert it, or copy and paste it.

### Find phrases for contract agreement terms
1. Open **Contracts**; click **+ Add a Contract** (top right) or click an existing **Contract Name**.
2. Expand **4. Add Agreement Terms**.
3. Click **Show available replace phrases**.

## Gotchas
- Each email has its own phrase list; a phrase valid in one email may not work in another.
- Replace phrases work only in notifications and contract agreement terms. Marketing Suite campaigns/automations use merge tags instead.
- Text messages: in the Mobile body text field, phrases must be copy-pasted (clicking the list does not insert them). Limit is 100 characters, or 160 on Accelerate and Ultimate, measured after phrases expand; longer messages may be truncated, split, or fail. Example: <STUDIOURL> counts as 11 characters while editing but expands to the full address.
- Package-dependent features.
