# Understanding Mindbody software packages

Explains the four subscription tiers, add-ons, and why a feature, report, or permission might be missing. Check this first when a menu item or setting does not appear.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: owner login > Mindbody Subscription link on the site to see the current package and billing.

## Settings & fields
- **Packages**: Starter (foundations), Accelerate (email campaigns, smart contact lists, text confirmations), Ultimate (automated marketing, referrals), Ultimate Plus (automated marketing plus a custom branded app).
- **Add-ons** (extra monthly fee; some included in or limited to certain packages): Branded Mobile App, Messenger[ai] (AI front-desk and conversational SMS), Branded web tools (widgets for schedules/appointments on your own site; existing Starter users gain access as their uplift date arrives), On-demand video, Livestream, Insights (real-time metrics across locations and staff). Third-party add-ons via Mindbody Integrations are unsupported by Mindbody.
- **Reports** are tied to the package; a separate comparison article lists which reports each includes.
- **Staff permissions**: Accelerate and Ultimate allow customizing and assigning permissions by permission group; other packages have fixed pre-set permissions.

## Gotchas
- A feature listed for your package but missing from your site is likely due to your country or payment processor.
- Upgrades go through Business Pricing / a Mindbody rep; downgrades go through Contact Support. Read what changes on a package switch beforehand.
- Package changes alter subscription cost; get user confirmation first.
