# How to get started with your Mindbody software FAQ

Quick answers for new users of the business software: where common things live and short recipes for frequent fixes (wrong sale, wrong pricing option, cash for autopay, cancelling classes).

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/linksandshortcuts (Links); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_rpt_mailer.asp?category=Clients (Mailing Lists); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true (Class Schedule)

## Steps and answers
- **Add clients / staff**: see [Adding-Editing-Clients-Mindbody-Experience](../clients/Adding-Editing-Clients-Mindbody-Experience.md) and [Adding staff](../staff/203253673-Adding-Staff-Instructors-Teachers-Stylists.md).
- **Payments setup**: click your name at top right of the login toolbar > **Payments Portal** (apply for and manage merchant account).
- **Website integration**: **Settings > Communications & Marketing > Links**. Support will not code links into a website.
- **Site ID**: shown in the footer after login.
- **Dashboard differs by role**: owners see business metrics; staff see their daily calendar; screens depend on permissions. Messenger is reachable from the dashboard.
- **Find a client**: **Find a client** field next to the magnifying glass at the top, or the Client Directory search.
- **List of all clients**: **Insights > Reports > Clients > Mailing Lists**; first 1,000 rows display, export to Excel for more.
- **Remove a client**: deactivate (see [deactivate a client](../clients/203257863-How-do-I-delete-deactivate-a-client.md)).
- **Family accounts**: a **Manage Family Account** link appears under the Mindbody account section on the client info screen.
- **Intro offer**: Pricing Options screen; mark as introductory and limit to one purchase per client or new clients only; after buying it disappears from the online store and POS for that client.
- **Contracts/autopay**: see [Setting up contracts](../pricing-memberships/203253843-Setting-up-contracts.md).
- **Early bird**: tick **Early Bird Discount** on a pricing option; does not work with contracts.
- **One pricing option for two people**: give the clients a "Shares pricing option with" relationship.
- **Pay for another client**: supported; the sale is in the payer's Purchase History.
- **Autopay paid in cash**: autopay needs a stored card/bank account. Workaround: sell with **Account** as payment method (creates a negative balance), then at POS find the client, click **Receive Payments** (top right of Add Item), confirm/edit the prefilled amount, add item, complete the sale.
- **Fix an incorrect sale** (⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first): search client > **Purchases** > click **Sale ID** (Manage Sales). If visits are attached, click red **In Use**, then **Early** (cancel visits) or **Reassign** payment. Then void via **Yes, Void This Sale**; re-ring correctly at Point of Sale.
- **Exchange an item**: return for credit, then spend it; see exchange article.
- **Move visits to another pricing option**: client **Account Details** > three-dot menu on the pricing option > **Show Visits** > **Reassign Payment** dropdown per visit. Unpaid visit: **Recalculate** at the top; if the pass expired, **Edit** it to extend the expiration/duration to today or later, **Save**, then **Recalculate** again. A used pricing option cannot be voided until visits are moved.
- **Cancel or delete a class**: **Classes** > three-dot menu > **Cancel or delete class**: **Cancel this one class** (roster access, cancellation auto email), **Cancel a block of classes** (date range and weekdays), **Cancel all following**, **Permanently remove class** (only if no one has ever signed up). Cancelled classes can be hidden; use "View hidden class cancellations" from the main gear.

## Gotchas
- Staff permission changes can take up to 15 minutes for logged-in staff; have them log out and in.
- Some screens from the old software differ or are missing in the new experience.
- At-Risk clients on the dashboard come from an ML model: churn = at least one booking in the last 3 months and none in the next 2.
- Features depend on software package.
