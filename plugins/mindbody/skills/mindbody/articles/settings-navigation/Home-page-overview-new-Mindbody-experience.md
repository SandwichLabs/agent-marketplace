# Home dashboard overview

Describes the owner's landing Dashboard: alerts, today's schedule, AI client predictions, Messenger and the Insights snapshot. Read-only reference for interpreting what the home screen shows.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Dashboard** in the left navigation panel (owner's default landing screen).

## Settings & fields
- **Who sees what**: Dashboard is visible to the owner only and cannot be customized. Staff land on the Dashboard Calendar (Staff Schedule) instead.
- **Notifications and alerts** (owner login only): business alerts for expiring intro offers, expiring cards for autopays, failed autopays, plus a collapsible summary of yesterday's metrics.
- **Schedule widget**: toggle between appointments and classes/courses (classes show first if both offered). Appointment status colors: gray completed, blue booked, green confirmed, red no-show, yellow arrived. Clicking a time-slot link opens that appointment or the class/course sign-in screen. Shows which classes have spots or are full.
- **Client AI predictions**: Big Spenders and At-Risk clients widgets. Hover a name and click the **X** to remove a client from the list. Unavailable if the site has a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement.
- **Messenger**: real-time SMS/webchat with clients. Automatically on for owners; staff need the "Access Messenger" permission (Clients section). Opened via the Messenger alerts icon in the login toolbar; Inbox options open the chat dashboard. "Unavailable"/"Not Available" means temporarily down or not yet provisioned (contact Support). Two-way messaging in the widget is noted as coming soon.
- **Insights snapshot** (not on all sites; recalculated nightly; all platforms included): the three-dot menu on a box and the top toggle show or hide breakdowns; "Since last month" compares to same dates last month.
  - **Sales**: total sales, accrual basis, excluding sales tax; "Go to report" opens the Sales report.
  - **Clients**: unique purchasing clients split new vs existing, plus average spend (sales / clients served).
  - **Utilization (appointments)**: Scheduled / Total hours; shows Scheduled, Remaining, Total hours; excludes unavailable time; box shows early plus late cancels with a link to the Cancellations report.
  - **Capacity (classes/courses)**: Booked / Total spots; Booked, Remaining, Total spots. Dropdown switches between utilization and capacity.

## Gotchas
- Package-dependent features.
- Scheduled appointment hours can exceed total hours (negative Remaining) with multi-person appointments or double-booking.
- For recent sales and client activity snapshots, the Client Acquisition dashboard is the alternative.
