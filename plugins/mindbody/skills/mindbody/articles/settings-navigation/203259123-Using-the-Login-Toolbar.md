# Search and Login toolbar overview

Describes the toolbar in the top-right of every Mindbody business page: client search, follow-ups, tasks, messages, Messenger, support links, time clock and the login menu. Useful for navigating, signing out, or reaching account/billing options.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: toolbar at the top of any page after logging in (no separate URL).

## Steps
- **Find a client**: click the **Find a client** field (magnifying glass) and type a name.
- **Contact Log Followups**: click the icon (a red dot shows the count of new assigned followups) to open the inbox.
- **My Tasks**: to-do list for creating and assigning tasks to yourself or other staff.
- **Messages**: click the megaphone icon (blue dot = new) for product and software-update notices.
- **Messenger alerts**: click the Messenger icon to open the alerts box at the bottom of the screen.
- **Support**: click the **?** for Mindbody Support Center, Software Training (Learning Center), Mindbody One Community, and Contact Mindbody Support (email form).
- **Time Clock**: clock in or out; with the right permission, clock other staff in or out.
- **Login menu**: click your name.

## Settings & fields
- Staff login menu: **View Profile** (Staff Profile screen), **Account Information** (change email and password), **Log out of Mindbody Apps** (signs out of multiple Mindbody apps), **Sign Out**.
- Owner login menu: **Account Information**, **Payments Portal** (submit a payment processing application, manage merchant account), **Mindbody Subscription** (view and download subscription invoices), **Log out of Mindbody Apps**, **Location owner** (only with more than one active location), **Sign Out**.

## Gotchas
- Messenger is only on certain packages or as an add-on; it is automatically active for owners and available to staff only with the Access Messenger permission.
- Time Clock behavior for other staff depends on Time Clock staff permissions.
- Availability depends on software package.
