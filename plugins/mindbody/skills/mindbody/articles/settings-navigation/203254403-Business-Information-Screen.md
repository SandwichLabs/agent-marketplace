# Locations and Mindbody App Listing screen

Also called the Business Information screen. Holds each location's address, time zone, and Mindbody app listing details, and acts as a hub to related business settings (contact info, logo/colors, tax rates, client forms).

## Where in Mindbody
- Locations and Mindbody App Listing: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/BusinessAndConnectLocations (**Settings** > **General** > **Locations and Mindbody App Listings**)
- Contact Information: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/businessinfo/ContactInfo (**Settings** > **General** > **Contact Information**)
- Logo and Colors: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/businessinfo/Logos (**Settings** > **General** > **Logo and Colors**)
- Tax Rates: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/businessinfo/TaxRateInfo (**Settings** > **General** > **Tax Rates**)
- Client Forms & Liability Waiver: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/clients/waivers-intakes (**Settings** > **General** > **Client Forms & Liability Waiver**)

## Steps
### Update a location
1. Open Locations and Mindbody App Listing, select the location (multi-location/datashare: select the first location to manage the listing).
2. Edit address, time zone, diversity identifiers, photos, etc., click **Save**.

### Logo and colors
- **Choose Logo** to upload, **Remove Logo** to delete; click header or text color boxes to change consumer-mode colors; **Revert to default colors** clears all color choices.

### Tax rates
- Enter rates as percentages (type 7 for 7%) plus tax registration numbers.

## Gotchas
- Needs the **Business Information Screen** permission (Marketing section). Client forms need **Manage client forms**; without it the option is hidden.
- If the business moved, contact Mindbody support to update owner and location info before changing the address here.
- Only tax rates used by the business show on pricing options and membership setup; multi-location sites show all active rates.
- Contact info and these listing values feed notification defaults and email footers.
- Some features depend on software package.
