# Software Packages features and options (full breakdown)

Reference for which Mindbody package unlocks which feature. Use when a screen, setting, or report is missing and the cause may be the studio's subscription.

## Packages
- **Starter**: foundational tools.
- **Accelerate** (Version 1 = legacy, Version 2 current): adds email campaigns and text confirmations.
- **Ultimate** (Version 1 legacy, Version 2 current): unlimited automated marketing and feedback tools.
- **Ultimate Plus**: Ultimate plus a custom branded app. No longer sold to new customers; existing subscribers (and their added locations) keep it. New customers can buy the Branded Mobile App as an Ultimate add-on.
- Legacy packages (Essential, Pro) are compared in a separate article. Features may vary by region and purchase date. Accelerate V1 and Ultimate V1 are no longer sold.

## Availability (summarized from the tables)
### In every package (Starter and up)
- Scheduling: classes, courses, appointments; waitlists; automated booking/purchase/cancellation notifications (email); formula notes, gender preferences; service-category pricing relationships; custom schedule windows; API integrations; prep/finish times; family accounts; Facebook booking; event payment plans, workshops, certification and kids courses.
- Clients: Client Info/Schedule/Visits/Purchases/Account Details screens; relationships and shared memberships; client indexes; tagging; client alerts, documents, forms; liability waiver; pricing option scheduling restrictions; autopay billing (needs integrated processing); membership tracking; Self Check In.
- Staff/admin: unlimited staff logins; preset manager/staff permissions; Google Calendar integration; Words and Phrases; client view settings (tab management); Links.
- POS: discounts, tax, custom payment methods, split tender; selling contracts; prepaid gift cards (needs integrated processing); SpaFinder cards.
- Marketing/apps/add-ons: Mindbody app listing, Affiliate Network, dynamic pricing, promoted intro offers; Business app, Mindbody app, Check-in app (classes only); branded web tools.
- Caveats within Starter: Manage Sales reachable only from the Sales report or a client's Purchases screen; basic inventory only; products cannot be in contracts; tasks only self-assignable; online store sells services only; Sales Team Management has reduced features (no assigning staff as reps); multi-location management is included with extra locations.

### Accelerate and above (not Starter)
- Block copy / block cancel classes; makeups, semesters, advanced courses, levels, drop-in capacities; resource scheduling; travel sheets; Classic Setup screens.
- Advanced client search by relationship; client types; custom client fields; SOAP/progress notes; club arrival tracking; referral tracking and rewards; martial arts belts; costume tracking; class tests; provider IDs; Client Check In (sign-in screen).
- 1-way/2-way text notifications; WhatsApp (select countries only); 2-way appointment confirmation texts.
- IP restrictions; customizable permissions and permission groups; payroll integrations (ADP, Exact Payroll, Paychex); Sales by Rep report; class assistant scheduling and pay; Google Analytics; cross-regional functionality; SMTP settings; staff nicknames; appointment staff sort order.
- POS: third-party payers; selling packages; advanced inventory; professional products (Ultimate onward, see below); assignable gift cards (needs integrated processing); promo codes; online store with products and services; print barcodes; shipping; customizable number deducted; Media; Quick Cash products.
- Optional add-ons: Data Mart Basic; Utility Messages (France, Germany, Netherlands, Spain, Switzerland; mandatory there for SMS); Messenger[ai]; Insights.

### Ultimate and above
- Professional products.

### Accelerate V2, Ultimate V2, Ultimate Plus (not V1)
- Pick-a-Spot; No-Show/Late Cancel fee management; Progress tracking; Marketing Suite Dashboard; Campaigns; Smart lists.

### Ultimate V2 and Ultimate Plus only
- Lead Management; Automated Substitution Management; Automations; trackable phone number; Fill Last Minute Openings; Boost campaigns; Fill Slow Days; Rescue Lost Customers; Reminder to book again; Limited time specials; automated membership upselling; Feedback and Reviews; Net Promoter Score; automated referral program; review widget; custom "send from" email address; Messenger; Attentive (included).
- Ultimate V2 only: AI Concierge. Accelerate V2: Attentive optional.
- V1 only (Accelerate V1 and Ultimate V1): Retention Marketing Dashboard emails (replaced by Marketing Suite emails).

### Add-ons offered across packages
- On-demand video and Livestream: optional everywhere. Branded Mobile App: not on Starter, optional on Accelerate/Ultimate, included in Ultimate Plus. Messenger[ai] and Insights: not on Starter, optional elsewhere (Insights Dashboard is included in Ultimate Plus).

## Gotchas
- Features needing integrated payment processing (autopay, prepaid/assignable gift cards, online store, direct debit) are limited to the US, Canada, UK, Australia, and Europe.
- For some brand-new Ultimate/Ultimate Plus accounts in US, CA, UK, AU (not HIPAA businesses) Attentive is the default marketing tool and the Marketing Suite menu is hidden.
- **Save This Report** is unavailable on Starter. Report availability per package is in a separate article.
- Existing Starter customers gain branded web tools per their uplift date.
