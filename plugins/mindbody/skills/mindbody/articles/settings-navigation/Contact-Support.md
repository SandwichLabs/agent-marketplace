# How to contact Mindbody Customer Support

In-product help for Mindbody and FitMetrix: an AI Assistant first, with escalation paths to human support. Useful when an agent hits something it cannot resolve itself.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **AI Assistant** button at the bottom left of the logged-in site.

## Steps
1. Click the **AI Assistant** button (bottom left) and ask a question; it answers using the Support Center and the site's subscription profile, and links to product pages.
2. Minimize the window to the bottom-right corner to keep working; the conversation persists while navigating.
3. Click **Take Me There** in a reply to jump to the relevant screen.
4. If unresolved, choose a human option inside the assistant (below).

## Settings & fields
- **Case/Email**: submit a request for follow-up.
- **Live Chat**: offered only in certain situations.
- **Call Me**: request a callback, no hold time.

## Gotchas
- Booker customers use a different route (see Related).
- Other departments are listed in the Department guide and directory.
