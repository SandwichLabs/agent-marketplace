# How to set up emails and texts on the Notifications screen

How to turn on automated client emails/texts (booking confirmations, receipts, birthdays, etc.), enable each one, set reply/copy addresses, and customize content. Separate from Marketing Suite communications.

## Where in Mindbody
- General Setup & Options: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp (**Settings** > **General** > **General Setup and Options**)
- Notifications: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/settings/notifications (**Settings** > **Communications & Marketing** > **Notifications**)

## Steps
### Enable the feature
1. Open General Setup and Options, expand **System Settings**.
2. Check **Notifications**, click **Update**.

### Enable and configure individual emails
1. Open **Notifications**. Search, or sort by name, enabled status, or category.
2. Toggle an email on/off (required emails are locked on).
3. Click an email to edit: fill in **Business reply to name**, **Business reply to email**, optional **Business copy email** (BCCs the business on every send). These override defaults from the Locations and Mindbody App Listing screen.

### Allow HTML in emails
1. General Setup and Options > **System Settings** > check **HTML** > **Update**.

## Settings & fields
- **Business mode Always Send/Default On**: sends automatically unless staff disable it for that action.
- **Use in Business Mode**: staff are prompted whether to send.
- **Copy teacher**: CC/BCC the staff member (needs email on their profile; they only get copies when the client is opted in, except important mails like appointment reminders).
- **Enable email attachment**: adds a calendar-importable file (Outlook, Google, iCal).
- **Replace Phrases** link (per email): list of merge tags valid for that email only.
- **SMS Volume MTD** column: attempted SMS count this month per notification (not success-filtered, resets monthly, no history).
- Default sender: [businessname][siteID]@mindbodyonline.com; change via SMTP settings.
- Footer is automatic: business name (first active location, not editable), address (no line 2), email, phone, website, Unsubscribe, preferences, Terms & Privacy links, "Powered by Mindbody". Pulled from primary active location.

## Gotchas
- SMS depends on country and package; Starter/Essential/Pro email-to-text notifications are deprecated because carriers dropped support. WhatsApp only in selected countries. Utility Messages add-on only in France, Germany, Netherlands, Spain, Switzerland.
- Text versions ignore HTML; links are plain text, clickable depending on device.
- Per-email contact details override the location defaults.
- Some features depend on software package.
