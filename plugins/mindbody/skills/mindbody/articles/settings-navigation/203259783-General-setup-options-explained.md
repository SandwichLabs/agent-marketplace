# General Setup & Options screen: Directory

Index of the big settings screen that controls how the Mindbody site behaves (payroll, scheduling, client handling, retail, online store, policies, payments). Use it to find which section holds a setting, then follow the section-specific article.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp
- Menu path: **Settings > General > General Setup and Options** (left navigation)

## Settings & fields
Sections on the screen, in order, with representative settings:
- **System Settings**: notifications and email format, automated texts, WhatsApp, SMTP, Google Calendar, payroll integration and company code, ignore discounts in payroll, resource scheduling (classes and appointments), Require Multi-Factor Authentication, retail inventory, sales team/rep and contact-log options, promo code generation, member referral discounts, belt/progress tracking, async reporting toggles, analytics IDs (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Tag Manager).
- **Client Management**: account purchases, negative balances (general, autopays, Consumer Mode), reconcile unpaids, client alerting, document uploads/permissions, contract suspension fees, ID cards, invoice display options, client lookup scope, membership/pricing option sharing, pricing option activation date rules (enforce, auto-update, autopays), prospect defaults, auto-deactivation of clients, schedule display, family sign-up experience, minimal user profile.
- **Consumer Mode Settings**: allow new client accounts, billing info storage and autopay billing edits, booking for other clients, scheduling cutoff for same-day services, scheduling hours/start time, show pricing descriptions, purchase history online.
- **Online Store Settings**: online retail, shipping origin and methods, in-store pickup, free-shipping threshold, stock display, out-of-stock handling, charity donations, gift card purchases without login.
- **Business Policies**: liability waiver, cancellation, purchase, terms and conditions, return/refund, privacy policy, welcome text (blank = default).
- **Retail Settings**: POS defaults, gift card options, commissions, contract defaults (Pay Now, prorate rounding, start date restriction, activation offset), receipts/invoices, tips, open tickets, barcodes, third-party payers.
- **Staff Display Options**: use staff nicknames; show staff last names on schedules.
- **Merchant Account Processing Settings**: card swiper, days to resubmit declined autopays, convert declined autopays to negative balances after X days and the associated fee, SmartBatching.
- **Client Rewards Program Settings**: point value, minimum points to redeem, pay-in-full requirement, opt-in, expiration, points earned for purchases/referrals/online bookings.

## Tips
- **Expand All** / **Collapse All** (top right) opens or closes every section.
- Click **Update** to save before leaving, or changes are lost.
- Browser find (Ctrl/Cmd+F) works on the setting names.

## Gotchas
- Some settings only exist with certain software packages.
- Whether you can open this screen depends on staff permissions.
- Many changes affect payments, payroll or client-facing behavior business-wide; confirm with the user before toggling.
