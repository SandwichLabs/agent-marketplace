# How to manage closed business days

Closed business days block new bookings for classes, courses and appointments on given dates (holidays, vacations, repairs). They do not cancel anything already booked.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/settings/closed-days
- Menu path: **Settings > General > Schedule a Closed Business Day** (or search it), or **Classes**/**Appointments** > **More** (top right) > **Schedule a Closed Business Day**

## Steps
### Prepare
1. Run Schedule at a Glance to find existing bookings on those dates.
2. Early cancel those reservations/appointments (and cancel No Registrations Rate classes, or teachers still get paid for them).

### Fully closed day
1. Enter **Start Date** and **End Date**.
2. Enter **Name of closed day / holiday** (max 40 characters).
3. Leave **Service Categories** on **All Service Categories (Business Closed)** (default).
4. Click **Add closed day**.

### Partially closed day
1. Enter dates and name as above.
2. Open **Service Categories**, tick only the categories to close; unticked ones stay open.
3. Click **Add closed day**.
4. Then cancel the specific classes that day and add unavailability to affected staff appointment schedules.

### Edit or remove
1. In the Scheduled Closed Days list click the **Edit** icon, or the **trash can** icon to remove a closed day.

## Gotchas
- Needs owner login or **Manage closed day/holiday scheduling** permission (Settings).
- Existing bookings are NOT cancelled and clients are not notified; cancel first so clients are informed and unavailability/resource schedules are freed.
- Class Schedule Total Capacity reflects the closure only if you cancel the class first, then add the closed day.
- Closing one appointment category shows no visual marker on the Appointment Schedule; booking attempts get a "closed for the day" message.
- Closed days are not displayed in the Mindbody Business app or Messenger[ai] but availability is still blocked.
- Branded app may take 24+ hours to update; for short notice, cancel visits manually.
- Removing a closed day (trash) re-opens booking for those dates.
