# How to edit appointment availability

How to change when a staff member can take appointments: end an old schedule and start a new one, edit blocks on the Appointment Availability screen, or tweak a single day from the Appointment Schedule.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff
- Menu path: **Staff** > staff name > **Appointment Availability**; or three-dot menu beside the name > **Manage Schedules**; or click an empty time on the Appointment Schedule.

## Steps
### Change a schedule going forward (from Staff)
1. Open the staff member's **Appointment Availability**.
2. Click the existing block, set the end date of its **Date Range** to the day before the change, **Save**.
3. Create a new availability starting on the change date with the new start/end times.
4. Set the service categories the staff member offers.

### Edit blocks on Appointment Availability
1. Click a block; use dropdowns for start/end times, service categories, date range.
2. Conflicts show highlighted in red; fix them. Trash icon (on hover) deletes a block.
3. Privacy icon: **Allow clients to see schedule**, **Mask staff member's name**, or **Hide schedule from clients**.
4. **Save**. For a repeating block, choose one day or multiple days when prompted.

### From the Appointment Schedule
1. Click an unscheduled slot and choose edit this day's schedule, edit weekly schedule, or mark unavailable.
2. Day edit: change location, times, services, privacy in the sidebar, or drag block edges; clicking an empty time re-anchors the whole block's start/end. **Save**.
3. Weekly edit opens Appointment Availability. Unavailable links to the unavailability article.

## Gotchas
- Needs **Staff member availability** and **View/edit staff member personal information** permissions (Settings).
- Existing booked appointments are untouched when availability changes; they vanish from view if all availability is removed but return when availability is re-added. If edit is locked after remove/re-add, open Modify Appointment and click Update.
- Appointments are not moved if they fall outside new hours; the staff member and all appointments disappear from the schedule once the last availability is removed. Name shows on the schedule for one month after last availability.
- "Conflict with a schedule on this day" notice can come from old temporary schedules or class/course commitments; end the current schedule the day before and start a new one.
- "Please input a valid date" error: switch Ongoing to Custom dates with a near end date. Custom dates default to a two-week end date.
- Masking even one availability entry masks the name across the branded Appointment widget; confirmations show "Instructor"/"Staff".
- This covers Custom dates; Ongoing availability has its own article.
