# How to schedule staff appointment availability

Staff must have availability on the schedule before anyone can book appointments with them. This covers marking staff as instructors, finding the Appointment Availability screen, and entering availability or unavailability (breaks, vacations).

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/appointment-types
- Menu path: **Staff** > staff name > **Appointment Availability**; or **Staff** > three-dot menu > **Manage Schedules**; or **Services & Products > Appointments > Add Staff Schedules**

## Steps
### Mark staff as an instructor (prerequisite)
1. **Staff** > click the person.
2. Under Settings tick **Instructor/Therapist (for appointments)**, **Save**.

### Open the Appointment Availability screen (any one)
- **Staff** > three-dot menu beside name > **Manage Schedules**.
- **Staff** > name > **Appointment Availability**.
- **Services & Products > Appointments** > **Add Staff Schedules** > **View/edit schedule**.

### Add availability
1. On the staff member's Appointment Availability screen click **+ Add New Schedule**.
2. Fill in the Add Availability form (fields below).
3. Click **Add**, or **Save and Add Next** for another.

## Settings & fields
- **Show staff as**: **Available** (bookable) or **Unavailable** (blocks the schedule).
- **What services does [staff] offer at this time?** (available only): choose service categories.
- **Reason** (unavailable only): e.g. lunch, vacation; may be visible to clients depending on Privacy.
- **Studio**: location; unavailability applies to all locations when multi-location.
- **Date Range**: **Ongoing** (runs effectively forever) or **Custom** (default end is 2 weeks from creation).
- **Days**, **Time** (if a time is missing, adjust Scheduling Increments).
- **Privacy**: **Allow clients to see schedule** (default for availability; shows name and slots, shows unavailability reason), **Mask staff member's name** (not available for unavailability; also removes name from auto emails/texts), **Hide schedule from clients** (default for unavailability; slot hidden on consumer site).

## Gotchas
- Needs the **Staff member availability** permission.
- Availability can be set for one location at a time.
- Service category must be assigned to a custom view (tab) on the consumer site or you cannot add schedules for it.
- Bi-weekly availability is not possible directly: schedule all weeks, then add unavailability for skipped weeks.
- "This schedule conflicts with another existing schedule": existing ongoing (un)availability or the staff member teaching a class then; remove/schedule around, or substitute the class teacher.
- Deleting availability that has booked appointments keeps the appointments but the staff member shows as no longer available; the appointments must be rescheduled/moved before they can be modified or checked out.
- Adding unavailability over an existing appointment triggers a Scheduling Conflict warning; the appointment stays but no new bookings are possible.
- ⚠️ Deleting a schedule is destructive to availability — confirm with the user first.
- Quick Add Staff Availability exists as a faster alternative.
- Terms "Instructors"/"Teachers" can be renamed on Words and Phrases.
