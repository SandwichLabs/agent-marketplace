# How to move an appointment to another staff member's schedule (Modifying Appointments)

Two ways to reassign a booked appointment to a different staff member: drag-and-drop on the schedule, or the Modify screen.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Appointments** (schedule), or appointment > **Modify** > Change Instructor

## Steps
### Drag and drop
1. On the Appointment Schedule, hold the mouse button on the appointment.
2. Drag it onto the other staff member's column and release.
3. Click **Save** in the "Move appointment" pop-up. If the Appointment Change Notification auto email is enabled, **Send Change Notification** is pre-checked.

### Modify screen
1. Click the appointment > **Modify**.
2. In **Change Instructor**, click the new staff member's name.
3. Click **Update and Close** (top right).

## Gotchas
- Checked-out appointments cannot be moved; early cancel and rebook under the other staff member.
- To notify the client, enable the Appointment Change Notification auto email BEFORE moving/cancelling.
- The receiving staff member must have open availability and be assigned to that appointment type, otherwise the move fails.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first if the fallback (early cancel and rebook of a paid visit) is needed.
