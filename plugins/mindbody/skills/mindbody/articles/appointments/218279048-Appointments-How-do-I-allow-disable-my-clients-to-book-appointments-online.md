# How to allow or disable clients to book appointments online

Three levels of control over online appointment booking: per service category, per appointment type, and a site-wide setting that switches between direct booking and request-only.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/appointment-types
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_appt.asp (Appointment Options)
- Menu path: **Services & Products > Appointments**; **Settings > Services > Appointment Options > Appointment System Policies**

## Steps
### Whole service category
1. **Services & Products > Appointments**, click the category's three-dot menu.
2. Click **Allow online scheduling**. A checkmark means online booking is on; click again to remove it.

### One appointment type
1. **Services & Products > Appointments**, click the appointment type name (or use the **Bookable online** toggle in its row: right = on, left = off).
2. On Edit appointment type, answer **Yes** (or **No**) to the question about allowing clients to book it online.
3. Click **Save**.

### Site-wide
1. **Settings > Services > Appointment Options**, expand **Appointment System Policies**.
2. Tick **Allow Client Booked Appointments** (untick to allow only appointment requests).
3. Click **Update**.

## Gotchas
- Unticking **Allow Client Booked Appointments** limits clients to requesting appointments, which staff then manage in Appointment Requests.
- Category toggle affects every type in it; type toggle overrides per service.
- Needs software package support.
