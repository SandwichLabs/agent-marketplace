# Appointments Schedule screen overview

The main appointment calendar: book, modify, cancel, check in/out and check out appointments, and jump to staff availability tools. This is the hub screen for nearly every appointment task.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index
- Menu path: **Appointments** (left nav)

## Steps
### Book (booking mode)
1. Click an open time block; the sidebar asks for a client (search, or **+ Add new client**).
2. Pick service details and book single, recurring or multiple appointments, or rebook a past visit.
3. Optionally email a confirmation (manual send).

### Edit (editing mode)
1. Click an appointment; edit details in the sidebar (waiver status, type, start time, duration, room, prep/finish times, notes, add-ons) and click **Save**.
2. To move: drag the block to a new time; to resize: drag the bottom edge; click **Save**.

## Settings & fields
**Top filters:** **Today**, arrows, **Day** (many staff) / **Week** (one staff), **All locations** (other locations remain visible 60 days after availability ends), **All service categories**, **All instructors** (Day view multi-select; Week view one; only shows staff with availability that date; staff with availability but not marked Instructor appear but are not in the list), click a staff name for their week view.

**More menu:** **Create New Appointment Service**, **Edit Appointment Rates**, **Edit Appointment Options**, **Set Up Notifications**, **Schedule a Closed Business Day**, **Appointment Size** (Auto/Small/Medium/Large), **Refresh Schedule Every** (auto-refresh; disables inactivity logout while active).

**Staff menu (arrow by name):** Go to week/day view, **Edit schedule** (availability and privacy), **Add unavailability**, **Assign appointment types**, **View profile**, bio; **+ Add** quickly adds availability for a staff member.

**Schedule-mode sidebar buttons:** Print schedule (Schedule at a Glance), Check In / Check Out, Find an appointment (also waitlist), Travel sheets, View requests, View waitlists; plus color key (color by status, category or type).

**Appointment action menu:**
- **Checkout**: completes the visit (apply or sell pricing option); status becomes Completed.
- **Retail**: Point of Sale if unpaid; one-click checkout if prepaid/paid.
- **Apply Payment**: deducts a session but does not complete.
- **Early Cancel** / **Late Cancel** (late cancel appears after payment/checkout, deducts a session, status "late cancel").
- **Cancel Remaining**: mass cancels upcoming recurring appointments.
- **Mark Confirmed** (confirmations via Business app or Messenger don't show here) / **Mark Arrived** (still counts as a no-show on Client Visits unless checked out).
- **Travel Sheet**, **Reschedule** (drag into new slot or another staff member's schedule, then **Save**), **Modify** (full Modify Appointment screen), **Prebook/Rebook** (Prebook if today), **Documents**, **Groups**.

**Commonly used Appointment Options:** default Day/Week view, block sizing, instructor order, color coding, max instructors per page, show client names/notes/room/category/type in blocks, service category and gender filters, show appointments and day view in consumer mode.

## Gotchas
- Screen layout depends on staff permissions.
- Classes appear grayed out on this screen; two-instructor classes show only the main instructor.
- Staff with availability in multiple service categories may show unavailability blocks from other categories.
- + Add another appointment may require adjusting the confirmation email replace phrase to get one email per appointment.
- Missing-billing icon only appears if the missing-billing client alert is enabled.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: Checkout, Apply Payment, Late Cancel (deducts a session), Cancel Remaining.
