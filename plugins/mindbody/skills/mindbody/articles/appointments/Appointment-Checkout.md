# How to complete an appointment using the Appointment Checkout feature

Appointment Checkout is a cart that opens on the appointment schedule so staff can pay for an appointment, add other items, rebook, and take payment without leaving the schedule. Use it to complete and charge appointments.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index (Appointment Schedule)
- Menu path: **Appointments** > click the appointment > **Checkout**

## Steps
### Check out an appointment
1. Click the appointment on the schedule, choose **Checkout**.
2. Under Unpaid, use **Select pricing** to choose a pricing option, package or contract (auto-selected if only one fits), then **Add to cart**.
3. Optionally **Edit** or **Remove** the item; add sales rep, tip, promo, extra items.
4. Click **Checkout** (bottom right), review, pick a payment method.
5. On the final screen confirm details and options (split payment, store card), click **Complete Sale**.
6. Optionally tick **Email receipt** / **Print receipt**, then **Done**.

### Add an unscheduled service
1. In the cart click **+Add-on Service**, choose **Service** and **Select staff**, click **Done**.
2. Select pricing for both items, **Add to Cart**; reselect other past/future appointments and **Checkout** to add them to the same ticket.

### Settle other unpaids
1. After adding pricing, click **Settle** in the "Settle this client's other unpaids?" banner (unpaids from last 30 days).
2. Tick appointments, choose pricing for each, **Checkout**. Older unpaids: Client Visits screen or Unpaid Visits report.

### Prebook/Rebook
- Use **Prebook** in the cart sidebar, or from the appointment action menu (**Prebook** for today, **Rebook** for future dates), even while the cart is minimized.

### Gift card payment
1. Choose **Gift card**, enter the number, optionally **Check Gift Card Balance**, click **Next**.

## Settings & fields
- **Payment methods**: cash, check, credit card (saved, app-stored, **Enter card**, **Swipe card**), Account, prepaid gift cards, third-party payers, ACH, custom methods.
- **Split Payment**: splits evenly across selected methods by default; amounts editable.
- **Cart extras**: location (top right, multi-location), **No Sales Rep Assigned** link for sales rep, per-item **Edit** for **Discount percent** / **Discount amount** and **Commission recipient**, **+ Tip** (15/20/25% presets or custom, **No Tip**), **+ Promo**, **Favorites** and **Browse** tabs (Products, Services, Autopays/contracts, Packages, Gift Cards, Account payments, Tips).
- **Windows**: minimize cart to bottom-right to run several carts; X/Cancel or navigating away saves the cart.
- **Receipts**: email option needs the Purchase Receipt (Business Mode) auto email; a typed email applies to that receipt only. Print offers customer copy, signature slip, gift receipt (Ctrl/Cmd+P if no auto print).

## Gotchas
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: **Complete Sale** charges the client. Undoing an already checked-out appointment has a separate article.
- Permissions: Check out appointments, view schedule, view appointment details, Modify appointments, Apply payment, Complete sales transactions at POS, Ability to view all clients. **Run stored credit cards** needed to use saved cards; otherwise enter/swipe manually.
- Appointment Checkout lacks some POS features (e.g. changing sale date, adding new products); use Point of Sale for those. Valutec and assignable gift cards are POS-only.
- Select pricing lists all options assigned to the appointment type, even without an active pricing relationship.
- Cards stored in the Mindbody app appear only in Appointment Checkout, not POS or Client Info.
- Account payment shows only with enough positive balance if **Allow Client Negative Balances** is off.
- Pricing option must be active; extend an expired one first.
- One discount only; discounts and promo codes do not override each other. Product quantity max 10. Contract notes max 225 characters.
- Changing the sale location removes location-restricted items; needs permission to switch locations.
- Contracts sold via Select pricing take the appointment date as start date (not changeable); commissions apply to first payment only; no discount if "Make first autopay free?" is Yes.
- If an appointment type's # deducted is 0, editing price resets it to $0.
- Tip slips print only with Track Appointment Confirmations and Arrivals enabled; tips show as separate line items on Client Purchases.
- If the client already has a prepaid option, **Retail** from the action menu may be quicker.
