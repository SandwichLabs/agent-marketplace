# How to reschedule appointments

Four ways for staff to move an existing appointment: sidebar edit, drag and drop, the Modify Appointment screen, or the Reschedule action (best for moving a client's appointments to another day/location).

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index
- Menu path: **Appointments**

## Steps
### Sidebar (same day)
1. Click the appointment, change options (e.g. start time) in the left sidebar, click **Save**.

### Drag and drop
1. Click and hold the appointment, drag to a new time, release, click **OK**.
- **Day view**: change time or staff on the same day. **Week view**: change day/time within the week, same staff.

### Modify Appointment
- See the Modify Appointment screen article.

### Reschedule action (multi-day / multi-location moves)
1. Click the appointment > **Reschedule** in the action menu. All of that client's appointments for that day load into the left sidebar.
2. Drag an appointment from the sidebar to a new slot; use the calendar, Day/Week buttons, or filters to change days, staff or locations.
3. Repeat for others, then click **Save**.

## Gotchas
- "This staff member is not available for this type of appointment": update that staff member's availability; on multi-location sites check they are not booked at another location.
- Only one client can be rescheduled at a time; navigating away clears unsaved changes. Appointments left in the sidebar return to their original time.
- During rescheduling, staff who don't perform the service temporarily disappear from availability.
- Once dragged out of the sidebar you cannot put it back; drop it into its original slot to revert.
- Appointment reminders send only for the initial booking; with Appointment Change Notification enabled, clients get an updated email (full day itinerary) each time you save.
- Drag-and-drop outside the current week requires the Reschedule action instead.
