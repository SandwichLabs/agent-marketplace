- [How to allow clients to reschedule appointments (Mindbody consumer site)](204244693-Why-can-t-my-clients-reschedule-their-appointments-online.md) — The two settings that must both be on for clients to reschedule their own appointments online. - [How to allow or disable clients to book appointments online](218279048-Appointments-How-do-I-allow-disable-my-clients-to-book-appointments-online.md) — Three levels of control over online appointment booking: per service category, per appointment type, and a site-wide setting that switches between direct booking and request-only.
- [How to allow staff members to only edit their own appointment availability](203270073-How-do-I-allow-my-staff-to-edit-their-OWN-appointment-availability-only.md) — Permission-group change that stops staff from editing other people's availability while still letting them manage their own.
- [How to book appointments](206615788-How-do-I-book-an-appointment.md) — Covers booking single, multiple, recurring and rebooked appointments from the business-side Appointment Schedule, plus two-staff bookings. Use whenever an agent must put a client on the appointment book.
- [How to complete an appointment using the Appointment Checkout feature](Appointment-Checkout.md) — Appointment Checkout is a cart that opens on the appointment schedule so staff can pay for an appointment, add other items, rebook, and take payment without leaving the schedule. Use it to complete and charge appointments.
- [How to edit appointment availability](203259013-Appointments-Changing-a-staff-member-s-availability.md) — How to change when a staff member can take appointments: end an old schedule and start a new one, edit blocks on the Appointment Availability screen, or tweak a single day from the Appointment Schedule.
- [How to manage appointment cancellation](203258403-How-do-I-early-cancel-late-cancel-or-reschedule-appointments.md) — Staff-side procedures for early cancelling or late cancelling an appointment (three entry points), cancelling recurring series, and restoring a cancelled appointment.
- [How to move an appointment to another staff member's schedule (Modifying Appointments)](222455187-I-need-to-change-which-staff-member-took-an-appointment-How-do-I-move-the-appointment-to-another-staff-member-s-schedule-Modifying-Appointments.md) — Two ways to reassign a booked appointment to a different staff member: drag-and-drop on the schedule, or the Modify screen.
- [How to reschedule appointments](Rescheduling-Appointments-From-the-Sidebar-using-the-Reschedule-function-action.md) — Four ways for staff to move an existing appointment: sidebar edit, drag and drop, the Modify Appointment screen, or the Reschedule action (best for moving a client's appointments to another day/location).
- [How to schedule staff appointment availability](203254013-Appointments-Scheduling-staff-appointment-availability.md) — Staff must have availability on the schedule before anyone can book appointments with them. This covers marking staff as instructors, finding the Appointment Availability screen, and entering availability or unavailability (breaks, vacations).
- [How to set up appointment add-ons](203253993-Appointments-Add-ons.md) — Add-ons are extra paid services attached to a main appointment (e.g. hot stones with a massage). They are not separate appointments and do not lengthen the base appointment. Use to create, convert, assign, and staff them.
- [How to set up cancellation restrictions for your appointments](203961876-How-can-I-set-up-cancellation-restrictions-for-my-appointments.md) — Configures cancellation windows so clients can cancel/reschedule appointments online without penalty up to a cutoff; later cancellations become late cancels (charged).
- [How to use the Find an Appointment screen to book or reschedule an appointment](Find-an-Appointment-screen.md) — Alternate search-based way to find open appointment slots when booking a client or rescheduling an existing appointment, instead of clicking on the calendar grid.
- [Modify Appointment screen](226465767-THOR-Modify-Appointment-Screen.md) — Full-detail editor for an existing appointment: change client, instructor, type, date/time/length, room, notes, add-ons, apply payment or check out. Use when the quick sidebar edits on the Appointment Schedule are not enough.
- [Scheduling increments (active business hours for services)](203253593-Scheduling-increments-Active-Appointment-Times-active-business-hours-for-services.md) — Defines which clock times classes, appointments, courses and rooms can be scheduled/booked. The selected times populate start/end dropdowns across the site. Sometimes labeled Active Session Times or Active Appointment Times. If a time is missing from a dropdown or clients see no slots, check here first.
