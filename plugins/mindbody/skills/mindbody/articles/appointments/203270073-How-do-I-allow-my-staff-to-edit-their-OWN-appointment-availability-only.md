# How to allow staff members to only edit their own appointment availability

Permission-group change that stops staff from editing other people's availability while still letting them manage their own.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Settings > Staff Permissions**

## Steps
1. Open **Settings > Staff Permissions**.
2. In the **Staff Permissions** dropdown (top left) choose the permission group to change.
3. Expand **Settings**, find **Staff member availability**, UNtick it.
4. Click **Update** (top right).
5. Expand **Appointments**, find **Edit own info/appointment schedule**, tick it.
6. Click **Update** again.

## Gotchas
- Applies to everyone in the chosen permission group, not one person; create a separate group if only some staff should be restricted.
- Both changes are needed: removing the broad permission alone leaves staff unable to edit anything.
- Requires a software package that supports staff permissions.
