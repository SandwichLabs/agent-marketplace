# How to set up appointment add-ons

Add-ons are extra paid services attached to a main appointment (e.g. hot stones with a massage). They are not separate appointments and do not lengthen the base appointment. Use to create, convert, assign, and staff them.

## Where in Mindbody
- Appointments: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/appointment-types (**Services & Products** > **Appointments**)
- Staff: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff

## Steps
### Create an add-on
1. On the Appointments screen, click **Add** beside a service category name and choose **Add-on**.
2. Fill in the fields below, then **Save** (bottom of screen).

### Convert an existing appointment type
1. Click the appointment type, scroll to Advanced options.
2. Under **Do you want to convert this appointment to an add-on?** choose **Yes**, **Save**.

### Assign an add-on to appointment types
1. Click the add-on, open the **Assigned to** dropdown, search and tick each appointment type, **Save**.

### Edit an add-on
1. Click it, change details on Edit add-on, **Save**.

### Make staff available for it
1. **Staff**, three-dot menu by the person > **Manage Appointment Types**.
2. **+ Assign Multiple Appointment Types**, tick the add-on, **Assign**.
3. Back on Appointments, click the number in the Staff column, set the **Pay rate** dropdown for the person, **Save**.

## Settings & fields
(* = required)
- **Name***, **Duration in minutes*** (only used when booked standalone), **Assigned to*** (must be set for online booking; clients get a "Customize your service" screen after choosing an assigned type).
- **Price for a single session*** (or tick **Add pricing later**; per-staff pricing allows per-person prices), **Revenue category*** (or **Add new**; match the service category name), **Sales tax or other** (tax rates in use).
- **Would you like to allow your clients to book this appointment online?**: Yes lets clients add it online; leave off for staff-only add-ons. **Online description** appears when enabled.
- **Color** (only with Color Coding System set to "Color Booked by Appointment Type"), **Capacity*** (default 1; match the base type's capacity so all slots stay bookable), **# Deducted*** (sessions taken from a pricing option), **Sort order*** (0 for all = by staff then alphabetical; 1 = top).
- **Back Bar Charge** (only if back bar charge feature on; reduces percentage pay only) and **CPT Code** (only if ICD/CPT codes feature on).

## Gotchas
- Assigned rooms are not available for add-ons.
- Unassigned add-ons will not be offered to clients booking online.
- Add-on capacity influences availability shown in Mindbody app/web, branded app and web tools.
- Some features depend on software package.
