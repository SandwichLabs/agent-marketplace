# How to manage appointment cancellation

Staff-side procedures for early cancelling or late cancelling an appointment (three entry points), cancelling recurring series, and restoring a cancelled appointment.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index (Appointment Schedule)
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_tlbx_advcanc_rest.asp?category=Clients (Cancellations report)
- Menu path: **Appointments**; **Insights > Reports > Clients > Cancellations**

## Steps
### From the appointment schedule
1. **Appointments**, click the appointment, choose **Early Cancel** or **Late Cancel** in the action menu.
2. If the client has other same-day appointments, pick **Cancel this appointment** or **Cancel multiple appointments** (tick which ones).
3. Click **Complete Early Cancellation** or **Complete Late Cancellation**.

### From Modify Appointment
1. Click appointment > **Modify**.
2. Top right, click **Early Cancel** (any time) or **Late Cancel** (after checkout), then **OK**.

### From the client's Schedule screen
1. Open the client's Schedule screen, click the three-dot **Actions** menu beside the appointment.
2. Choose **Early Cancel** or **Late Cancel**, click **OK**.

### Cancel all recurring appointments
- Use the "cancel all upcoming recurring reservations" article (Cancel Remaining).

### Undo a cancellation
1. **Insights > Reports > Clients > Cancellations**, set filters, **Generate**.
2. Click **restore** on the far right of the appointment.

## Gotchas
- Late Cancel appears only after payment is applied (no add-ons) or checkout is done (with add-ons); late cancelling withholds/deducts the session.
- Needs the **Override cancel policy** permission to early cancel after the cancellation window.
- Restore via the Cancellations report is described for cases where payment had been applied to the original booking.
- Fees: late cancel fees can be automated (standard fees as autopays) or charged manually as custom fees.
- Pending client forms tied to the service are removed on cancel; completed ones stay under Client Documents.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: Late Cancel (charges a session or fee) and cancelling paid appointments.
