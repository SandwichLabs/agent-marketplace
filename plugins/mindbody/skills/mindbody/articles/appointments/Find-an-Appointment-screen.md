# How to use the Find an Appointment screen to book or reschedule an appointment

Alternate search-based way to find open appointment slots when booking a client or rescheduling an existing appointment, instead of clicking on the calendar grid.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index
- Menu path: **Appointments** > magnifier icon in the Book Appointment panel

## Steps
### Book
1. Open **Appointments**, click the **magnifier** icon in the Book Appointment panel (screen defaults to the last client you worked with; there is no client search here).
2. Choose the appointment category (or click **Find more of these** beside a past appointment to prefill criteria).
3. Set criteria (appointment type, instructor, day, date, time), click **Search**.
4. Click **Book** beside the desired instructor slot.
5. Confirm on Book Appointment, click **Book Appointment**.

### Reschedule
1. From the client's Schedule screen click **Reschedule** beside the appointment.
2. Click **OK** on the alert (the original is NOT cancelled until the new booking is made).
3. Adjust the prefilled criteria if needed, click **Search**.
4. Click **Book** by the new date/time, confirm on Book Appointment, click **Book Appointment**.

## Gotchas
- Staff permissions (Appointments section): **View entire appointment schedule** (or **View own info/appointment schedule** to limit), **View appointment details**, **Book appointments for all staff** (or **Edit own info/appointment schedule** to limit to self).
- Because there is no client search, make sure the correct client is loaded before booking.
- Rescheduling only replaces the original once the new booking is completed.
