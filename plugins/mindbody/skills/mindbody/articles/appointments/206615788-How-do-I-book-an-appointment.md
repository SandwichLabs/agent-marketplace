# How to book appointments

Covers booking single, multiple, recurring and rebooked appointments from the business-side Appointment Schedule, plus two-staff bookings. Use whenever an agent must put a client on the appointment book.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index
- Menu path: **Appointments** (left nav)

## Steps
### Single appointment
1. Pick a date, find a staff member with open availability and click the time slot.
2. In the left sidebar search and select the client.
3. Click **Service** and choose the appointment type.
4. Confirm start/end; adjust in the sidebar or by dragging the block (or dragging its bottom edge to lengthen).
5. Optionally add notes; **+Add** next to **Add-ons** for extra services.
6. Review contact info, **Send Confirmation** checkbox, requested-staff star (yellow when set), rooms/resources, ICD/CPT codes, provider IDs.
7. Finish with **Book** (pay later), **Book & Buy** (opens POS with client and service loaded), or **Book & Apply** (only if a pricing option can cover it; applies it).

### Multiple appointments
1. Set up the first appointment, click **+Add another appointment** (it goes under Pending Appointments, a line marks its end time).
2. Pick another slot and fill it in; repeat.
3. Click **Book All**.

### Recurring appointments
1. Set up the first appointment (service and room chosen), click **Book a recurring appointment**.
2. Fill **Select recurring options**: **Select days**, **Book this appointment every**, **Start date** (prefilled), **End date**, **Auto-Set End Date**, **Room**, **Status** (**Confirmed?**/**Arrived?**), **Apply payment**, **Send client confirmation**.
3. Click **Check for Unavailable Dates**, review the skipped dates and total count.
4. Click **Book Recurring Appointment**.
- To cancel a series: click an appointment > **Modify** > **Cancel Recurring Appointment** (removes all occurrences).

### Rebook a past visit
1. Click an open slot, select the client.
2. Under Rebook a Past Visit, click a past service (grouped by date, shows staff and price; hover for details).
3. Review sidebar, click **Book**. Clicking more past visits or slots adds more appointments.

### Two staff on one appointment
- Add-on method (tracks attendance/payroll for both): create an appointment add-on, book staff 1 with the normal type and staff 2 with the add-on; check pricing at checkout.
- Block method (no payroll tracking for staff 2): book staff 1, mark staff 2 unavailable at the same time, note details in Reason.

## Settings & fields
- **Auto-Set End Date**: derives end date from the client's active pricing option expiry; requires options that activate on the sale date; uses expiry not session count, so limited-visit options can overbook.
- **Display payment buttons when booking in Business Mode** (Appointment Options > Appointment System Policies): turns Book & Buy / Book & Apply on or off; click **Update**.

## Gotchas
- Sidebar and Appointment Details options vary with site settings.
- Shortening length is blocked if prep time exceeds appointment time or finish time; adjust prep/finish slider first.
- Add-ons do not lengthen the appointment.
- Notes do not appear on the consumer site; include them in booking confirmation emails only via the <NOTES> replace phrase.
- Available times are solid-colored, unavailable gray; check the color key in the sidebar.
- Recurring online booking by clients cannot be disabled. Appointment count = days x interval to end date, minus unavailable dates.
- Rebook list needs staff permissions **View client visit history** and **View client account/purchase history** (otherwise no pricing). It lists only completed or paid visits; real price shows only for single-session options, otherwise a green paid marker.
- Use filters (top right) to narrow staff offering the appointment type.
