# How to allow clients to reschedule appointments (Mindbody consumer site)

The two settings that must both be on for clients to reschedule their own appointments online. Use when clients say they cannot see a reschedule option.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_appt.asp (Appointment Options)
- Menu path: **Settings > More > Appointment Options > Appointment System Policies**

## Steps
1. Open **Appointment Options**, expand **Appointment System Policies**.
2. Tick **Allow appointment rescheduling in Consumer Mode**.
3. Scroll to **Cancellation Windows** and tick **Enable Cancellations in Consumer Mode** for each service category clients may cancel AND reschedule.
4. Optionally set cancellation window minutes for those categories.
5. Click **Update** (top right).

## Gotchas
- Rescheduling is tied to online cancellation: the per-category cancellation checkbox must be on or no reschedule option appears.
- Cancellation windows (recommended) limit how late clients may reschedule/cancel without penalty.
- Requires a software package that includes the feature.
