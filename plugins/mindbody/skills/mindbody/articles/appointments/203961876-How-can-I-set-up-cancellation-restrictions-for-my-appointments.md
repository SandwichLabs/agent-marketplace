# How to set up cancellation restrictions for your appointments

Configures cancellation windows so clients can cancel/reschedule appointments online without penalty up to a cutoff; later cancellations become late cancels (charged).

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_appt.asp
- Menu path: **Settings > Appointment Options > Appointment System Policies > Cancellation Windows**

## Steps
1. Open **Appointment Options**.
2. Expand **Appointment System Policies**, scroll to **Cancellation Windows**.
3. Tick the checkbox for each appointment service category that clients may cancel online.
4. Enter the minutes before start allowed for cancelling (24 hours = 1440).
5. Click **Update**.

## Settings & fields
- Cancellation types: **Early cancel** (no penalty), **Late cancel** (deducts a session / charges for the missed visit), **No-show** (left on the schedule for tracking, or late-cancel after the time passes).

## Gotchas
- Inside the window clients can early cancel or reschedule online; outside it they cannot, and a cancellation is processed as a late cancel (client charged).
- Restrictions are keyed to the SERVICE category, not the category of the pricing option used.
- Appointment requests on the consumer site cannot be cancelled once the start falls inside the window.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: shortening windows or late-cancelling affects client charges.
