# Appointment scheduling and management overview

Hub article summarizing how to book, confirm, track, pay for, cancel and reschedule appointments, with pointers to the detailed how-tos. Start here to find which article covers a specific appointment task.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Appointments** (Appointment Schedule screen: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index)

## Steps
Setup prerequisites (do before booking):
1. Add appointment service categories and appointment types.
2. Add staff members and staff availability.
3. Assign appointments in Staff Appointment Setup.
4. Optional: appointment pay rates and add-ons.

Task overview:
- **Book**: single visits, multiple sessions, recurring services, or rebook past visits (see "How do I book an appointment").
- **Confirmation emails**: turn on the Appointment Booking Confirmations (Single/Recurring) notifications in Notification settings and make sure clients are opted in. While booking, enter the client's email and tick **Send Confirmation** in the left sidebar (this also updates their Client Info email). The Appointment Confirmation (Manual) notification can be sent from the Appointment Schedule or Modify Appointment screens.
- **Waitlists**: add clients when the desired time is full; fills slots on cancellations or added availability and shows potential earnings.
- **Statuses (confirmed/arrived)**: toggled on or off per the Appointment Arrival feature; Check In / Check Out can mark arrival then check out after service.
- **Check out**: marks the appointment complete and paid; the appointment can no longer be edited afterward.
- **Apply Payment**: deducts a visit from an existing pricing option without completing the appointment; handy for late cancels or missed visits with no extra charge.
- **Cancel / reschedule**: separate how-to articles.
- **Availability/unavailability**: schedule or delete directly on the appointment calendar.

## Gotchas
- Sidebar and Appointment Details options vary with site settings; availability depends on software package.
- ⚠️ Checkout is effectively final: a checked-out appointment cannot be edited.
- Pending forms set to send on booking are removed if the client cancels; forms already completed stay in the Client Documents tab.
- Staff can mark a liability waiver complete or email an unsigned waiver from the profile or the calendar booking.
