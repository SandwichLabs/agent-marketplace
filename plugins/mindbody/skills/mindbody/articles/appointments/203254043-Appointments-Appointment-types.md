# Appointments screen overview

The Services & Products > Appointments screen is where appointment types (services), their pricing, assigned staff and staff schedules are set up. Each distinct service (e.g. "60-minute Signature Massage") needs its own appointment type inside a service category.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/appointment-types
- Menu path: **Services & Products > Appointments**

## Steps
### Add an appointment type
1. Open **Services & Products > Appointments**, find the service category.
2. Click the **Add** dropdown > **Appointment** (or **Add-on**).
3. Fill in details, click **Save** (top right).
4. Click the service name later to edit. Next: add pricing and assign staff.

### Add pricing
1. In the Price column click the **dollar amount** button (slide-out opens).
2. Click **Create**, pick type (single, multiple, unlimited, or autopay/contract), OR choose an existing option from the dropdown and click **Assign** (**Assign and add another** for several).
3. Otherwise fill the form (below), then **Add** (or **Add with Additional Options** if advanced settings are needed).

### Remove pricing
1. Open the Price slide-out, click the three-dot menu on the option.
2. **Disassociate this pricing option** (no longer pays for this service; option stays active) or **Deactivate Pricing Option** (no longer sellable).

### Assign staff
1. Click the staff link (or "# staff assigned") in the Staff column.
2. **Assign Staff**, tick staff (or **+Add New Staff**), choose pay rate, booking time and (with Per Staff Pricing) prep/finish times.
3. **Go back to Services and Pricing**. **Remove** unassigns a staff member.

### Add staff schedules
1. Open the staff slide-out, click **Staff schedules** (bottom left).
2. **+Add schedule** (or **View/edit schedule** > **+Add New Schedule**).
3. Enter availability/unavailability on Add Availability, then **Add** or **Save and Add Next**.

## Settings & fields
**Pricing option form:** **Pricing option name**, **Price** (total for all sessions), **Sell online** (also shows in Mindbody app if listed), **Tax**, **Expires** (default 12 months for single/multiple, 1 month for unlimited; 1-999 days/months; activates on sale date or first visit), **Number of sessions**, **Is this an introductory offer? (limit of 1 per client)** (No / Yes new clients only / Yes new and existing), **Promote in MINDBODY app** (intro offers only), **Revenue category** (match the service category name).
**Additional options:** **Membership**, **Only allow clients to purchase this in a contract or package?**, **How often can this be used to pay for services?**, advanced-settings question.

**Service category three-dot menu:** **Allow online scheduling** (toggle per category), **Rename service category**, **Show inactive appointment types** (**Activate** to restore), **Manage pricing relationships** (appears with multiple categories), **Payroll integration** (service category code and assistant service category code), **Deactivate service category** (can be reactivated via View inactive service categories).

**Settings menu (top right):** links to Appointment Options, Scheduling increments, Staff members.

## Gotchas
- Booking time per staff member is required even if you do not track payroll; otherwise the software cannot tell what the staff member offers.
- Staff with no default pay show "No Pay".
- Every new appointment type gets an auto-linked single-session pricing option (may be $0.00 or created fresh in several cases). To make it unusable: disable Sell online, require contract/package, or restrict to a membership.
- Set up scheduling increments (business hours) first.
- Deactivated types/categories are kept for reporting; deactivating stops new bookings and sales (confirm with user first).
- Semi-private/group appointments and add-ons have their own articles.
