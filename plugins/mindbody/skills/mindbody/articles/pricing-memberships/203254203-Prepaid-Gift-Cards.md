# How to set up and sell prepaid gift cards

Covers turning on the prepaid gift card payment method, defining gift card products, and selling them at Point of Sale. A prepaid card's value is tied to a unique card ID.

## Where in Mindbody
- Payment Methods: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_paymeth.asp (Settings > Retail > Payment Methods)
- Gift Cards: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/productmanagement/giftcardsummary (Settings > Pricing > Gift Cards, or Point of Sale > More > Gift Cards)
- Point of Sale: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_retail.asp?fl=true&tabID=3

## Steps
### Enable the payment method
1. Settings > Retail > Payment Methods; tick **Active** beside "Prepaid Gift Card"; **Update**.

### Create a gift card
1. Settings > Pricing > Gift Cards > **+Add New**.
2. Fill the Add a Gift Card form (expand **Advanced options** for more) and click **Add**.

### Sell one
1. Point of Sale, look up the client, open the **Payments/Gift Cards** tab in the Add Item box.
2. Pick the gift card, enter a unique card ID (re-enter if confirmation is required). Optionally **Generate Random** (12 digits).
3. For custom-amount cards adjust price (and value if allowed), click **Add Item**, choose payment method, save sale.

## Settings & fields
- **Gift card is sold at**: multi-location sites only; all locations by default; cross-regional sharing possible if corporate requests it.
- **Card value**: preset, "Other", or "Customer chooses value at time of sale". **Discount the price of this gift card** sells below value (e.g. $100 for $75).
- **Gift card name**: auto-generated from value and price; editable.
- **Sell online**: on by default with Mindbody merchant processing; otherwise optional and clients must pay with account credit.
- **Allow staff to set price and value independently?**: Yes lets permitted staff differ them; No forces value = price (staff can still change price, value follows).
- **Can anyone purchase this gift card?**: No restricts to chosen memberships.
- General Setup & Options settings: **Make Gift Cards Reloadable**, **Require Staff to Confirm Gift Card Numbers**, **Generate Random Gift Card ID**, **Allow Gift Card Purchases Without A Login** (disable to remove guest checkout).
- Card ID: 1 to 50 characters; longer is safer; swiper/scanner works.

## Gotchas
- Permissions: Set up payment methods; Manage account credits, gift cards, contracts, packages; Complete sales transactions at POS.
- Once used, the original gift card sale cannot be voided or returned until all purchases made with it are voided. Voiding a purchase restores the balance. ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: returning a gift card removes the entire balance.
- Tax is charged on redemption, not at sale (EU/UK VAT: treated as multi-purpose; no single-purpose option).
- Balance checking at POS only appears when the payment method is active.
- Trash can on the Gift Cards screen removes the card option from POS.
- In contracts, recurring gift cards reuse the first-sale ID each cycle; a discounted card's price and value cannot be edited inside a contract.
