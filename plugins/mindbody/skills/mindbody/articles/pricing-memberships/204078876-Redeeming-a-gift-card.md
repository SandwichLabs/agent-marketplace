# How to redeem a gift card using the Point of Sale screen

Staff steps for applying a prepaid or assignable gift card balance during checkout, plus the online/in-person redemption rules.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_retail.asp?fl=true&tabID=3
- Menu path: Point of Sale

## Steps
### Prepaid gift card
1. Point of Sale, search the client, add services/products to the ticket.
2. Click the **Prepaid Gift Card** payment method.
3. Enter, scan or swipe the card ID, click **Look Up**.
4. The balance is applied and the post-sale remaining balance is shown.
5. If the card does not cover the total, add a second payment method and fix each amount (the total is split evenly by default, so reallocate).
6. Click the preferred **Save** option.

### Assignable gift card
1. If not yet assigned, enter the ID in **Assign Gift Card** and click **Assign**; the balance becomes the client's positive account balance.
2. Add items, choose **Account** as payment.
3. Optionally add a secondary payment method; otherwise any shortfall becomes a negative account balance owed.
4. Complete the sale.

## Gotchas
- Assignable cards are not on every site; an Account Payment gives the same function.
- Online-bought assignable cards ask for the recipient's name but are NOT auto-assigned; staff must assign them. At POS, "Existing"/"New" client sales auto-assign; "Undesignated" needs manual adding.
- Prepaid cards bought online can be redeemed online or in person; prepaid cards bought in person cannot be redeemed online (sequential IDs are guessable). Online-sold cards get random 12-digit IDs.
- Assignable cards are redeemed only at the business; the value then sits in the client's account usable online.
- Balance can be checked on the POS screen or via the Gift Cards report.
- A prepaid balance can pay part of a contract at POS.
- VAT (EU/UK) is collected at redemption.
- "Require Staff to Confirm Gift Card Numbers" controls whether two ID fields appear.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: completing a sale with a gift card spends the balance.
