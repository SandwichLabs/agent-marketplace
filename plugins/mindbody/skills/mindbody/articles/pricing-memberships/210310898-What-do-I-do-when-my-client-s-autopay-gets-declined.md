# Steps to take when your client's autopay is declined

Workflow for fixing and retrying a declined autopay: verify billing info, then re-run the charge from the Autopay Schedule or the Autopay Detail report.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_eft_det.asp (Autopay Detail)
- Menu path: Insights > Reports > Payment Processing > Autopay Detail
- Client path: client profile > Client Info > Billing Information; Account Details > Autopay Schedule

## Steps
1. Open the client's Client Info screen and expand **Billing Information**.
2. Verify the details and that the card is not expired; click **Save**.
3. Go to **Account Details** > **Autopay Schedule**, tick the checkbox on the far right of the declined transaction, and click **Run All Checked Transactions Now**. (Staff may need extra permissions for this screen.)
4. Alternatively, find declined items in **Autopay Detail**, tick the declined autopay, and click **Run All Checked Transactions Now**.
5. If it declines again, contact the client.

## Gotchas
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: re-running charges the client's card.
- Decline-code references exist per processor: TSYS/Elavon US, US ACH, Mindbody Payments (all regions), Paysafe, Ezidebit, Bluefin Canada (Elavon).
- Related options: change the autopay's payment method, convert declined autopays to a negative account balance, or run an autopay on time without charging the card.
