# Memberships settings screen overview

How to create memberships (tiers with perks such as discounts, priority and restricted booking), attach them to pricing options, and manage discounts and restrictions. A client becomes a "member" by owning a pricing option that has a membership attached.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_membership.asp
- Menu path: Settings > Clients > Membership Settings (or search "Membership Settings")
- Pricing screen: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/pricing
- Settings > Pricing > Pricing Options: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_series.asp
- Retail products: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_prod.asp

## Steps
Flow: create membership, attach to pricing option, sell that pricing option to the client, track via the Membership report.

### Attach a membership to a pricing option
- New option: Services & Products > Pricing > **Add Pricing Option** > **Additional options** > **Membership** dropdown > **Add**.
- Existing option: Pricing > click the option > **Additional options** > Membership > **Save**.
- Existing option plus past buyers: Settings > Pricing > Pricing Options, click the option, choose the membership from the **Number of sessions** menu, tick **Update Type for Past Sales**, **Save**.

### Member discounts
1. On Membership Settings, in Members Only Discount enter percentages in **Members Discount for Retail** and **Members Discount for Services**, then **Save** (or **Add** for a new one).
2. Assign the membership to each pricing option or product: for a pricing option, open it > Additional options > Yes for advanced settings > **Save with Additional Options** > Advanced Settings > **Apply members discount** (and **Restrict to members only**) > **Save**. Accelerate/Ultimate can use the Pricing Options settings screen instead. For a product: Retail Products > product > Membership Settings & Discounts > **Restrict who can purchase this product?** / **Do any member discounts apply to this product?** > **Save**.

### Deactivate or reactivate
1. Membership Settings > pick tier in **Select Membership Tier** (**Show Inactive** to see inactive ones).
2. Clear or tick **Active**, **Save**.

## Settings & fields
- **Membership Name**, **Active**, **Membership Icon** (shown by client name).
- **Sort Order / Priority**: 0 is highest; when a client holds several memberships only the highest-priority one's benefits apply; ties go to the first sold.
- **Allow Clients in this Membership to Schedule Unpaid**: appears only if Client Unpaid Scheduling Restrictions (Class and Course Options > Booking & Sign-in Policies) is "Allow Members Only" or "Allow Members or Clients with AutoPays Only".
- **Online Booking Restricted to Our Members Only**: none highlighted = open to all; some highlighted = those categories members-only online; all = only members can book online.
- **Allow Early Access Booking** plus **Days before scheduling window**: members book earlier than non-members (works with Rolling, Weekly, Monthly windows; rolling adds days to the window, e.g. 14 + 7 = 21). Applies to direct booking only; waitlist opens to everyone at the normal window.
- **Allow Priority Waitlisting**: members are auto-added from waitlist ahead of non-members. Not compatible with "first to claim" automation.
- **Restrict Self Sign In to Members Only**.
- **Allow Members to Book Appointments Without Paying**: consumer site and Mindbody app only, not branded app.
- **Allow Members to Purchase Non-Members Services / Products**: usually left checked.

## Gotchas
- Staff permissions (Settings): Membership Setup screen; Item/membership discounts & restrictions; Edit pricing option details; Edit product details.
- Member discounts do not apply to contracts or packages and do not stack with other discounts (discount priority decides).
- Setting a percentage is not enough; the membership must also be assigned on each pricing option/product.
- Deactivating a membership keeps perks for clients with active pricing options but removes them from the Membership report.
