# How to create pricing relationships between service categories

By default a pricing option only pays for services in its own service category. Pricing relationships let one category's pricing options pay for visits in another. Use this when a client says their pass will not pay for a different class type.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path (recommended): Services & Products > Classes / Appointments / Courses / Arrivals
- Classic path: Settings > Classic Setup > Service Categories
- Direct link (classic): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_tg.asp

## Steps
### From Services & Products
1. Open **Services & Products** and pick **Classes**, **Appointments**, **Courses**, or **Arrivals**.
2. On the service category row click the three-dot icon, then **Manage pricing relationships**.
3. Click a service category pair to show the possible relationships.
4. Choose one: stop sign (neither pays for the other), arrow right (left category pays for right), arrow left (right pays for left), or both ways.
5. Click **Save**.

### From Classic Setup (Pro, Accelerate, Ultimate)
1. Settings > search **Service Categories** (or Classic Setup > Service Categories).
2. Expand **Filters** and tick **Show related service categories**.
3. In the new column "Other service categories that pay for this", click the categories that may pay for the one on the left (Ctrl/Cmd-click for several).
4. Click **Save**.

## Gotchas
- Staff need the Service categories screen permission.
- All categories in the relationship must remain active.
- Allowed between classes, courses and arrivals. Appointment categories can pay for classes, courses or arrivals, but class/course/arrival pricing cannot pay for appointments.
- Avoid class-to-arrival relationships if using Client Check In or Self Check In: signing into a class auto-deducts an arrivals visit.
- Related pricing options that pay for courses must be assigned to each course.
- Between two appointment categories you must also assign extra appointment types to each pricing option.
- The booked service's schedule window wins over the pricing option's window.
- Booking with an existing prepaid option works on all platforms; book-and-buy without one works everywhere except branded web tools.
- Related appointment pricing options do not auto-show in the consumer-site checkout or in Book & Buy at POS; staff/client must choose the related service category from the dropdown. In the Business app, branded app and Mindbody app they appear automatically.
