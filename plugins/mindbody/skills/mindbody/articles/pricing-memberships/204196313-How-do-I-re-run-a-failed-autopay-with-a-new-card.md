# How to re-run a failed autopay with a new card

After a card-related decline, collect the money either through Receive Payments (if the failure became a negative balance) or by re-running the autopay on the Autopay Schedule. Which path applies depends on the site's failed-autopay setting.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: client profile > Account Details > Autopay Schedule; or Point of Sale > Receive Payments

## Steps
### Declined and converted to a negative account balance
1. Update the card in the client's Billing Information.
2. Point of Sale, search the client, click **Receive Payments**, then **Continue**.
3. Choose the credit card under **Payment Method** and complete the sale.

### Declined and NOT converted
1. Update the card in Billing Information.
2. Client profile > **Account Details** > **Autopay Schedule**.
3. On the failed autopay, set the **Payment method** dropdown to **Credit card**.
4. Tick the checkbox at the right of that autopay and click **Run All Checked Transactions Now**.

## Gotchas
- Staff need the "Run autopays" permission (Payment Processing) and possibly more for the Autopay Schedule screen.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: both paths charge the client's new card.
- If you cannot tell which case applies, check whether the client has a negative account balance from the failed autopay.
