# Suspending, cancelling, terminating, and deleting contracts overview

Explains the difference between suspend, terminate and delete for a client's contract and where the actions live. Use it to choose the right action before touching a client's contract.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: client profile > Account Details > Contracts section (action links per contract)

## Steps
1. Find the client, open **Account Details**.
2. In the Contracts section click the action link (View, Print, Autopays (x/x), Suspend, Terminate, Delete) and follow the prompts.
3. If a contract is missing, use the **Show All Dates** calendar at the top to load more.
4. Afterward, review the client's full autopay schedule.

## Settings & fields
- **View / Print**: show or print the contract document.
- **Autopays (x/x)**: jumps to that contract's autopay schedule.
- **Suspend**: pauses for days/weeks/months; missed autopays are pushed to the end (a 4-month suspension of a 1-year contract adds 4 months at the end).
- **Terminate**: removes all future autopays and immediately deactivates the contract's services. A termination can be cancelled, which reactivates services and remaining autopays, but missed autopays are not pushed out. Not applicable to month-to-month contracts: after termination a new contract is needed to restart.
- **Delete**: removes the contract and its autopay schedule from the account entirely. Items from the initial purchase (pricing options, products) must be handled separately through normal returns.
- Suspension fee: can be set when suspending; added as future autopays on the original dates in place of regular autopays, optionally under its own revenue category.

## Gotchas
- Required staff permissions: Ability to view all clients, View client account/purchase history, Auto-renew and suspend contracts, Delete client contracts.
- Each contract is managed individually; manually created autopays are not covered.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: terminating and especially deleting a contract (delete cannot be undone and leaves purchased items needing returns).
- Clients may also cancel auto-renewals online under click-to-cancel rules.
