# How to create an intro offer

An intro offer is a pricing option each client can buy only once, normally to draw in first-timers (optionally also existing clients). Use this to create one or understand who can buy it.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link (Client Acquisition dashboard): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/NetworkDashboard/Index
- Menu path: **Services & Products** (add a pricing option), the **Pricing Options** settings screen, or **Insights > Client Acquisition**

## Steps
### From Services & Products
1. Start adding a pricing option (see the "Pricing options, memberships" article) and fill in its details on **Add a Pricing Option**.
2. Under "Is this an introductory offer?" choose **Yes, for new clients only** (clients with no purchases yet, or marked prospects) or **Yes, for new and existing clients** (each can buy once).
3. Optionally tick **Promote in Mindbody app** to list it as a Deal.
4. Click **Add**.

### From the Pricing Options screen
1. Create a pricing option (or open an existing one) and fill in details.
2. Expand **Advanced Settings**.
3. Tick **Introductory offer** and choose **New clients only** or **New and existing clients**.
4. Click **Add** (or **Save** when editing).

### From the Client Acquisition dashboard
1. Go to **Insights > Client Acquisition** and scroll to Intro Offers.
2. Click **Create Intro Offer** or **+ Add Intro Offer** (depends on version).
3. Enter details; optionally tick **Promote in Mindbody app**.
4. Under "Who can buy this?" choose the new-only or new-and-existing option.
5. Click **Add**.

## Gotchas
- Needs "Add and deactivate pricing options" (Settings permission). Client Acquisition dashboard and Analytics dashboard, and Manage Mindbody Promote settings (Marketing) are also needed for the dashboard and app Deals listing. Availability depends on software package.
- Once a client buys it, it disappears from their consumer site, the online store and the Point of Sale for them.
- Mindbody suggests keeping the offer available roughly a month.
- Clients can pick multiple intro offers in their first online purchase.
- With **Auto Complete Closed Date** on (General Setup & Options), a purchasing prospect loses prospect status and can no longer buy "New clients only" offers; a $0 purchase does not count, and re-marking them as a prospect on Client Info restores eligibility.
- A client who booked an appointment and cancelled before attending counts as a client and loses app eligibility.
- A client who already bought one can still buy one for another eligible client.
- A $0 promoted intro offer bought via the Mindbody app triggers a 20% promoted-intro-offer fee on the client's next purchase.
- Messenger[ai] booking supports intro offers for classes only; appointments show standard pricing.
- Guidance: for appointment businesses, sell discounted multi-session packages rather than a discounted single massage or facial; for class businesses, a low-price one-month offer works well.
