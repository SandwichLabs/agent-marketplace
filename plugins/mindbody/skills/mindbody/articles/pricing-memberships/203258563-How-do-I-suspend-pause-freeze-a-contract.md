# How to suspend (pause or freeze) a contract

Temporarily pauses a client's contract autopays (vacation, illness, injury). Used when a client asks to freeze a membership without ending it.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: client profile > Account Details > Contracts > three-dot **Actions** menu > **Suspend**

## Steps
1. Search for and select the client; open **Account Details**.
2. In Contracts, click the three-dot **Actions** icon on the contract and choose **Suspend** (use **Show All Dates** if the contract is hidden).
3. Optional: pick a **Suspension Types** preset (predefined fields, not editable; changing start date moves the end date).
4. Set **Start Suspension On**.
5. Without a preset, set **Duration** (number) and **Unit** (**Days**, **Weeks**, **Months**), or tick **Open-ended suspension** if the length is unknown. An "Ends on" date is shown.
6. Optional: **Suspension notes**; and **Suspension fee** if no type was chosen and you charge one.
7. Click **Add Suspension**. Details appear at the bottom; use **Unsuspend** or **Delete** there to lift or remove it, and check the Autopay Schedule screen for the new dates.

## Gotchas
- No way to suspend contracts for all clients at once.
- Month-to-month contracts and contracts that run autopays when pricing options run out cannot be suspended; terminate and resell later instead.
- Suspended autopay dates are fixed; edit the suspension rather than the dates.
- Suspension starting after the last autopay on an auto-renewing contract: the renewal runs unsuspended (suspension applies only to the original contract).
- Scheduling a suspension on the day of an autopay is often too late; it probably ran that morning, and the pricing option it sold gets extended after the suspension.
- If "Scheduling suspensions" is on in General Setup & Options (Client Management), suspending a contract also suspends the client's booking privileges.
- Multiple suspensions allowed per contract but they must not overlap (error otherwise).
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: suspension fees change what the client is charged.
