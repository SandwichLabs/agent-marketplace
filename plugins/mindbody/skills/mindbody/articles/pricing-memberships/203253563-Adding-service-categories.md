# How to add service categories

Service categories group similar services (and their pricing). Use this to create, edit, deactivate or reactivate categories for classes, appointments, courses, arrivals, media or rooms.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link (classic): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_tg.asp
- Menu path: Services & Products (option 1) or Settings > Classic Setup > Service Categories (option 2)

## Steps
### Option 1: Services & Products
1. Click **Services & Products** and choose the service type (**Classes**, **Appointments**, **Courses**, **Arrivals**).
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **+Add new service category** (Arrivals: **+Add a new service category**, no type prompt). Confirm "Which type of service would you like to set up?"
3. A section titled like "Category 1" appears; click **Rename**, type the name, click outside the field to save.

### Option 2: Classic Setup
1. Settings > search **Service Categories**.
2. Click **Add new service category** (top right). Default categories may already exist; reuse, overwrite or deactivate them (uncheck **Active**, click Update).
3. Pick the service type: Appointments, Arrivals, Classes, Courses, Media, or Rooms.
4. Enter a name, set **Allow online Scheduling**, **Default Capacity** (most common 1), and the tabs the category appears on (not needed for Rooms/Arrivals).
5. Click **Add New**.

### Edit or find inactive categories
1. On the Classic Setup screen change fields inline and click **Save**.
2. For inactive ones, click Show Filters and tick **Show Inactive Service categories**; they show in red.

## Settings & fields
- **Active**: uncheck to retire a category.
- **Service Type**: changeable, but Class and Arrival categories cannot become Appointment; only Course categories can.
- **Allow Online Scheduling**: unchecked hides the services from the consumer site (staff only).
- **Default Capacity**: appointments only; prefills capacity of each appointment type in the category (editable on the Appointment Type setup screen).
- **Staff Pay Code / Assistant pay code**: for third-party payroll; only visible once Payroll Integration is set up.
- **Tabs this Service Category is on**: which consumer-site views show it (includes active and inactive custom menu items).
- **Color (RGB/HEX)**: used on the resource schedule and on the Appointment Schedule when Color Coding is by service category.

## Gotchas
- Max 21 appointment service categories; other types unlimited.
- Franchise studios may be blocked from editing by corporate permissions.
- A scheduled event that does not appear on the consumer site: check the "Tabs this service category is on" setting.
- Products do not need service categories; prices live on pricing options, not categories.
- Members get different pricing via memberships; letting one membership pay across categories needs pricing relationships.
- Accelerate/Ultimate can add up to two assistants per class.
