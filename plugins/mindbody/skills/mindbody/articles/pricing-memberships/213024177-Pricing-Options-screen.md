# Pricing Options on the Settings screen

How to create, edit, discontinue and configure pricing options (passes, memberships, drop-ins) from the Settings-area Pricing Options screen. Needed whenever an agent sets up or changes what clients can buy.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_series.asp
- Menu path: **Settings > Pricing > Pricing Options** (or search "Pricing Options")
- Alternate editor: the **Services & Products > Pricing** screen.

## Steps
### Add
1. Open the screen, click **+Add New** (top right).
2. Fill the minimum: name, number of sessions, service category, revenue category, Expires after (number + Days/Months), price, location (datashare sites), activation date.
3. Set any optional settings, scroll down and click **Add**.

### Edit prices
1. Change **Price** (staff/POS) and/or **Online Price** (clients), then click **Save** at the bottom.
2. If Online Price column is missing: **Settings > General > General Setup & Options > Online Store Settings**, tick **Online Retail Store**, **Update**.

### Discontinue
1. Edit the option and tick **Discontinued** (see the deactivate article). Deleting is not possible.
2. Find discontinued ones later via the **Discontinued** checkbox in Search for an Item.

### Enable low-visits / time-running-out emails
1. Click the pricing option name, expand **Notification**, open the email, tick **Enable** on the Notifications screen, **Save**.
2. Back on Pricing Options, tick the boxes and enter session counts (e.g. 5, 2, 1) or days (e.g. 14, 7, 1). Clear the boxes to disable.

## Settings & fields
- **Number of sessions**: Limited, Unlimited, or Membership (benefits come from Membership Settings; members get a colored icon until the option expires). Changing it affects future sales and future autopays.
- **Service category** (appointment categories add an **Appointment type** field, then **Assign**), **Revenue category**, **Secondary category** (reporting only).
- **Sell online** / **Online price**: blank online price means 0, so clients could buy it free.
- **Sales Tax**, **Set number of sessions**, **Use per session pricing** (auto-on when sessions = 1; price multiplies by sessions at checkout).
- **Sell at** / **Use at** (datashare multi-location; blank = all). Sell at only affects business site/app, not online sales. Cross-regional sites have an "Only allow this pricing option to pay for services at this location" box.
- **Activation date**: on the sale date; on the client's first visit after purchase (rolls over until first visit; moves if first visit is cancelled); or a custom date (not allowed if the option is in a contract; backdating can sell an already-expired option). Options expire at 11:59pm on the expiry date. Tick **Update activation date for past sales** to apply to existing purchases.
- **Low Session Alert Thresholds**: needs the Low Session Alert client alert; 0 disables.
- **Access to Video on Demand**: only with the on-demand video add-on; paywall is set on the Video screen.
- Advanced: **Only allow clients to purchase this in a contract or package**, **Priority** (default medium; needs the "Manage account credits, gift cards, contracts, and packages" permission), **Barcode**, **Early bird discount**, **Max Make Ups** (1-10 or unlimited; only with Make-ups feature), staff-paid-on-use dropdown, **Service notes**, **Introductory offer** (New clients only / New and existing clients / Promote in app; one purchase per client unless returned or voided), **Third-party discount pricing** (e.g. ClassPass; auto-returns on cancellation), **Restrict to members only**, **Apply member discounts**, **Receipt notes**, **Commission Rates**.
- **Scheduling Restrictions**: Max Sessions, Disallow Consecutive Days, Daily Restriction, Day of Month Scheduling Opens, Time Access.

## Gotchas
- Staff need pricing permissions in the Settings section.
- Scheduling restrictions do not show when creating a new option; only on existing ones. Schedule window settings override them.
- No limit on how many clients can use an option.
- Edits generally affect future sales only, but these can apply retroactively to purchased options: name, type (incl. membership changes), price in existing contracts/packages, activation date, priority, members-only restrictions and discounts, scheduling restrictions. Some show an "Update ... for past sales" checkbox.
- Changing allowed locations applies to past and future sales.
- Do not restrict to members who already hold the option itself, or new clients cannot buy it.
- Membership customization lives in the Membership Setup screen, not here.
- Emails' wording is shared across all pricing options; only timing is per option.
