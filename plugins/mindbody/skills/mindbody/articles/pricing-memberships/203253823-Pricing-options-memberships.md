# How to add pricing options overview

Directory of the three places pricing options (passes clients buy for classes, courses/enrollments, appointments, arrivals) can be created. Use it to decide which screen to work in.

## Where in Mindbody
- Pricing screen: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/pricing (**Services & Products** > **Pricing**)
- Classic Pricing Options settings: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_series.asp (**Settings** > **Pricing** > **Pricing Options**)
- Per-service creation: **Services & Products** > Classes / Courses / Appointments / Arrivals

## Steps
### Pricing screen
1. Go to **Services & Products** > **Pricing**.
2. Click **Add pricing option**, or click an existing option to edit.

### Pricing Options settings screen (advanced)
1. **Settings**, search "Pricing Options" (path **Pricing** > **Pricing Options**).
2. Click **Add New**, or select an existing option.

### From a service
- Add pricing from within the class, course, appointment, or arrival setup (Appointments article anchor: add_pricing).

## Gotchas
- Services & Products screen needs **View Services & Pricing** permission; creating/editing options needs the pricing-option management permissions (Settings section).
- The classic Pricing Options screen exists only on Accelerate and Ultimate packages.
- That screen exposes every setting (e.g. scheduling restrictions) that Services & Products omits; recommended for advanced users only.
- Memberships can be attached to pricing options from Services & Products but must be customized on the Membership Setup screen.
