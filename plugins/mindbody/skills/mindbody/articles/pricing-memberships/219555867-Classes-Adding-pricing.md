# How to add class pricing on the Add a Pricing Option screen

Shows how to attach a pricing option to a class service category straight from the Classes screen so clients can buy and book online.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/servicesandpricing/Classes
- Menu path: Services & Products > Classes

## Steps
1. Open Classes and expand the class service category.
2. Click **+Add Pricing** and choose a type: **Single session**, **Multiple sessions**, **Unlimited sessions** (any number of classes in the period), or **Autopay/Contract**.
3. On the Add a Pricing Option screen, enter name, price and any other settings.
4. Click **Add** to save. If advanced settings are needed, answer Yes to the advanced-settings question, click **Add with Additional Options**, finish on the Pricing Options screen, and click **Save**.

## Settings & fields
- **Pricing option name**: should tell clients what it pays for (e.g. "10-Class Yoga Pass").
- **Price**: total for all sessions; numbers only.
- **Sell online**: also lists it in the Mindbody app if the business is listed there.
- **Tax**: tick the tax rate(s); all active rates across locations show.
- **Expires**: defaults are 12 months after sale for single and multiple sessions, 1 month after sale for unlimited. Enter 0 to 999 plus Days or Months, then choose activation on sale date or client's first visit.
- **Number of sessions**: single, multiple or unlimited; changeable.
- **Is this an introductory offer?**: No; Yes for new clients only (never bought any pricing option); Yes for new and existing (each client may buy it once). **Promote in Mindbody app** puts it in "Deals Near Me".
- **Revenue category**: pick or **Add New**; recommended to mirror the service category name for cleaner reporting.
- Additional options: **Membership** (buying it makes the client a member; pick or add one), **Only allow clients to purchase this in a contract or package?**, **How often can this be used to pay for services?** (limits per week, calendar month or rolling month).

## Gotchas
- Pricing in a service category pays for every class in that category; use separate service categories for different pricing.
- Time-access or scheduling restrictions can stop a pricing option from paying for some classes.
- Some advanced settings (member discounts, restrictions) exist only on the Pricing Options settings screen.
- Contract setup has different options; see the contracts article.
- Some features depend on the software package.
