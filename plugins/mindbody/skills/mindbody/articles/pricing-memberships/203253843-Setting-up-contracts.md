# How to add and set up a contract

Contracts bill clients on a recurring schedule for services, products or access. Use this when creating or editing a contract (autopay) definition: its items, billing rules, and agreement terms.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/contracts
- Menu path: Service & Products > Contracts, then **+Add a Contract** (top right)

## Steps
1. Open Contracts and click **+Add a Contract**.
2. Fill **What would you like to call this contract?**
3. In Pick Contract Items, add items (see below). Item kinds: pricing options (**Classes**, **Appointments**, **Courses**, **Arrivals**), **Rooms**, **Products**, **Account Payments**, **Gift Cards**, **Registration Fee**.
4. For each item pick from the popup list (search bar / **Filter by**), set **One-time item** (only in the first purchase, does not renew), **Price** (applies only inside this contract), **Quantity**, then **Assign**.
5. To create a new pricing option inline: **+ Add a new item**, supply name, service category, revenue category, expires, number of sessions, membership status, video-on-demand access, price, frequency, tax, then **+Add**.
6. Registration fee: enter amount, choose or add a revenue category, **Assign**.
7. Set contract options (below), optionally add agreement terms, review, then click **Add** (top right).
8. To change an item afterward, click it in Contract Items (change price, quantity, one-time vs recurring); hover and click the trash can to remove.

## Settings & fields
- **Is this an introductory offer?**: No / Yes for new clients only / Yes for new and existing.
- **Run autopay**: On a set schedule (recommended) or When the pricing options run out (charges only after sessions hit 0 with none booked in future; uses sale date as placeholder dates; each pricing option gets its own schedule).
- **How often will clients be charged?**: Set number of autopays (default 12; choose interval and weeks/months/years) or Month-to-Month (continues until terminated).
- **Number of autopays**: max 99. Contracts over 90 weeks display in branded web/app as indefinite auto-renewing subscriptions with FTC-style wording and no fixed duration.
- **Pre-sales contract**: create before the start date is known and set dates later.
- **When will clients be charged?**: sale date, 1st, 15th, last day, 1st or 15th, 1st or 16th, 15th or last day, or Specific date (prefills at Point of Sale). For the 1st-of-month option online, clients choose the next month up to 3 months out; at POS staff get a window of 13 months back to 6 months ahead.
- **What happens after N payments?**: contract expires or auto-renews every N payments (not shown for month-to-month).
- **Sell online**: lets clients buy and autopay is created automatically; reveals **Online store link** and **Online description**.
- Additional options: **Discount %** (all items except deposits), **Contract deposit** (collected at sale; returnable as credit or refund; can allow staff to edit), **Make first autopay free?** / **Make last autopay free?** (optional apply-on-renew; on existing contracts "Apply this setup change to ongoing autopays?" cannot be undone), **Where can a client purchase this contract?** (locations for multi-location sites; online ignores it), **Can clients terminate this contract online?** (applies retroactively to existing contracts), **Restrict who can purchase this contract?** (limit to certain memberships), **Barcode ID**.

## Pricing option setups (activation/expiry)
- No rollover (unlimited/monthly/use-it-or-lose-it): activate on sale date, duration equals the autopay interval.
- Limited rollover: activate on sale date, duration equals how long clients keep unused sessions.
- Ongoing rollover (never expires with the contract): activate on client's first visit, duration your choice.
- Perks without sessions: use a zero-session pricing option, duration equals billing interval.
- For weekly session allotments, bill every four weeks rather than monthly so counts line up.

## Gotchas
- Staff need the permission "Manage account credits, gift cards, contracts, packages".
- Products in contracts require the Pro, Accelerate, or Ultimate package.
- New pricing options created inline cannot have quantity set until the contract is saved; then edit it from Contract Items.
- Max 100 one-time plus 100 recurring instances of one item; only one deposit and one registration fee per contract; quantity can be 0 for one type but not both.
- Prices on account payments/gift cards are editable only if they were created with "Force price and credit fields to match in Business Mode"; others sit in the non-customizable list.
- Recurring gift card items reuse the same gift card ID each cycle and add balance.
- Editing agreement terms does not affect already-signed clients; terminate and resell for them to accept new terms.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: applying free first/last autopay changes to ongoing autopays.
