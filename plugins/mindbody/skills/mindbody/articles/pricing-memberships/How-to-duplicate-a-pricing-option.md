# How to duplicate a pricing option on the Pricing screen

Clone an existing pricing option (optionally into another service category) instead of building a similar one from scratch.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/pricing
- Menu path: Services & Products > Pricing

## Steps
1. Open the Pricing screen and find the pricing option.
2. Click its three-dot menu and choose **Duplicate**.
3. The name is prefilled with the original plus "Copy" and the same service category. Rename it and change the category if needed.
4. Click **Confirm**. The copy appears in the list.
5. Optionally click its name to open Edit Pricing Option and adjust more.

## Gotchas
- Staff need the "Edit pricing option price" permission.
- A new service category must be the same service type (class to class category, not to a course or appointment category).
- Appointment option kept in the same category retains its assigned appointment types; moved to a new appointment category, it has none assigned, so assign them on its edit screen.
- A duplicated course pricing option must be assigned to the course(s) it should pay for.
