# How to manage contracts and autopays

How to edit, run or delete the scheduled autopays of a contract already sold to a client, for fixed-duration and month-to-month contracts.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: client profile > Account Details > Contracts > Actions menu > **Autopays**

## Steps
### Open a contract's autopay schedule
1. Search for and select the client, click **Account Details**, scroll to Contracts.
2. Click the **Actions** icon beside the contract and pick **Autopays**.

### Fixed-duration contract
1. For any scheduled autopay you can change the date (type or calendar icon), the amount, the processing location, and the payment method; optionally re-point it to another of the client's contracts via the **Contract** dropdown (discouraged except in special cases).
2. To charge now: tick the checkbox on the right and click **Run All Checked Transactions Now**.
3. To remove: tick it and click **Remove (Delete) Checked Transactions**.
4. Click **Update**.

### Month-to-month contract
1. End Date shows "Month-to-month" and there is no "Auto renewing" checkbox. The next autopay appears at the top; edit date, amount, location, payment method, run now, or delete as above, then **Update**.
2. **Show month-to-month autopays** displays the next 11 in the queue.

## Gotchas
- Editing one autopay does not change the others, except that changing the price, date or payment method of the final autopay carries into future autopays when the contract auto-renews.
- Month-to-month: deleting the next payment just moves the following month's into the slot; to stop all payments, terminate the contract. Queued month-to-month autopays do not appear in autopay reports. Each becomes editable only when next in line.
- A declined autopay that is not set to convert to a negative balance pauses the contract until it is run successfully.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: **Run All Checked Transactions Now** charges the client's payment method; deleting scheduled transactions removes charges.
