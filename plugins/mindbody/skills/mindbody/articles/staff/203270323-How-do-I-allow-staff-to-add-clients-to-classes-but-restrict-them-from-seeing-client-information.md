# How to allow staff to add clients to classes and restrict them from accessing client information

Permission recipe for staff who can book clients into classes but cannot open client profiles. Requires owner or admin-level manager login.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_staff.asp
- Menu path: **Settings > Staff > Staff Permissions**

## Steps
1. Open **Staff Permissions**; use the **Select** dropdown to choose the permission group.
2. Expand **Classes & Courses**; tick **View reservations** and **Make reservations** (optionally **Make unpaid reservations**, only if the business allows unpaid reservations).
3. Expand **Sales Team Management**; tick **Ability to view all clients**.
4. Click **Update**.
5. Expand **Clients**; untick **View client info** and **Edit client info**.
6. Save (**Update**).

Result: staff can still add clients to classes, courses and appointments but get an error when trying to open client information.

## Gotchas
- Ability to view all clients stays ON (needed to find clients to book); the restriction comes from turning off View/Edit client info.
- Features depend on software package.
