# Staff Profile screen

Reference for a staff member's profile: login credentials, role/permissions, location access, contact details, role flags (instructor, desk, commissions, tips), and deactivation. Use when creating logins or changing how a staff member behaves in scheduling, payroll, or sales.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Staff** (left nav), then search or click a staff name.
- Staff's own account page: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/SecurityInfo/UserInfo (initials top right > **Account Information**)

## Steps
### Edit a name
1. Click the name on the profile, edit, **Save**. This does NOT change the staff identity record; the staff member should also change it themselves in **Account Information**.

### Set up / change a login
1. Use **Set up login** / **Edit login**. Username must be an email; password needs 8+ chars, at least one capital and one special character, alphanumeric.
2. **Re-send email** resends the setup email. **Remove Login** disables sign-in to this studio.
3. For a new login a **Role** must be chosen via **Set up permissions** / **Edit permissions**.

### Deactivate
1. Use **Deactivate Staff Member** (deletes their login). Find them later via Staff screen **Status** filter = **Inactive**.

## Settings & fields
Credentials
- **Photo** (**Choose an image**): auto-resized to 200 x 151 px; shown in business site, Mindbody app, branded app; on consumer site only when a public bio exists. Shown on or hidden from both sites together.
- **Role**: permission group applied to the person. **Location(s)**: for multi-location datashare sites. If the "Allow users to switch between locations while logged in" permission is on, the person has all locations, picks one at login, may tick **Set as default location** (reset by logging out and clearing browser history, or by toggling their permission group away and back). If off, assigned locations are chosen at login and switching requires re-login.
Additional info
- **Gender** (required; used by appointment finder), **Staff ID #** (Payroll report, Self Clock In), **Provider ID** (invoices/statements), **Nickname** (needs "Use Staff Nicknames" in General Setup & Options; overrides other name settings), **Employment dates**.
Contact
- **Email address** (auto emails go here), **Mobile phone** (texts also need Automatic notification preference set to mobile text only plus a mobile provider), other phones/address (appear in Phone Book report), **Automatic notification preference**, **Private notes** (not client-visible), **Public biography** (shows in apps and when clients click the staff name), **Client Profile** link (needed for Professional products charges), **Opt in to feature updates from Mindbody**.
Settings (need "Manage staff member Settings & Sales settings" permission)
- **Desk Staff** (informational only), **Instructor (for appointments)**, **Instructor (for classes)** (also covers courses), **Assistant** / **Assistant 2**, **Independent contractors** (tips show under that Pay Type on Tips report), **Allow overlapping scheduling** (suppresses double-booking warnings for classes/events; appointments still blocked during classes), **Hide in payroll export**, **Schedule sort order** (lower first, ties by last name).
Sales settings
- **Rep 1-6** (sales rep / lead management; Rep 2-6 enabled in General Setup & Options; term renameable via Words and Phrases), **Can be assigned followups**, **Earns commissions** (needs commissions enabled), **Earns tips** (needs tips enabled).
Other
- Google Calendar Integration; **Log out of Mindbody Apps** (top right name menu; needs View/edit staff personal information and Administer Staff Logins).

## Gotchas
- Accessing contact info needs **View/edit staff member personal information** permission.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: deactivating deletes the login.
- Deactivation effects: appointment schedule hidden (reschedule manually; Schedule at a Glance report lists them); scheduled classes stay until instructor edited or cancelled; room-rental supervisor name stays but editing defaults to first active staff alphabetically; they vanish from many reports (e.g. Payroll). Pay them and reschedule before deactivating.
- Ex-staff may stay logged into the Business app and Class Check-in app; force out with **Log out of Mindbody Apps**.
- Without a login a staff member sees only what clients see.
- With multiple locations, the login email works across all studios; to change the email for one location, **Remove Login** then recreate.
