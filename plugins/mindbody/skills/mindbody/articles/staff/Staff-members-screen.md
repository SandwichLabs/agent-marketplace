# Staff members screen

Overview of the Staff screen: the list of staff, search and filters, per-staff tools, and the More menu. Use it to find a staff member, open their profile, or reach pay-rate, schedule, and permission tools.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Staff** in the left navigation panel.

## Steps
### Find a staff member
1. Open **Staff**; the list shows 10 per page, with page numbers at the bottom.
2. Type in the staff name search field (partial matches work, e.g. "John" also finds "Becky Johnson") or click a name to open the profile.
3. Narrow with the **Role** dropdown and the **Status** dropdown (all, active, inactive; inactive shows deactivated profiles).

## Settings & fields
- **Add New Staff** button: starts creating a staff member.
- **More** dropdown (top): **Time Clock Tasks**, **Staff Permissions**.
- **Name** column: opens the profile; click the header to sort ascending/descending.
- **Role** column: set at creation, editable later.
- **Phone**, **Email** columns: the email is the login; creating a login emails the staff member to set their password.
- **Three-dot menu** at the right of each row: **Manage Appointment Types**, **Manage Schedules**, **Manage Class Pay Rates**, **Edit Staff Profile**.

## Gotchas
- Adding or editing profiles requires the Staff permission **View/edit staff member personal information**.
- This updated staff management experience is still rolling out and may not be on every site.
- Some features depend on software package.
