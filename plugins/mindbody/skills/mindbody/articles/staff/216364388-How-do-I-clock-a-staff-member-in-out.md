# How to clock staff members in or out

Clock someone else in or out via Time Clock, for example when a staff member forgot to punch and you are fixing their time card.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff/time-clock/clock-others
- Menu path: **Clock** icon (top right) > **Clock Others In/Out**

## Steps
1. Click the **Clock** icon at top right.
2. Click **Clock Others In/Out**.
3. Click **Clock in** or **Clock out** beside the staff member's name.

The entry is stamped with the current date/time; adjust it afterward if needed (see [How to manage time clock entries](How-to-manage-time-clock-entries.md)). The Time Clock report shows hours worked, pay rates and amount owed.

## Gotchas
- The acting staff member needs the Time Clock staff permissions enabled to clock others.
- Some features depend on software package.
