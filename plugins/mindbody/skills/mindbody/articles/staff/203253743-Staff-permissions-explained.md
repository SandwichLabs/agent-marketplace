# Staff Permissions explained

Overview of the Staff Permissions screen, which decides what each permission group (and so each staff login) can do. Needed when restricting or granting access, setting IP restrictions, or choosing a login landing page.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_staff.asp
- Menu path: **Settings > Staff > Staff Permissions** (or search "Staff Permissions" in the top search bar)

## Steps
### Set permissions for a group
1. Open Staff Permissions and pick a group (e.g. Manager) from the top-left dropdown.
2. Use **Expand All** or click a category to see its permissions, and enable or disable them.
3. Staff must log out and back in for new permissions to apply.

### Set a default landing tab per group
1. On Staff Permissions, click **Add/Edit Groups** (top right).
2. Choose a **Default launch tab** beside each group.
3. Click **Update**.

### IP restrictions
1. In **Add/Edit Groups**, tick **Enable IP Restrictions** (or the "Restrict IP" box on one group).
2. Click **Setup IP List** and add allowed addresses.

## Settings & fields
- **Members list** (per group): staff name (links to profile), username, work locations, trash icon that removes only the login permission for the group.
- **Permission categories**: Marketing, Settings, Classes & Courses, Appointments, Clients, Reports, Analytics, Time Clock, Sales Team Management, Payment Processing, Ratings and Reviews. Each has its own detail article, plus per-software-package breakdowns.
- Default groups: **Manager** and **Staff**, editable depending on package.
- Four notable restrictive permissions: issue sale refunds to credit cards; Schedule at a Glance/Attendance (own schedule only); Cancellations (view own only); Payroll/Payroll Export/Tips/Assistant/Commission reports (view own only).

## Gotchas
- Editing permissions is only available on Accelerate, Ultimate and legacy Pro packages.
- Managing groups and IP restrictions needs the **Administer permission groups** permission (Settings section).
- Members only show under a group if the group was assigned on the staff profile; to actually remove a staff username/password, go to their profile.
- Some settings are owner-login only.
- IPv4 restrictions work; IPv6 cannot be restricted. Multi-location: one shared IP makes staff choose a location at login.
- Franchise businesses may be unable to edit permissions (contact corporate).
