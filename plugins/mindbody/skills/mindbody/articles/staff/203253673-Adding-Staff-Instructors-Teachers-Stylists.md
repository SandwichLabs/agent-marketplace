# How to add staff members

Staff (instructors, teachers, stylists, front desk, etc.) can be created from the Staff screen or inline while scheduling a class, course, or assigning an appointment type. Needed before anything can be scheduled.

## Where in Mindbody
- Staff screen: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff
- Classes: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/servicesandpricing/Classes
- Courses: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/servicesandpricing/enrollments
- Appointments: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/appointment-types
- Menu paths: **Staff**; **Services & Products** > **Classes** / **Courses** / **Appointments**

## Steps
### From the Staff screen
1. Go to **Staff** and click **+Add Staff** (top right).
2. Fill in details, then **Save** (or **Save and Add Another** for multiple).

### While scheduling a class
1. **Services & Products** > **Classes**; click **+Schedule** next to the class (for a brand-new class, use "Add a Class" in its service category first).
2. At the staff dropdown, choose **Add New** (bottom of list), enter details, click **Add**.
3. Finish the schedule details and click **Schedule Class**.

### While scheduling a course
1. **Services & Products** > **Courses**; click the **# Schedule** link beside the course (or **Schedule this Class** if no schedules exist), then **+Schedule** for the specific class/course. New course: use "Add a new course" at the bottom of its category first.
2. At the staff dropdown, choose **Add New**, enter details, click **Add**.
3. Complete the rest and click **Schedule**.

### While assigning an appointment type
1. **Services & Products** > **Appointments**; click **Staff** (or **Add Staff**) beside the appointment type. For a new appointment, use the **Add** dropdown on the category and pick **Appointment** first.
2. Click **Assign Staff**.
3. On "Assign an Existing Staff Member", click **+Add New Staff**, enter details to create and assign.

## Gotchas
- Requires the **Add new staff members** (Settings) staff permission.
- At least one staff member must exist before scheduling any class, appointment, or course. Even solo owners should add themselves.
- Non-teaching people (contractors, receptionists, bookkeepers) can also be added via the Staff screen and given permissions to manage bookings or hours/payroll.
- After creation, use the staff profile for photo, login, and other settings.
- Some features depend on the software package.
