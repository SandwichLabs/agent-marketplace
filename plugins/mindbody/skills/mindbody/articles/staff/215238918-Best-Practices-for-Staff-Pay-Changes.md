# Best practices for staff pay changes

Editing the dollar amount of an existing pay rate rewrites past payroll reports retroactively. To give a raise without distorting history, create or assign a new pay rate from a start date instead.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff ; https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true (Class Schedule); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_enroll.asp?fl=true (Course Schedule)
- Menu path: **Staff**, **Classes**, **Courses**

## Steps
### Classes
1. **Staff** > click the staff member > **Class/Hourly/Commission Pay Rates**.
2. Pick a pay rate slot not currently used, enter the new rate info, **Save**.
3. **Classes** > click the class name > **Make Schedule Change**.
4. Choose **From Date Forward**; enter the start date of the new rate.
5. Tick **Teacher Change**, select the same teacher, select the new pay rate, **Submit**.

### Courses
1. Do the class steps above using **Courses** (Course Schedule).
2. The change creates a new schedule with no pricing: open the course, use **Select pricing option** to re-add pricing options, **Save**.

### Appointments
1. **Staff** > staff member > **Staff Appointment Setup**.
2. Hover the appointment type, click the **X** left of its name, click **Unassign**.
3. Click **+Assign Another Appointment Type**, search and select the type, enter the provider's time length, choose **Flat Rate** or **Percentage Rate**, enter the amount, **Assign**.

## Gotchas
- Changing an amount in place is retroactive for all past payroll reports. ⚠️ Financial — confirm with the user first.
- Course pay changes drop pricing options until re-added.
- Unassigning an appointment type then reassigning is the appointment workaround; the article does not say what happens to existing booked appointments.
- Features depend on software package.
