# Non-service staff: Desk staff, managers, and more

How to put front-desk or manager shifts on the Appointment Schedule (shaded like availability) for people who neither teach nor take appointments, and pay them hourly through the Time Clock.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/services-products/appointment-types ; https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff
- Menu path: **Services & Products > Appointments**; **Staff**

## Steps
### 1. Create a dedicated service category
1. **Services & Products > Appointments** > **+Add a new service category** (bottom).
2. Name it (e.g. "Front Desk Staff Schedule"; the name appears on the Appointment Schedule) and click **Add**.
3. Click the **three-dot menu** and make sure **Allow clients to book online** is NOT checked.
4. Do not add appointment types.

### 2. Add availability for each staff member
1. **Staff** > click the person's name.
2. In Settings tick **Instructor**; **Save**.
3. Click **Appointment Availability** > **Add New Schedule**.
4. Fill in: **Show staff as** available; tick the new service category under services offered; choose **Location** (multi-location); set date range/days/times; set **Privacy** (Allow clients to see schedule / Mask the staff member's name / Hide from clients).
5. Click **Add**. Repeat per staff member.

### 3. Verify
- Check the Appointment Schedule. Block lunches/breaks by adding unavailable times to their availability.

### 4. Pay
- Pay them hourly via the Time Clock.

## Gotchas
- Because no appointment types exist, nobody can accidentally book them.
- "Hide from clients" still shows the shift on the business site but not the consumer site.
- Tip from the source: set up each weekday separately; it saves effort when schedules change.
- Features depend on software package.
