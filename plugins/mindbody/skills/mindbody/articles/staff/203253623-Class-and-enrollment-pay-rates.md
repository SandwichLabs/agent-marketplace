# Class and course pay rates

How to define what a staff member earns for teaching classes and courses (enrollments/workshops/events) on the Class / Hourly / Commission Pay Rates screen. Needed before scheduling a teacher if payroll is tracked in Mindbody.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff
- Menu path: **Staff** > click the staff member's name > **Class / Hourly / Commission Pay Rates** (top right)

## Steps
### Enter rates
1. Open the staff member's pay rates screen; confirm the right person is selected top left.
2. Fill in the rate type you want (below) and save.

### Default rate
1. Pick a rate in the **default pay rate** menu (top left) and click **Save**. It pre-selects when the person is scheduled; recommended before scheduling or using Quick Teacher Substitution.

### Copy rates
1. On the template staff member's screen, choose targets in **Copy these rates to** (up to 10) and click **Copy**.
2. Appointment pay rates must be copied separately via the Staff Appointment Setup screen.

### Rename rates / change increments
1. Click **Edit pay rate descriptions** (top right).
2. Edit names or increments, then click **Update Pay Rate Descriptions**. Descriptions are global, not per staff member.

## Settings & fields
- **Per class (Rates 1, 2, 9-16)**: flat amount per class; optional bonus per client over a threshold; **Min pay**; **Max pay**; **No Registrations Rate** (classes only, pays when nobody registered).
- **Per client (Rates 3-6)**: amount per client; optional bonus per client above a threshold (only for extra clients) or a bonus for every client once attendance reaches a threshold; min/max pay; No Registrations Rate.
- **Percentage (Rates 17, 18)**: percent of each visit's revenue; min/max per client; No Registrations Rate.
- **Incremental (Rates 7, 8, 19-21)**: flat pay by attendance band (1-5, 6-10, 11-15, 16-20, 21-25, 26+); bands editable to up to six custom ranges, and can be switched from amount to percentage.
- **Hourly**: not set here; use Time Clock.
- **Commission**: not for services; pays a percentage of products/pricing options a staff member sells, in addition to normal pay.

## Gotchas
- Needs owner login or the **Staff pay rates** (Staff section) and **View/edit staff member personal information** (Settings section) permissions.
- This screen does not cover appointment pay (separate article).
- Rate 1 is the default assistant rate. Assistants get no per-class or per-client bonus; an instructor assigned with Rate 1 does. Leave Rate 1 blank and start at Rate 2 if you may use assistants later.
- Unpaid class signups still pay per-client and incremental rates.
- Percentage rates are unpredictable with unlimited/time-based pricing options (revenue per visit drops as visits grow) and use the discounted price paid. The **Ignore Discounts when Calculating Payroll** setting (General Setup & Options) pays on the standard price.
- To raise a pay, assign a new rate (e.g. Rate 9) rather than editing the existing one; editing an existing rate rewrites past Payroll report figures retroactively.
