# How to manage staff login credentials

How to create staff members with logins, assign roles/locations, and trigger password resets. Staff only need an active email; they set their own password from an emailed link.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff
- Menu path: **Staff** (left nav)

## Steps
### Add a new staff member with login
1. **Staff > Add New Staff**.
2. Fill required fields, tick **I want to create a login for this staff member**, enter an active email.
3. Click **Save**; a setup email goes out automatically.

### Add or edit login on an existing profile
1. **Staff**, click the staff name (or three-dot menu > **Edit Staff Profile**).
2. Under Login credentials click **Edit Login** (first time: **Set up login**); edit the email; **Save**.
3. Click **Edit Permissions** (first time: **Set up permissions**).
4. Choose a **Role** from the dropdown.
5. With multiple locations, pick the location(s) where they may sign in. If their permission group may switch locations while logged in, you can also pick a default location; otherwise they must log out and in to change location.
6. Set the date to send the setup email (today = immediately; a future date sends at 12:01am that day).
7. Click **Send setup email**.

### Staff completes setup (done by the staff member)
1. Open the "Set up your Mindbody account" email, click **Setup Your Account**.
2. Confirm details, choose a password (8-30 chars, upper and lower case, at least one number or special character), change country if needed, click **Confirm**.
3. Click **Continue to sign in**.

### Reset a staff password
1. **Staff**, open the profile, **Edit Login**, click **Send reset password email** (change the Email field and **Save** first if the address needs updating).

## Gotchas
- Needs the "View/edit staff member personal information" permission (Settings section).
- Owners/managers cannot set or reset another person's password, only send the email.
- Setup emails expire 24 hours after sending.
- Email cannot match another staff or owner profile; it may match a client profile only if both staff identity and consumer identity are enabled.
- A permission group must be selected before login creation; missing one causes login errors.
- Changing the email sends a verification to the new address; staff must verify before logging in again.
- Reactivated staff must use Forgot Your Password or the reset email.
- If the email is not arriving: check spam, whitelist confirm@mindbodyonline.com.
- "Email in use" error: find it under **Staff Permissions** groups (Username column), delete it with the trash icon there, then re-add on the correct profile.
- Staff Identity is still rolling out; if missing from the site, contact Support.
