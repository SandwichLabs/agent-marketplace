# Staff Identity overview

Explains Staff Identity, the cloud-based login system behind staff credentials, and what staff see when it is switched on. Useful when a staff member is prompted to confirm an email or cannot log in.

## Settings & fields
- What it gives: staff self-service for their own profile and password resets, credentials verified and stored in the cloud, central management of staff accounts across a brand, Mindbody-verified emails, and no manual credential upkeep by owners.
- Staff log in with the Mindbody credentials set on their staff profile, on the website or in the Mindbody business app.

## Steps
### What staff are asked to do at next login (one time)
- Staff with a random username: change the username to their own email, set a new password (or reuse the current one if it meets strength rules), then verify the email.
- Staff with an email username: confirm it is valid and set or keep a sufficiently strong password.

## Gotchas
- No retraining needed; an upgraded account is created automatically at next sign-in.
- Each staff login needs a unique, active email address.
- Each staff account must be assigned a permission group when logins are set up.
- If you fix staff usernames by hand, do it from a desktop computer, not the Mindbody business app.
- Confirm-your-email prompts just mean the site has been moved to Staff Identity.
