# How to allow staff members to only access their clients' profiles

Limit a permission group to clients assigned to that staff member, using Sales Team Management "Rep" assignments.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp (General Setup & Options); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff ; https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_staff.asp (Staff Permissions)
- Menu path: **Settings > General > General Setup and Options**; **Staff**; **Settings > Staff > Staff Permissions**

## Steps
### 1. Enable Sales Team Management
1. **General Setup and Options** > expand **System Settings**.
2. Tick **Sales Team Management**; click **Update**.

### 2. Designate reps
1. **Staff** > click the staff member.
2. Under **Sales Settings** tick **Rep 1**.
3. Confirm the permission group shown on the left is correct; **Save**.

### 3. Set permissions
1. **Settings > Staff > Staff Permissions**; choose the group.
2. Expand **Sales Team Management**; make sure **Ability to View All Clients** is unticked.
3. Expand **Clients**; make sure **Add Client** and **View Client Info** are ticked (adjust others as needed).
4. Click **Update**.

### 4. Assign clients
1. Search for the client; open the **Client Info** screen.
2. Expand **Rep [#]**; choose the rep from the **Rep** dropdown; **Save**.
3. Repeat for every client to assign.

## Gotchas
- Each client must be assigned to a staff member or that staff member cannot open the profile.
- Feature depends on software package.
