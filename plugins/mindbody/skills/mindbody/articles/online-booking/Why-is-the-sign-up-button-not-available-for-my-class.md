# Reasons your clients may not be able to sign up for classes online

Diagnoses a missing Sign Up Now button or failed online class booking, using the built-in sign-up restrictions tool, and lists each restriction with its fix.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true (Class Schedule)
- Menu path: **Classes** in the left navigation.

## Steps
### Run the restrictions scan
1. Open **Classes**.
2. Click the **three-dot menu** at the right of the affected class.
3. Choose **View client sign-up restrictions** from the gear menu.
4. A red or yellow alert means issues were found: click the warning to see and fix each. Green means none (then the issue is specific to the client's account).
5. Click **Okay** to exit after fixing.

### Fix each restriction (click the message, change, **Save** unless noted)
- **Class Capacity** ("booking capacity of 0"): enter a number in **Capacity**.
- **Member Purchasing Is Restricted** ("members cannot purchase non-members services"): **Remove** beside the membership.
- **Consumer Mode Tab Needed**: use **Select a Tab** to put the class's service category on a client view tab shown on the consumer site.
- **Online Booking Is Restricted** (only certain members can book that category): **Remove** beside the membership to open it to everyone.
- **Purchasing or Scheduling Restrictions**: click it, then click the pricing option's name, then the specific message:
  - **Time Access Restriction** (option usable only on certain days/times): **Remove** the detail.
  - **Members Only Service**: **Remove** the membership restriction.
  - **Service Not for Sale Online**: click **Yes** to sell online.
  - **Location Restriction** (option cannot be used at the class's location): click **Yes** to allow it.
- **Client Types Prerequisites**: **Remove** the prerequisite.
- **Age Prerequisites**: change **Minimum age** or **Remove**.

## Gotchas
- After launching a class or changing the schedule, test with a test client profile on the consumer site.
- If no class-wide issue is found, check for duplicate client profiles, which can also block online sign-up.
- Removing restrictions changes who can buy and book for all clients; confirm with the user first.
