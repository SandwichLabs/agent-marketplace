# How to customize business policies in your Mindbody site

Where to edit the policy texts clients see online (cancellation, purchase, terms, refund, privacy, welcome message) and where each one displays.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp
- Menu path: **Settings > General > General Setup and Options > Business Policies** (expand section)

## Steps
1. Open **General Setup and Options**.
2. Expand **Business Policies**.
3. Edit each text box as needed.
4. Click **Update**.

## Settings & fields
- **Liability Waiver**: managed on the Client Forms and Liability Waiver screen, not here; shows when clients create accounts and can also be in a client form.
- **Cancellation Policy**: shown on the consumer site My Schedule screen (alongside your cancellation windows) and in the Mindbody app under the class description. With Messenger[ai], the policy must be set in Messenger[ai] settings instead.
- **Purchase Policy**: shown on consumer-site Check Out/Place Order.
- **Terms & Conditions** (recommended): Check Out/Place Order, footer of all automated notifications, Settings in the branded app.
- **Return / Refund Text**: Check Out/Place Order.
- **Privacy Policy** (recommended): same places as Terms & Conditions.
- **Welcome Paragraph Text**: first screen of the web scheduler (My Info tab under the business name before login); blank = default text.

## Gotchas
- HTML tags can style the text; setting labels can be renamed through Words and Phrases.
- Policies can also be sent for signature before a service using Client forms.
- Features depend on software package.
