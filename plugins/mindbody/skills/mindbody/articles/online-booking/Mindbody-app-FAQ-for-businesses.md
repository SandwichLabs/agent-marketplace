# Mindbody app FAQ for businesses

Behavior of the consumer Mindbody app (marketplace) as it affects a studio: visibility, prices, rooms, schedule sync, client accounts and staff checkout. Use it to explain or troubleshoot why something shows (or does not) in the app.

## General
- **Appearing in the app** requires: business listed, location mapped via the Google Maps integration, at least one online-bookable service, and services assigned to standard categories/subcategories or keywords.
- **Rooms**: with room requirements, clients can still book but cannot choose the room; the system auto-assigns. For tighter control, limit clients to appointment requests instead.
- **Deals** (intro offers, last-minute price-drop classes): unlimited to create; search results show one deal per business; clients see them in the Deals screen.
- **Prices shown**: online prices (may differ from in-store); intro offers incl. $0 can be promoted but not negative prices; pricing relationships make related-category options show; the price above Book is the cheapest single-session pass labeled "Drop-In", shown for the next 21 days even if a cheaper multi-session option exists; existing valid passes are offered at booking instead of charging again.
- **Schedule changes**: can take 24-48 hours to reach the Mindbody app and branded app. Virtual classes: viewable on Android, not displayed on iOS; buying passes in the mobile app is not supported.

## Clients
- **Phone number**: cannot be required; site Required Fields are ignored. New clients get a prompt before reserving; skipping triggers a second warning that they cannot book without a number (by design). Not shown to existing clients or those with a phone on their app profile.
- **Rewards**: points earned for booking classes, reserving appointments, purchases; referral points only when staff assign the "Referred By"/"Referred" relationship; clients cannot redeem points in the app (only on the consumer site or in person).
- **Calendar sync**: shows class name, studio name, address, date, time, instructor, description (no location name, so add it to the class name for multi-location). Waitlisted classes do not sync until the client moves into the class.
- **Shares pricing option relationship** is respected; the pass appears only on the purchaser's account.
- **Purchase done but booking failed** ("Your purchase was completed, but we couldn't book this appointment..." or "Your appointment was not booked"): usually an overlap with an existing class/enrollment reservation, or someone took the slot mid-payment. Suggest another time.
- **App users are not auto-added** to the business client list; a local profile is created when they book, buy or otherwise interact. Same login works on desktop.

## Staff
- Staff can pick a card stored only in the client's Mindbody app account during appointment checkout.
- In app appointment search, staff are filtered by appointment type, then staff eligibility, then location; location is applied only after an instructor is chosen, so staff may look available at the wrong location.

## Gotchas
- Features depend on software package.
