# How to allow clients to cancel their reservations online

Turns client self-cancellation on or off (per service category) for classes/courses and for appointments on the consumer site. Use when clients can't cancel themselves, or when the studio wants to stop them.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link (classes/courses): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_res.asp
- Direct link (appointments): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_appt.asp
- Menu path: **Settings** > **Services** > **Class and Course Options**, or **Services** > **Appointment Options** (searchable from the top bar)

## Steps
### Classes and courses
1. Open **Class and Course Options**.
2. Expand **Booking and Sign-in Policies**.
3. Scroll to Cancellation Windows.
4. Tick (enable) or untick (disable) **Enable Cancellations in Consumer Mode** beside each service category.
5. Optional: in **When**, enter the minimum minutes before start that clients may still cancel.
6. Click **Update** (top right).

### Appointments
1. Open **Appointment Options**.
2. Expand **Appointment System Policies**.
3. Scroll to Cancellation Windows and repeat steps 4 to 6 above.

## Gotchas
- Staff need the Class & Events (Course/Enrollment) Options screen and Appointment Options screen permissions (Settings section).
- Feature depends on software package.
- Also set up cancellation windows to discourage late cancels.
- Clients can cancel class reservations from "My Schedule" on the consumer site even when the class is not bookable online (subject to these settings).
