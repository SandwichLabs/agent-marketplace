# How to set up Cancellation Windows

Cancellation windows define how close to the start time a client can cancel a class, course or appointment before it counts as a late cancel (which can carry a fee) rather than an early cancel. Configured per service category.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_res.asp (Class and course Options); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_appt.asp (Appointment Options)
- Menu path: **Settings > Services > Class and Course Options** or **Settings > Services > Appointment Options**

## Steps
### Classes and courses
1. Open **Class and Course Options**.
2. Expand **Booking and Sign-in Policies**.
3. In Cancellation Windows, enter **Minutes (prior to class)** for each service category; cancelling inside that many minutes is late.
4. Set **Enable Cancellations in Consumer Mode** per category.
5. Click **Update**.

### Appointments
1. Open **Appointment Options**.
2. Expand **Appointment System Policies**.
3. In Cancellation Windows, enter **Minutes** for each appointment service category.
4. Set **Enable Cancellations in Consumer Mode** per category.
5. Click **Update**.

## Settings & fields
- **Minutes (prior to start time)**: notice required. 60 = 1 hour, 1440 = 24 hours. Cancels before the cutoff are early; after are late.
- **Enable Cancellations in Consumer Mode**: ticked shows clients a cancel button online (early or late depending on the window; with no window, always early). Unticked blocks online cancelling for that category, so clients must contact staff, who can process an early or late cancel if windows exist.
- The two settings work independently or together.

## Gotchas
- Needs the Class & Events Option screen and/or Appointment Options screen permission (Settings). Availability depends on software package.
- When a visit is paid by a pricing option from another service category (pricing relationships), the visit's own category window applies, not the pricing option's.
- Windows do not apply to waitlisted clients; they can leave the waitlist anytime with no fee or session deduction.
- For appointments, once the window is reached the online cancel option appears only if payment has been applied to the booking; otherwise the client must contact the business.
- The window enforces, but does not itself define, the written cancellation policy (set in General Setup & Options). Fees are charged separately.
