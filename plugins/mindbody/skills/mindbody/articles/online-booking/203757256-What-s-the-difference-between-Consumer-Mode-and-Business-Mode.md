# Mindbody consumer site vs. Mindbody business site

Short conceptual explanation of the two experiences of the same Mindbody site. Useful so an agent knows which login/view it is in.

## Key points
- **Consumer site** (formerly "Consumer Mode"): what clients see after logging in. Shows their own information, the pricing options and products they may buy, and services they may sign up for. Clients cannot change site settings and generally book only for themselves or family members.
- **Business site** (formerly "Business Mode"): what owners and staff see after logging in. Layout and options differ, and staff typically have far more control, scaled by their staff permissions.

## Gotchas
- Separate login credentials are needed for each experience; a business login does not act as a client login.
- Some features depend on the software package.
