# Mindbody consumer site overview

Describes what clients can do when they log in to the studio's client-facing Mindbody site (consumer mode), and which business-side settings control each area. Useful for understanding what a client sees and where to change it.

## Where in Mindbody
- No business-side screen; this describes the client experience. Related settings live on Class and Course Options, Appointment Options, and General Setup & Options.

## What clients can do
- **Log in**: existing clients use email and password under "Sign In"; new clients use "Create an account"; Mindbody app users synced to the business can use their Mindbody account credentials. A client must fully log out of one Mindbody site before using another.
- **Book appointments** (Appointments tab): controlled by the Appointment Options screen. **Browse Appointment Schedule** (upper right) shows a schedule-style grid.
- **Sign up for classes** (Classes tab): controlled by the Class and Course Options screen. **Sign Up Now** button; day and week views only (Month view exists only on the business site). The button still appears when class and waitlist are at online capacity; clients then land on a "full" message.
- **Sign up for courses** (Courses tab; also called enrollments/workshops/events): same settings screen, **Sign Up Now!** button, same full-capacity behavior.
- **Buy services**: via the online store. If prepayment is required, booking sends the client straight to the cart then Check Out. Account credit can be applied, with any remainder paid by a second method. Purchase policy, terms, and return/refund text show at Check Out.
- **Buy products**: via online store; orders appear in the Manage Online Orders report.
- **Waivers and contracts**: the Liability Waiver client alert makes new accounts acknowledge the waiver electronically (clients signed up by staff are prompted at next login). The Contract Confirmation client alert prompts clients with unsigned contracts.
- **My Info tab** sections: Profile (editable personal data, linked family, email preferences), My Schedule (upcoming bookings; cancel if allowed), Visit History (with Cancellation History link), Purchase History (hide via General Setup & Options setting "Show Client Purchase History in Consumer Mode"), Account (balances by pricing option, purchases, rewards), Documents (only shown if "Allow Clients to View Their Documents in Consumer Mode" is enabled).

## Gotchas
- Paying in the online store needs an integrated Mindbody merchant account. With an outside processor, clients can only buy online using a house account.
- Contracts cannot be bought through the consumer site's book-and-buy flow; they can be bought during booking through branded web tools widgets (Appointment, Schedule, Enrollment).
- On mobile, clients navigate through the three-line menu icon at top right.
