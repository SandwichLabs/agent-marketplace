# How to create links to your Mindbody consumer site

The Links screen generates shareable URLs (classes, appointments, store items, app download, etc.) for a studio's website or emails. Use it whenever a direct link to something bookable or purchasable is needed.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/marketing/links
- Menu path: **Settings** > **Communications & Marketing** > **Links** (or search "Links")

## Steps
1. Open the Links screen.
2. Expand the section for the kind of link wanted.
3. Apply any filters offered.
4. Click the generated link to copy it to the clipboard.
5. Paste it into the website or email.

## Link sections (each has its own article)
- Mindbody app, Branded app (Get Our App buttons)
- Home (consumer site default launch tab), Marketing, Favorite Reports, Video
- Appointments, Classes, Courses
- My Info (consumer mode screen)
- Online Store: Products, Services, Gift Cards, Account Payments, Contracts / Packages

## Gotchas
- Sections shown depend on software level and enabled settings; staff permissions also control visibility.
- To test a link, log out of business mode, use a private window, or use a different browser; otherwise the business login interferes.
- If "Promote the app with my clients" is on (Locations and Mindbody App Listing screen, owner login only), mobile visitors see a "Download the Mindbody app" splash on first visit.
- Dropdown sections on the Links screen can be edited or added from the Client View Settings screen.
