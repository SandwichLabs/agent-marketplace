# How to link to a specific class

Two ways to build a URL that sends a client straight to the reservation flow for one class occurrence.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true (Class Schedule); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/marketing/links (Links)
- Menu path: **Classes**; **Settings > Communications & Marketing > Links**

## Steps
### From the Class Sign In screen
1. **Classes** > click **Sign In** beside the class.
2. At the top beside the class name, right-click **Link** > **Copy link address**.
3. Paste it into an email or website.
- The client must log in first, then lands on Make a Reservation. If the class is free they are signed in directly and taken to My Schedule.

### From the Links screen
1. **Settings > Communications & Marketing > Links**; expand **Classes**.
2. Choose **Service category**, **Class Type**, schedule view **Day** or **Week**, **Location**, **Staff member**, **Date**.
3. Optionally enable the box to start clients on the Log In screen.
4. Click **Copy**.
- **Preview in Consumer Mode** shows the client view but logs you out of the business site; use a second browser to avoid that.

## Gotchas
- Features depend on software package.
