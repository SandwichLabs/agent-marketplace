# How to list your business (Mindbody app)

Publish the studio's listing on the Mindbody consumer app and web through the Mindbody App Dashboard.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path (depends on marketing tools on the site): **Marketing > Marketing Suite > Visit Mindbody App Dashboard**, or **Marketing > Add-On Services > Mindbody App Dashboard**

## Steps
1. Open the Mindbody App Dashboard by one of the paths above.
2. Click the **List My Business** banner at the top.
3. Enable the Mindbody app listing.
4. Click **Edit App Listing** (top right).
5. Review and publish the listing.

## Gotchas
- Only the owner login can opt in.
- Removing or unpublishing the listing requires contacting Mindbody Customer Support.
- Staff need the Business Information Screen permission to change address, location photos or app description.
- Appearing in the app and on the web can take 48 hours or more, also for newly added locations; after clicking List My Business the listing goes live at the next sync (a few hours).
- Unlisted businesses show an "Unpublished" status and banner on the dashboard.
- Clients can only book if pricing options are available for online purchase; otherwise they must contact the business.
- Features depend on software package.
