# No-Show/Late Cancel Fees screen overview

Overview of the screen where standard no-show and late cancel fees are defined per service category and then batch-charged by scheduling autopays. Use it when a studio wants to bill clients for missed or late-cancelled bookings.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/noshowlatecancel
- Menu path: **Settings > Clients > No-Show/Late Cancel Fees** (or search the name in Settings)

## Steps
### Set up fees (Manage Fees tab)
1. Open the screen, go to **Manage Fees**.
2. Per service category (classes and appointments) set cancellation window, late cancel fee and/or no-show fee; optionally add membership-based exemptions (waive N instances per membership billing period) and add/remove tax on the fees. Detail lives in the "manage fees" article.

### Charge fees (Charge Fees tab)
1. Open **Charge Fees**; set the period (up to 90 days) and filters; search by name or ID; sort by column.
2. Review the Member Details table: Name, Location, Classes/Appointments, Date and Time, Pricing Option, Membership, Instances, Exemptions, Fees, Fee Type.
3. Use the checkbox in each row's Fee Type column (labelled "No-show" or "Late Cancel") to add or remove the fee.
4. Click **Charge Fees**; each checked client gets an autopay scheduled to run the next day.

### Require a credit card when booking pay-later appointments (US only)
1. On Appointment Options, turn off "Require Client Payment for Booked Appointments".
2. On this screen ensure either the appointment cancellation window and late cancel fee are above zero, or the no-show fee is above zero.
3. Card is then entered and verified at booking on the Mindbody app and web.

## Settings & fields
- **No-show fee**: client missed/didn't cancel. A session can additionally be deducted via "Deduct no-shows from client's pricing option" (Class and Course Options).
- **Late cancel fee**: cancelled after the window; a session is also deducted from any applicable pricing option.
- Fee type follows the attendance status on the client's Visits screen.

## Gotchas
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first. **Charge Fees** schedules real autopay charges for the next day; verify the checked rows.
- Needs the "Apply No Show/Late Cancel Fees" (Settings) permission. Staff see and charge only locations in their staff profile's default locations.
- "Pays for / is Paid for by" relationships are not supported: the client who missed is charged.
- Clients without stored billing info get the fee debited to their account balance, possibly producing a negative balance.
- Review business policies and notifications before launching fees.
- Keep no-shows on the schedule (or late cancel them after the class) so they remain trackable.
- Reports: Autopay Summary, Autopay Detail (shows next-day scheduled fees immediately), Cancellations, No-Shows, Account Balances.
