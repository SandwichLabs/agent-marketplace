# How to manage early cancellations, late cancellations, and no-shows for classes

How staff cancel a client's class reservation without penalty (early), with penalty (late), or record a no-show. All done from the class Sign In screen.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass?fl=true
- Menu path: **Classes** > **Sign In** beside the class

## Steps
### Early cancel (no penalty)
1. **Classes**, click **Sign In** for the class.
2. Click **X** left of the client's name. They are removed from the roster; any pricing option session already used stays in their account for later use.

### Late cancel (session deducted)
1. **Classes > Sign In** for the class.
2. Tick the **Late Cancel** checkbox at the far right of the client's row. The name stays on the roster but the spot opens for others or the waitlist.

### Mark a no-show
1. **Classes > Sign In** for the missed class.
2. Untick **Signed In** at the far right of the client's row.

## Gotchas
- Permissions: **Cancel reservations** (Classes & Courses section); **Override cancel policy** (Clients section) to bypass the policy.
- Late cancel with no prepaid pricing option leaves the visit unpaid; collect payment or apply a late cancel fee if the business has that policy.
- Clients are normally marked absent once class starts, but the Signed In box may be auto-ticked by auto sign-in settings, and live stream attendees are always auto-signed in on joining.
- To track no-shows, leave the client on the schedule (or late cancel after class). They show as "Absent" on the Visits screen.
- Fees for no-shows: see the no-show/late cancel fees article. The No-Shows report lists them.
- To pay teachers for no-shows set "Pay Teachers for No-Shows" to Yes on the Class and Course Options screen.
