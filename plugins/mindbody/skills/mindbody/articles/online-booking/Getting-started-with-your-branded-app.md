# Branded app set up overview

Orientation to managing a studio's branded mobile app through the Branded App Portal: logins, managers, app settings, promos, push notifications, retention messages, reviews and performance tracking.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://brandedapp.mindbodyonline.com/users/login (Branded App Portal, separate from the business software)

## Key points
- **Login**: credentials are issued during branded app setup; case-sensitive; used only for the app portal. Reset via **Forgot Password?** on the login screen.
- **Managers**: staff can manage app settings with owner-level portal access but cannot create more users. See the add-user, disable-access and staff-login articles.
- **App settings** (portal): hide/show instructor names; hide/show available spots; allow or disallow recurring reservations; disable mobile signups; hide canceled classes; show class/appointment/waitlist tabs under My Info (My Attendance, My Appointments, My Waitlist); enable/disable online signups; appointment appearance and loading; which pricing options are for sale; class self-check-in settings.
- **Promos**: add tabs to the app menu for offers, events, social links.
- **Push notifications (Social Push)**: custom messages to client devices (promotions, urgent schedule changes).
- **Retention Center**: automatic reminders to clients who have not opened the app recently.
- **Ratings and Reviews**: only for apps using the Mindbody Login; prompts opted-in clients shortly after a class or appointment ends.
- **Performance**: Client Acquisition dashboard shows app bookings, sales, intro offers, new profiles.
- **More locations**: each location in the app has a separate charge; add via the data share new-location process.
