# Reasons you may not be able to void a sale

Troubleshooting list for when the void option is missing or fails, with workarounds. Use when a void is requested and the Purchases screen does not allow it.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/appshell/manage/staff (Staff list, for permissions)
- Menu path: client **Client Info > Purchases > Return/Void**; permissions under **Staff > [name] > Permission group**

## Steps
### Fix missing permission
1. Click **Staff** and click your name.
2. Note your permission group, click **Permission group**, and pick it in "Select Permissions Group".
3. Expand **Settings Permissions** and tick **Void/edit past sales**.
4. Expand **Client Permissions** and tick **View client account/purchase history**.
5. Click **Update**, then retry the void.

### Sale pays for class visits
1. Look up the client, open **Client Info**, then **Purchases**.
2. Find the purchase and click **Return/Void**.
3. Click the red **In Use** link beside "Payment Ref #".
4. Click **Make All Unpaid** (or reassign visits to another pricing option).
5. Return to Purchases and void.

### Sale pays for appointment visits
1. Follow steps 1-3 above to reach **In Use**.
2. Use the **Reassign payment** dropdown to move visits to another pricing option; ideally ring the replacement sale first.
3. If none exists, note each appointment's details, click **Early cancel**, then **OK**.
4. Void the sale, then rebook any early-cancelled appointments.

## Gotchas
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first. A void permanently removes the sale; early-cancelling appointments changes the schedule.
- Auto capture (Mindbody Payments or TSYS) settles immediately, so there is no void; refund only.
- On the Essential software package level, only the owner login can void.
- A settled credit card transaction cannot be voided; refund instead. Check the Settled Transactions report, or the Purchases screen, where unbatched sales show "Unsettled transaction".
- An already-returned sale cannot be voided; the return must be undone first (see the undo-return article).
- Promote Payouts sales (Dynamic Pricing, Promoted Intro Offers, Affiliate Networks) can only be refunded: via the Mindbody business app back to the original card, or from desktop only to check, cash or account.
- Some processors do not allow voids: Ezidebit businesses in Australia/New Zealand cannot void; Payouts businesses have no void option but can return. Processor-specific guidelines exist for Paysafe, TSYS, Ezidebit, Elavon US, Elavon Canada/Bluefin and Mindbody Payments.
