# How to remove tax when checking out an appointment

Sell the appointment's pricing option at Point of Sale with tax switched off, then check out the appointment against it.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_retail.asp
- Menu path: **Point of Sale**, then **Appointments**

## Steps
1. Open **Point of Sale** and select the client.
2. In Add Item (right side) click **Services**; search or scroll for the pricing option (a service category dropdown narrows the list).
3. Tick the **No Tax** checkbox.
4. Optional: adjust session count, price or duration.
5. Click **Add Item**.
6. Choose the payment method (e.g. Credit (Visa/MC), Cash) and complete the sale. ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first.
7. Click **Appointments**, click the appointment, choose **Retail** in the menu to finish checkout (it uses the just-purchased pricing option).

## Gotchas
- Package-dependent.
- The tax is removed on the pricing option sale, not at appointment checkout itself, so the sale must be completed first.
- Source gives no guidance on tax-compliance; confirm the exemption is legitimate with the user.
