# How to set up tips, print tip slips, and leave tips for staff

Covers enabling tips, letting staff earn them, printing appointment tip slips, and ringing a tip at the Point of Sale or appointment checkout.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp (General Setup & Options); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/staff (Staff); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_appt.asp (Appointment Options); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index (Appointment Schedule); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_retail.asp (Point of Sale)
- Menu path: **Settings > General > General Setup and Options**; **Staff**; **Settings > Services > Appointment Options**; **Appointments**; **Point of Sale**

## Steps
### Enable tips (on by default)
1. Open General Setup and Options and expand **Retail Settings**.
2. Tick **Tips** and click **Update** (top right of the section).
3. If tips are included in paychecks, tick **Tips Included in Cash Drawer & Payroll** (appears only after Tips is enabled and updated).

### Let a staff member receive tips
1. Click **Staff**, then the staff member's name.
2. On the Staff Profile, tick **Earns tips** in Sales settings and click **Save**.

### Print a tip slip (appointments only)
1. In Appointment Options, expand **Appointment System Policies**, tick **Track Appointment Arrivals**, click **Update**.
2. On the Appointment Schedule, click the appointment and choose **Mark Arrived**.
3. Click it again, choose **Modify**, click **Print Tip Slip** at the top, then **Print Tip Slip** again to open the print dialog. Tip slips are also available from Check in/Check out.

### Enter a tip at Point of Sale
1. Open **Point of Sale** and select the client.
2. Under Add Item click **Tips**.
3. Choose the recipient in **Search or select tip recipient**.
4. Enter the amount and click **Add Item** (editable on the ticket afterward).
5. Choose the payment method (e.g. Credit (Visa/MC), Cash) and complete the sale. Works for prepaid appointments too.

### Tip when completing a card/check/cash appointment
1. On **Appointments**, click the appointment and choose **Retail**.
2. The pricing option auto-fills (changeable; price, quantity and discount editable).
3. Enter the tip amount or percentage on the right; edit on the ticket if needed.
4. Choose payment method and complete the sale.

## Gotchas
- Tip slips are appointment-only. They print the highest-priced option that is active, not contract-only, and either single-session or covering the needed visits per the number-deducted setting, so it may differ from what the client is using.
- Tips always appear on the Tips report and Cash Drawer report. They appear on Payroll only if **Tips Included in Cash Drawer & Payroll** is on; if off, they show as Paid Out on the Cash Drawer report.
- Printing slips from the Appointments screen via arrivals also needs "Track Appointment Confirmations" and "Track Appointment Arrivals" enabled.
- A partial refund of a tip is removed entirely from staff payroll (other Payroll documentation describes proportional reduction; verify on the Payroll report).
- Appointment Checkout screen users can add tips after the sale.
- Availability depends on software package.
