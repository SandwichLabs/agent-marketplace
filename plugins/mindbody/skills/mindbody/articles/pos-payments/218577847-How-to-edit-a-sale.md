# How to edit a sale

Lets staff correct a completed sale: move it to another client, or change its date, location, sales rep, payment method, or commission recipient. Use for mistakes found after checkout.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: client lookup > **Purchases** > **Sale ID** > Manage Sales > **Edit Sale**

## Steps
1. Look up the client and click **Purchases**.
2. Click the **Sale ID** left of the sale (use **Go to detail view** to see items).
3. On Manage Sales click **Edit Sale** (right).
4. Change the needed fields.
5. Click **Update Sale**.

## Settings & fields
- **Change Client**: moves the whole sale record to another client's account. Does not change payment info (e.g. card used); to change who paid, void/return and re-ring. For a contract sale, only the contract item moves; the autopay schedule stays on the original account. To move autopay too, terminate the contract and sell it to the new client.
- **Sale Date**: new date by typing or calendar. Useful when a sale was entered late without backdating; do not use it to work around pricing option activation dates.
- **Location**: multi-location only. For card payments it only changes where income is reported, not the bank deposit. For products, inventory between locations must be adjusted manually (redoing the sale may be quicker).
- **Rep**: change the sales rep; updates Sales, Sales by Rep, and Sales by Category reports.
- **Payment Method**: changeable only if originally paid by check, cash, no-auth method, etc. Not changeable for credit card or bank account payments. A sale paid by account cannot be switched to another method, but cash/check/no-auth sales can be switched to account.
- **Commission Recipient / Split Commission With**: change the staff member; updates Commission and Payroll reports.

## Gotchas
- ⚠️ Financial records change; confirm with the user first.
- Edit Sale is unavailable if any item in the sale was previously returned; void the return first (credit card returns cannot be voided).
- Edit Sale button is not available for businesses in France.
- Required staff permissions: Settings: Manager Corrections - Edit Sales, Complete sales transactions at POS, Edit sale date, Void/edit past sales; Clients: View client account/purchase history; Sales Team Management: Ability to view all clients.
- Package-dependent features.
