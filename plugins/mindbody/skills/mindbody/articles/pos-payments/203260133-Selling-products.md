# How to sell products through the Point of Sale screen

Walkthrough for ringing up a retail product to a client at the POS. Use when a studio wants to sell merchandise, or when a product is not showing in the POS.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_retail.asp
- Menu path: **Point of Sale** in the left navigation panel

## Steps
1. Open **Point of Sale**.
2. Search for and select the buying client.
3. In the Add Item box on the right, open the **Products** tab.
4. Choose **All products** or **Favorites**. On All products, a dropdown can narrow by category.
5. Scroll the list, or type the product name and click **Search**.
6. Click the product name.
7. Adjust quantity, price, or commission (if commission is enabled) as needed.
8. Click **Add Item**.
9. Pick the payment method and receipt options to finish the sale.

## Gotchas
- Staff need the "Complete sales transactions at POS" (Settings) permission.
- The product must already exist (created on the Retail Products screen).
- Products cannot be sold through the Mindbody app, branded app, or branded web tools.
- Selling professional products to staff requires linking the staff member's client profile to their staff profile.
- If a product does not appear in the POS, check: it may be discontinued (inactive); the product may carry membership restrictions; or the client's membership may block non-member products (setting "Allow Members to Purchase Non-Member Products" on the Membership Settings screen).
- Check sales afterward with the Sales by Product report.
