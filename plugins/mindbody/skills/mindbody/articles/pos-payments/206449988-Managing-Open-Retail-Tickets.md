# How to use open tickets on the Point of Sale screen

Open Tickets lets staff save a client's POS cart to finish later, and optionally email or print it as a quote. Use for undecided buyers, tip pre-auth workflows, or parking a sale.

## Where in Mindbody
- Enable at: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp (**Settings** > **General** > **General Setup and Options**)
- Use at: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_retail.asp (**Point of Sale**)

## Steps
### Enable
1. Open General Setup and Options, expand **Retail Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Open Tickets**, tick it, click **Update**.

### Create
1. On Point of Sale, find or add the client and add items.
2. Click **Save Ticket**. A "[NAME] HAS A SAVED TICKET!" message appears and the client is added to the **Open Tickets** dropdown.

### Locate / edit
1. Pick the ticket from the **Open Tickets** dropdown (or tick the box in the saved-ticket message after selecting the client).
2. Add or remove items on the right, then complete the sale.

### Delete
1. Load the ticket, click the red **X** by each item. Removing all items deletes the ticket.

### Email a quote
1. Prerequisites: enable the Open Ticket Quote notification (Notifications settings) and mark the staff member as a sales rep (Sales Team Management).
2. Select the ticket and click **Email Ticket**.

### Print a quote
1. Select the ticket and click **Print Ticket**.

## Gotchas
- Staff permissions: "Complete sales transactions at POS" (create/complete); "Ability to edit others open tickets" (edit/delete others' tickets); "Notifications" (email settings); "Manage staff member settings" (set sales reps).
- Must be enabled if the business collects tips (uses tip-pending pre-auth). To turn Open Tickets off, first turn off Tips in General Setup & Options.
- Only staff marked as sales reps can email tickets; owner logins cannot.
- Multiple tickets per client allowed; each shows client name and save date. Tickets are tied to one client profile and cannot be reassigned.
- Pre-authorized tip tickets (CC Key/Stored or CC Swipe, preliminary receipt printed) are auto-voided if not completed within 7-10 business days. They can place a temporary full-amount hold, showing as pending charges on the client's bank statement (not duplicates). Delete unneeded tickets to release them.
- Cross-platform: POS tickets finish only on POS; tickets started in appointment checkout or the Business app can be completed in either of those two.
- Alternative: sell with the Account payment method to create a negative balance the client pays later.
