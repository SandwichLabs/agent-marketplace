# How to accept ACH payments (US clients)

ACH lets a US business charge a client's bank account (account and routing number). Equivalents elsewhere: Direct Debit, SEPA, EFT. This article explains how it is enabled and what to watch for.

## Steps
1. Contact Mindbody Merchant Support to add (or remove) ACH on the merchant account; they may send forms to complete.
2. Wait 2-5 business days for ACH to appear on the site.

(No in-app toggle is described; enabling is done through Merchant Support.)

## Gotchas
- Bank accounts must be debit/checking type, not savings.
- Declines can arrive 5-7 business days after the attempt and carry a decline fee, so one-time sales (e.g. class packs) are harder to recover than recurring autopay items. Prefer ACH for autopay.
- Always keep a backup credit card on file; prefer enrolling clients with a good card-payment history.
- Chargebacks are allowed; notice is by email only.
- Deposit times differ by payment method.
- Some features depend on software package.
