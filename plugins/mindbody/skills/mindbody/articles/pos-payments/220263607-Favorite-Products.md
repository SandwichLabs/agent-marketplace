# Favorite products

Marking retail products as favorites puts them in a quick list at the Point of Sale, and clicking one drops it straight onto the ticket. Works in the business site and the Mindbody Business app.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link (Retail products): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_prod.asp
- Direct link (Point of Sale): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_retail.asp?fl=true&tabID=3
- Direct link (Appointments): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index
- Direct link (General Setup & Options): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp
- Menu path: **Services & Products > Retail Products**; **Point of Sale**; **Settings > General > General Setup & Options**

## Steps
### Mark a product as a favorite
1. Go to **Services & Products > Retail Products** and click **Search** to list products.
2. Tick the star icon at the far right of each product to favorite. Click **Save**.
3. Alternative: click a product name, expand **Additional Information**, choose **Yes, this is a point of sale favorite product** (No means you must search for it at POS), then **Save**.

### Sell a favorite from Point of Sale
1. Click **Point of Sale** and find the client.
2. In Add Item, click **Products**, then **Favorites** (skipped if Quick Add is on).
3. Click the product; it is added to the ticket automatically.
4. Add anything else and finish the sale.

### Sell via Appointment Checkout (shows photo, colour, size)
1. Open **Appointments**, click the appointment, choose **Checkout**.
2. If unpaid, pick the paying pricing option and **Add to Cart**.
3. On the Checkout screen click **Favorites**, click the product, then complete the sale.

### Mindbody Business app
1. Tap **Retail**, look up the client, tap **Favorites**, tap the product (auto-added to cart), complete the sale.

### Quick Add (open POS on Favorites)
1. **Settings > General > General Setup & Options**, expand **Retail Settings**.
2. Set "Add Item - Default to" to **Products - Quick Adds**, click **Update**.

## Gotchas
- Products must already exist.
- Some features depend on the software package.
- If several variants of one product are favorited, POS shows only the main product name; variant detail is visible only in Appointment Checkout or the Business app.
- Favorites auto-add to the ticket and cannot be edited there (e.g. no discount). To edit first, find the product through Retail products instead.
- No option exists to turn off the auto-add; the "Auto Add Products to Ticket" setting in General Setup & Options only governs whether details can be edited before adding.
- If an appointment has already been checked out, sell the product from Point of Sale instead.
