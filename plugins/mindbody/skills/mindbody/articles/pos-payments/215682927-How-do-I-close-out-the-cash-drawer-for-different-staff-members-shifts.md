# How do I close out the cash drawer for different staff members/shifts?

Recommended cash-handling routine when several staff share one register, using the Cash Drawer report per shift and the Daily Closeout report at day's end.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/appshell/shuttle/Report/Sales/CashDrawer
- Menu path: **Insights** > **Reports** > **Cash Drawer**

## Steps
1. Opening count: first staff member of the day counts the drawer, records each bill count and the total (starting total). A standard paper/form for shift totals is recommended.
2. During the shift: only one staff member handles the drawer and runs all retail transactions for that period (accountability).
3. Close the shift: staff member counts the drawer, records the total, subtracts the starting funds, and hands over. Then in the Cash Drawer report:
   1. Section 1 Dates: select the date and time of the shift.
   2. Section 2 Filters: choose **Location**, then **Entered by** to pick the staff member (default: all staff members).
   3. Section 3 Options: **By Date** or **By Employee**.
   4. Section 4 Generate: click **Go!**.
   5. Compare the cash and check totals with the counted/recorded funds.
4. After the last shift, enter the day's totals in the **Daily Closeout** report.

## Gotchas
- Daily Closeout "Close Data" cannot be undone once a date range is closed (see the Daily Closeout report).
- Package-dependent features.
