# How the Daily Closeout report is used to balance the cash drawer at the end of the day

Short orientation to the **Daily Closeout** report: the end-of-day check that compares what the software recorded against the physical cash drawer.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_rpt_dailycloseout.asp?category=Sales
- Menu path: **Insights** > **Reports** > **Sales** > **Administrative** > **Daily Closeout** (or search "Daily Closeout")

## Steps
1. Run Daily Closeout for the day or date range to see cash recorded at Point of Sale.
2. Count the physical drawer and compare.
3. If they match, "Close" that date and bank the cash.
4. If not, investigate: wrong amount taken, wrong payment type chosen at sale, or missing money.

## Settings & fields
- **Include Subcategories** checkbox (report filter) shows primary and secondary revenue categories.

## Gotchas
- Closing a date is the final step after matching; detailed behavior is in the Daily Closeout report article (not repeated here).
- Some features depend on software package.
- Favorite the report if used daily.
