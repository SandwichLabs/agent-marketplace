# Options for charging US clients for credit card processing fees

Policy overview of three ways a US studio can recoup card fees: raising prices, offering a cash discount, or surcharging. Read before configuring any fee or discount at checkout. No click path here; it is rules and guidance.

## Where in Mindbody
- No screen given. For the Mindbody Payments fee tool see the Transaction Fee article (Related).

## Settings & fields
Options:
- **Pricing updates** (the recommended route): raise prices to absorb processing costs.
- **Cash discount**: advertise card price, discount cash payers. Fewer rules than surcharging.
  - Post details at entrance and point of sale (dual pricing works).
  - Offer the discount verbally before an in-person sale.
  - Show it as a separate discount line on the receipt; a receipt message restating the discount is compliant.
  - Apply a discount for cash payers; adding then removing a fee counts as a surcharge. A cash-discount transaction must not carry both a surcharge and a discount.
- **Surcharging**: fee added at purchase, shown as its own receipt line. Most regulated.
  - State law may forbid it; check first.
  - Credit cards only, never debit or prepaid, even if run as credit.
  - Max 3%.
  - Register with Mastercard at least 30 days before starting.
  - Notify the payment processor for Visa.
  - Post notice at the physical location and at POS/checkout.
  - Mindbody Payments supports it for some US clients via the Transaction Fee setting.

## Gotchas
- Mindbody and its processors do not recommend either cash discounts or surcharges; they suggest pricing to cover costs.
- Compliance risk sits with the business; confirm legal/network requirements with the user before enabling any fee.
