# How to update a client's billing information

Add, change or remove the card or bank account stored on a client profile (also the payment source for their autopays).

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: client profile > **Client Info** > expand **Billing Information**

## Steps
1. Search for and select the client, click **Client Info**.
2. Expand **Billing Information**.
3. Enter, edit or clear the details (below).
4. Click **Save**. If the name differs from the profile name an alert appears; click **OK** to continue.

## Settings & fields
- Credit card: **Copy from contact info** (autofills billing address), Cardholder Name / Address / City / State / Postal code, **Swipe card to store** (MagTek Magstripe Mini Swipe Reader only), **CC Type** (auto after save), **CC Number**, **CC Expiration** (month and year dropdowns).
- Bank account (only with a merchant account supporting ACH, PAP, Direct Debit or SEPA): Name on account, Bank routing number, Bank account number, Account Type (Checking or Savings). International labels may differ.
- Links at the bottom: **Autopay Schedule** (future), **Autopay History** (past), **Add New Autopay Schedule** (selling a contract is preferred).

## Gotchas
- Staff need View client billing information and Edit client billing information permissions.
- Only one card per profile; a new card replaces the old one and is also what autopays use. Use the separate article if two cards are needed for different autopays. Clients can keep other cards on a linked Mindbody account for appointment checkout.
- State/Province options follow the site's country; a card from another country may not be storable (client then uses their Mindbody app card).
- With no billing address saved, autopays send the profile address to the bank.
- Wrong data often comes from entering details in the wrong profile, merging the wrong clients, multiple open tabs, or browser autofill.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: replacing or deleting the stored card changes where future autopays are charged.
