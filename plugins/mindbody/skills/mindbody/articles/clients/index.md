# Clients

Client profiles, lookup, types and indexes, relationships/family accounts, documents and waivers, duplicates.

27 pages. Each links its Mindbody support source.

- [Client Account Details screen](215843418-Client-Account-Details-Screen-overview.md) — Overview of the per-client screen showing autopays, unpaid visits, pricing options, contracts, house account, rewards and third-party payments. Use it to inspect or fix a client's balances, passes, and contracts.
- [Client Documents screen](215843448-Client-documents-screen-overview.md) — Where staff upload, view and manage files, client forms, waivers and contract agreements for a specific client. Needed to store paperwork on a profile or to control what the client sees online.
- [Client forms overview](Client-Forms-screen-overview.md) — How to build paperless client forms (intake questions, policies, optional liability waiver, signature/initials) and control who gets them and when. Use when a studio wants forms sent automatically with bookings, on profile creation, or on membership purchase.
- [Client indexes and client index values](203259183-Client-Indexes.md) — Client indexes are custom categories (Location, Age, Skill Level) each holding a list of values; a client gets one value per index. Use for segmentation, searching, mailing lists and reports.
- [Client indexes and client types FAQ](203276923-About-Client-Indexes-and-Client-Types.md) — Explains how client indexes and client types differ and how to find, bulk-assign and auto-assign them. Use it when segmenting clients for lists, reports or marketing.
- [Client Info screen overview](Client-Info-screen-overview.md) — The Client Info screen is the hub for one client's contact data, billing card, waiver, status, indexes, types and more. It is a two-column layout: a left summary column and right-hand collapsible modules (14 default, up to 25 depending on enabled features). Needed for almost any client edit.
- [Client profile information FAQ](203273113-Questions-about-the-client-profile.md) — Quick answers for common Client Info screen tasks: email subscriptions, client IDs, creation info, documents, photos, reactivation, default location. Use when a client-profile field or button is missing or behaves oddly.
- [Client Purchases screen](215843338-Client-Purchases-Screen-overview.md) — The per-client history of sales, returns/refunds and account payments, newest first. Use it to find a sale, reprint or email a receipt, export purchase history, or jump to Manage Sales for a return, void or edit.
- [Client Relationship Types settings screen](203259403-Client-relationships-explained.md) — Explains the built-in and custom relationship types that link client profiles, some informational and some that change behavior (paying for another client, sharing memberships). Use before assigning a relationship or creating a custom type.
- [Client Types screen](203259193-Client-Types.md) — Client types are extra labels (e.g. senior, student, veteran) you create and attach to client profiles. Once assigned they can filter reports and act as prerequisites for classes or courses.
- [Client Visits screen](215843328-Client-Visits-Screen-overview.md) — Shows a client's past (and optionally future) visits with status, payment and who booked, plus login, appointment and cancellation views. Use it to check attendance, find who made a booking, or trace which pricing option paid.
- [Custom fields on the Client Info screen](203259333-Client-custom-fields.md) — Lets a studio add unlimited extra date, number or text fields to client profiles and search by them. Use when the studio needs to store data (due date, coupon code) that standard fields do not cover.
- [Family accounts FAQ](Family-accounts-FAQ.md) — Family accounts let one primary Mindbody account holder book, buy and manage profiles for dependents. This page covers what staff can and cannot do, client-side rules, and platform limits. Needed when a parent asks about adding kids, duplicate profiles, or linking accounts.
- [General Setup & Options screen: Client Management](General-Setup-Options-screen-Client-Management.md) — Reference for every toggle in the Client Management section of General Setup & Options. Many client features (alerts, documents, prospects, suspensions, negative balances) only appear after a setting here is turned on.
- [How to add new clients on your Mindbody site](Adding-Editing-Clients-Mindbody-Experience.md) — Describes the five places a client profile can be created (search bar, Client Directory, Appointments, Point of Sale, Class Sign In) and what the form fields mean. Needed whenever an agent has to create a client.
- [How to assign or remove client relationships](203259383-Assigning-Client-Relationships.md) — Client relationships link clients (parent and child, shared pricing options or memberships, one paying for another). Use this to add or remove those links from Client Info or while creating a new client.
- [How to manage active and inactive client profiles](203257863-How-do-I-delete-deactivate-a-client.md) — Clients cannot be deleted from Mindbody; they can be deactivated (hidden) and reactivated. Covers deactivating, reactivating, finding inactive profiles, and the automatic deactivation setting.
- [How to search for clients using the Client Directory (Advanced Client Search)](203259203-Looking-up-clients.md) — Explains quick client search in the toolbar and the full Client Directory with its search fields, filters and saved searches. Use it to find a client or build a filtered client list.
- [How to set up and manage client alerts](203259733-Client-alerts.md) — Client alerts are pop-ups shown when staff open a client (or when clients use the consumer site) to flag account issues such as failed autopays, missing waivers or low sessions. They do not change site functionality.
- [How to set up and manage liability waivers](203259873-Liability-waiver.md) — A business has one liability waiver that every client agrees to. This page covers creating it, expiry and reminder options, where clients sign, and how staff manage statuses.
- [How to set up client required fields](202988236-Client-required-fields.md) — Controls which client details must be filled in when a client account is created or edited, separately for staff-side (business) and client-side (consumer) entry. Use it when a studio wants to force collection of phone, address, etc.
- [How to update a client's billing information](203257913-How-do-I-update-my-clients-billing-information.md) — Add, change or remove the card or bank account stored on a client profile (also the payment source for their autopays).
- [Locate Duplicate Clients tool](203259613-Locate-Duplicate-Clients-tool.md) — Finds client profiles that probably belong to the same person (different email, formal vs nickname such as David and Dave) so they can be merged.
- [Merge Duplicate Clients tool](203259603-Merge-Duplicate-Clients-tool.md) — Combines two client profiles into one, carrying over purchases, visits, billing and other history from the profile being removed into the one being kept.
- [Tagging clients FAQ](203256033-Tagging.md) — Tagging lets the software remember the clients shown on one screen (a report, class roster, directory) so they can be reused: filter other reports to just those clients, build mailing lists, bulk-modify them, or register them all into a class or course.
- [Unmask Merged Clients tool](Unmask-Merged-Clients-tool.md) — Separates two profiles that were merged by mistake back into two working profiles. It is a partial rescue, not a true undo.
- [Where clients agree to the liability waiver in Consumer Mode](206430507-Where-do-my-clients-agree-to-my-liability-waiver-in-Consumer-Mode.md) — Explains how and where clients accept your liability waiver on the Mindbody consumer site, and how staff see that acceptance in business mode.
