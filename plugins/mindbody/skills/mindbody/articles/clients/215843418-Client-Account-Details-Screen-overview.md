# Client Account Details screen

Overview of the per-client screen showing autopays, unpaid visits, pricing options, contracts, house account, rewards and third-party payments. Use it to inspect or fix a client's balances, passes, and contracts.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Find a client** field at top > click client name > **Account Details**
- Can be set as the default landing page for client profiles (**More** dropdown, top right).

## Steps
### Navigate
1. Search the client, click their name, click **Account Details**.
2. Use **Show all Dates** or **Select date range** to widen what shows.

### Pricing option actions (three-dot menu in Actions column)
- **Return/Void**: goes to Manage Sales.
- **Edit**: opens Edit Pricing Option for sale notes, Active checkbox, number of sessions, activation date, duration (membership details themselves are not editable here).
- **Show Visits**: opens Account History to see visits paid by that option; per visit **Early Cancel** (no penalty), **Late Cancel** (keeps the payment), **Reassign Payment** (dropdown of other eligible options), **Make Unpaid** / **Make All Unpaid**.

### Contract actions
- **View**, **Print**, **Autopays (#/#)** (opens Autopay Schedule), **Suspend**, **Terminate**, **Delete**.

## Settings & fields
Sections (10 rows per table, paginated; sections expanded by default):
- **Autopays**: tabs **Autopay Schedule**, **Autopay History**, **Add New Autopay Schedule**.
- **Unpaid Visits**: Visit Date (booking date, not service date), Payment Ref # (hidden placeholder pricing option), Service Category, Scheduled (future unpaid), # Owed (past unpaid). Stays until visits are paid or cancelled; autopay clients booking before the autopay runs show here.
- **Available for Use**: Paid, Payment Ref #, Description, Payment Method, Amount, Scheduled (committed to future bookings), Remaining (net of scheduled), Activation Date, Expiration Date, Notes.
- **Inactive**: options that are fully used, expired, not yet activated, or returned. Reactivate expired ones via the activation-date edit article.
- **Contracts**: Agreement Date, Confirmed (green check or red dot; yellow envelope resends the Contract Reminder email), Sold by ("Client" for online purchases, "Auto Renew" for renewals), Contract, Notes, Start/End Date, Contract Deposit, **Auto Renewing?** checkbox (clear before last autopay to stop renewal), Autopays, Status (Terminated, Suspended, Active, Inactive, Future Start Date).
- **GIFT/Debit Account**: Paid, Account Activity # (link to Manage Sales), Description, Notes, Account Credit, Account Debit, Return/Void, Balance, Current Balance (negative in red), **Create Statement**.
- **Client Rewards**: Date, Time, Source, Status (Earned/Redeemed), Expiration Date (only affects points earned after enabling), Points, balance and value.
- **Third-party Payments**: Paid, Account Activity, Third-party payer, **Deny Payment** (turns the rejected charge into a negative balance), Notes, Account Credit.
- **Filters/buttons**: date range, **Recalculate** (re-links payments to unpaid visits and refreshes expired options still shown as available), **More**, per-column sort/filter/hide.

## Gotchas
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: **Return/Void**, **Terminate** (removes remaining scheduled autopays and moves options to Inactive), **Delete** contract (erases the contract record and its autopays; terminate is recommended instead), **Deny Payment**, and Late Cancel.
- Screen contents vary with staff permissions; section colors are fixed.
- If a client has a balance but GIFT/Debit Account is missing, widen the date range. Default window is the **Client History Windows - Default** setting in General Setup & Options.
- Autopays not tied to a contract are not in Contracts; use the Autopay Schedule screen.
