# How to set up and manage liability waivers

A business has one liability waiver that every client agrees to. This page covers creating it, expiry and reminder options, where clients sign, and how staff manage statuses.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/clients/waivers-intakes
- Menu path: Settings > General > **Client Forms & Liability Waiver**

## Steps
1. Open Client Forms & Liability Waiver.
2. In the Liability Waiver section, click **Create Liability Waiver** (top right).
3. Choose the settings below and type the waiver text.
4. Click **Done**.
5. To edit later, click the waiver name; the trash can icon deletes it.

## Settings & fields
- **Require waiver acknowledgement**: clients must acknowledge it during booking; also lets you attach it to client forms.
- **Expiration settings**: off by default (signed waivers never expire). When on, set a number plus Days, Months or Years (default 12 months).
- **Automated Reminders**: set Hours or Days before a booking; clients with a pending waiver get the Client Forms Notification. Nothing is sent at the moment of expiry itself.
- Waiver text box with formatting options (character limit applies).
- Expired shows as a label in Client Info (Additional information), on the appointment booking, and as an icon on Class Sign In.
- Staff fixes on Client Info: **Mark complete** (sidebar), **Mark Liability Waiver complete** (Additional information), or **Email liability waiver to client**.
- Sign-in channels: business site, Business app (captures signature), consumer site, branded web tools, Mindbody app and web, branded app (captures signature), Messenger[ai].

## Gotchas
- Only one waiver per business; it cannot differ by service category.
- Turning expiration on applies retroactively: waivers signed longer ago than the new period expire immediately, possibly for many clients at once. Turning it off does not revive them.
- A signed copy is uploaded to the profile automatically via the Mindbody app, Business app, branded app or branded web tools; other methods need manual upload or paper.
- To save waivers from the Business app, enable **Allow Clients to Upload Documents**; to let clients view them on the consumer site, enable **Allow Clients to View Their Documents in Consumer Mode** (both in Client Management settings).
- Client forms never show an expired label, and neither forms nor waivers show an upcoming expiry date.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: deleting the waiver or switching on expiry affects every client's status.
