# Client Relationship Types settings screen

Explains the built-in and custom relationship types that link client profiles, some informational and some that change behavior (paying for another client, sharing memberships). Use before assigning a relationship or creating a custom type.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_relships.asp
- Menu path: Settings > Clients > Relationship Types (also from the **More** menu on the Client Directory)
- Assigned relationships appear on the client's Client Info screen (Relationships module).

## Steps
### Create a custom type
1. Open Relationship Types.
2. Fill **Relationship Name**, **Associated Relationship Name** (shown on the linked client, e.g. Child or Employee) and **Sort Order**.
3. Click **Add New Relationship**.

### Manage existing types
1. Edit names, **Active**, **Sort Order** in the lower list, then click **Update**.
2. Delete removes a type permanently.

### Assigning to clients
- Business site: see the assign/remove relationships article; also possible in the Business app. Clients on the consumer site can assign family relationship types only (My Info > Profile shows parent, child, sibling, spouse only).

## Settings & fields
Informational types: Family of/for, Friend of/for, Parent/Guardian, Child of/for, Partner of/for, Sibling of/for, Spouse of/for.

Functional types:
- **Pays for / is Paid for by**: one client permanently pays for another's purchases; the sale shows on the payer, the service on the receiver's Account Details. Works on POS and the consumer online store, not on Appointment Checkout or other platforms.
- **Shares pricing option with**: each can use pricing options either buys.
- **Shares Membership with**: shares membership status and benefits.
- **is the Company of/for** and **is the Employee of/for**: corporate/third-party payer setups.
- **Receives Emails for**: copies of the other client's notification emails (e.g. a parent).
- **Referred / Referred By**: for Client Rewards or referral programs.

## Gotchas
- Permissions: View client info, Edit client info (assign), Set up relationship types (custom types), and Ability to view all clients.
- Default types vary by software package.
- Custom types do not change software behavior.
- Deactivating a type keeps existing assignments but blocks new ones. ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: deleting a type cannot be undone; a Pays for relationship moves charges onto another client.
- For a one-time payment for someone else, use the Pay for another client function at POS instead of a relationship.
