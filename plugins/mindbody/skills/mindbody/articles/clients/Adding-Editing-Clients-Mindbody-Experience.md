# How to add new clients on your Mindbody site

Describes the five places a client profile can be created (search bar, Client Directory, Appointments, Point of Sale, Class Sign In) and what the form fields mean. Needed whenever an agent has to create a client.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link (Client Directory): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_clt_lkup.asp
- Direct link (Appointments): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index
- Direct link (Point of Sale): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/main_retail.asp
- Direct link (Class Schedule): https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/classic/admmainclass
- Menu path: **Clients** (Client Directory), **Appointments**, **Point of Sale**, **Classes**; also the **Find a client** search bar at top of every page.

## Steps
### Search bar (fastest)
1. Click **Find a client**, then **+Add New Client**, fill the form.

### Client Directory
1. Click **Clients** in the left nav, then **Add New Client**.
2. Fill the form, click **Add New Client** at the bottom. You land on the new profile; add more details there.

### Appointments screen
1. Open **Appointments**, select a schedule block on the calendar.
2. Click **Add new client** (bottom left of booking panel), fill the left-panel form (the **More** dropdown reveals home phone, work phone/extension, address), click **Add**.
3. Finish booking the appointment.

### Point of Sale
1. Open **Point of Sale**, click **Add new client**, fill the form, click **Add Client**.
2. Add items to ticket and complete the sale.

### Class Sign In
1. **Classes**, click **Sign In** beside a class.
2. Click **Search for client**, then **Add new client** from the menu; fill the form, click **Add Client**.
3. Confirm the client appears on the roster.

## Settings & fields
Search bar form:
- **First and last name**, **Email**: locked for the business once the client has set up their own Mindbody account.
- **Mobile number** and **Mobile provider**: provider is required for text messaging.
- **Client ID**: set or change the ID, or tie it to a key tag/swipe card.
- **Locker**, **Referral Type** (or **Other**), **Prospective client** checkbox, **Birthday** (gift icon on class sign-in during birthday week), **Gender**, **Address** (and home studio if multi-location).
- **Emergency contact**: needs the Children's Program Features setting on.
- **Billing information**: store card; ACH goes in the Bank Account section.
- **Subscription preferences**: Account management (contracts/autopay), Reminders and schedule changes (bookings), News and promos (marketing). Staff can opt a client out of text; opting in to News and Promos by text must be done by the client when logged in online.
- **Home Phone**, **Work phone and extension**: only shown if made required fields.

Extra fields on the Client Directory form:
- **Swipe ID to store address**; **Company** (marks a business account for Third-Party Payers; reveals **Legal name** and **DBA**); **RSSID** (Client ID); **Middle name**.
- **Gender** defaults: Female, Male, No gender selected, Would rather not say (more can be added).
- **Prospect stage selection**, **Rep1 selection** (needs Sales Team Management), **Home studio**.
- **Send welcome email**: sends the New Client Welcome (Business Mode) email; appears and works only if that auto email is enabled and the client has opted in to Account management emails.
- **Client measurements** (nine fields, costume tracking), **Client indexes**, **Relationships** (search related client then choose relationship).
- **Add family members**: click **Add Family Member** repeatedly; all are created when you click **Add New Client**.

Point of Sale / Class Sign In forms add **ID #** (scan barcode), **Prospect**, **Mobile phone**, and home/work phone under **More** unless required.

## Gotchas
- Staff need the **Add Client** permission (Clients section of staff permissions).
- Profiles can only be created one at a time; bulk import exists only via the Client Contact Copy (CCC) conversion, handled by onboarding or Customer Support.
- Required-field changes take up to one hour to show in the search-bar form but are immediate in the Client Directory.
- **Add Family Member** creates ordinary profiles, not a Family Account. For a real family account, do not create dependents; the primary client adds them from their own login.
- Default notification preference is email only (privacy compliance).
- Enable "Add New Client - Default City" in General Setup & Options to autofill city.
