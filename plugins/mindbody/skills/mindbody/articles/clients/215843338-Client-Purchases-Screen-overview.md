# Client Purchases screen

The per-client history of sales, returns/refunds and account payments, newest first. Use it to find a sale, reprint or email a receipt, export purchase history, or jump to Manage Sales for a return, void or edit.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Find a client** (top) > select client > **Purchases** in the client submenu
- Can be set as default client landing page via **More** (top right).

## Steps
1. Search the client, click the name, click **Purchases**.
2. Choose **Show all Dates** or **Select date range**.
3. Click a **Sale ID** to open Manage Sales, or **Return/Void** in Actions.
4. For receipts: printer icon (Print Receipt, Print Gift Receipt, Email Receipt), envelope icon (email transaction), PDF icon.

## Settings & fields
- Columns: **Sale ID**, **Sale Date**, **Payment Method** (underlined = linked to settled Mindbody payment processing; non-underlined = external, e.g. "VISA/MC No Auth", versus integrated "CC Swipe"/"CC Key/Stored"), **Description**, **Studio**, **Color & Size**, **Price/Sale/Subtotal**, **Quantity**, **Discount** (blank if price manually adjusted), **Tax**, **Amount Paid**, **Payment Ref#**, **Actions**.
- **Detail or Summary view**: summary shows only transaction, date, method, total; detail recommended.
- **All Purchases** filter: Products Only or Services Only.
- **Download Purchases PDF**: whole date range; PDF icon beside a Sale ID exports that sale.
- **Relationship icon**: hover shows who the purchase was for (beside Payment Ref # in detail view).
- Column sort/filter/hide; default date window set by **Client History Windows - Default** in General Setup & Options (Client Management).

## Gotchas
- Items bought for another client appear only on the payer's Purchases screen, not the recipient's.
- PDF printing needs the **Account Balances & Invoices** staff permission.
- Reports lack time stamps; the printed receipt shows date and time.
- "Partner Sales" section lists charity donations when **Allow clients to donate to a charity** is on.
- App credit card receipts show the client's signature.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: Return/Void leads to refunds and voids.
