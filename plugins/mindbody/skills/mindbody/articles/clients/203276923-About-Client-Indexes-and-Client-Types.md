# Client indexes and client types FAQ

Explains how client indexes and client types differ and how to find, bulk-assign and auto-assign them. Use it when segmenting clients for lists, reports or marketing.

## Where in Mindbody
- Client Directory: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_clt_lkup.asp (Clients)
- Mailing Lists: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_rpt_mailer.asp?category=Clients (Insights > Reports > Clients > Mailing Lists)
- Classes: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/servicesandpricing/Classes

## Steps
### List clients with no client index value
1. Clients > expand **Filters** > **Client Indexes** dropdown = **Not assigned** > **Search**.
2. Click the yellow **Tag new** icon, confirm **OK**.
3. Go to Mailing Lists, choose **Mailing List**, tick **Tagged clients only**, **Generate**, then export (Excel icon) or print.

### Find clients by client type
1. Clients > Filters > select one or more in the **Client type** multi-select (Ctrl/Cmd-click) > **Search**.

### Auto-assign a client type from a class
1. Services & Products > Classes > open the class > scroll to **Additional options**.
2. Choose type(s) in **Auto-assigned client types**, **Save**.

## Settings & fields
- **Client types**: multiple per client; survey/category style (e.g. Yoga, Pilates, Spinning).
- **Client indexes**: exactly one value per client per index (e.g. one age range).
- **Required in Business Mode** checkbox on an index forces staff to pick a value.
- Bulk-assign indexes or types with the Modify Tagged Clients tool (tag clients first, for instance from Schedule at a Glance or Attendance Analysis results).

## Gotchas
- Auto-assign triggers when the client signs in to the class; works for classes only, not appointments.
- Clients registered before the setting was added do not get the type; early cancel and re-enroll them.
