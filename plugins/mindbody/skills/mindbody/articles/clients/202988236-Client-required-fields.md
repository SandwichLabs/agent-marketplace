# How to set up client required fields

Controls which client details must be filled in when a client account is created or edited, separately for staff-side (business) and client-side (consumer) entry. Use it when a studio wants to force collection of phone, address, etc.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/required-fields
- Menu path: **Settings** (left nav) > **Clients** > **Required Fields** (or search "Required Fields" in the top search bar)

## Steps
1. Open the Required Fields screen via the link or menu path.
2. Tick the checkbox for each field in the "Required in Consumer Mode" column and/or the "Required in Business Mode" column.
3. No save button: changes save automatically and a confirmation appears at lower left.

## Settings & fields
- Baseline: business site always requires first and last name; consumer site additionally requires email (locked, cannot be unchecked).
- Required fields show an asterisk (*) on client profile create/edit forms.
- If **Use Client Measurements** is on in General Setup & Options (also enabled by costume tracking), measurement fields (height, inseam, shoe size, etc.) also appear here.
- To require something not listed, create a client index and mark it required on both business and consumer sites (not available in the branded app).

## Gotchas
- Staff need the **Required fields** permission (Clients section of permissions).
- Changes can take up to an hour to apply.
- Consumer and business requirements need not match.
- Marking any one of Address, City, Postal code, or State required in business mode makes the whole address required.
- Custom fields cannot be required.
- Applies to desktop software, Mindbody Business app, and branded web tools. Client measurements are not supported in the Business app.
- Mindbody consumer app/web do not enforce required fields, though they prompt new clients for a phone number on first booking (bypassable). The branded app only checks at booking/request time and offers "Not Now" to skip.
- Existing clients are not retroactively forced; use the client alert **Missing Required Fields** to catch them.
- Supported with Messenger[ai].
- Some features depend on the software package.
