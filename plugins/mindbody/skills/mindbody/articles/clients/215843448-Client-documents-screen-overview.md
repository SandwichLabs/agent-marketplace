# Client Documents screen

Where staff upload, view and manage files, client forms, waivers and contract agreements for a specific client. Needed to store paperwork on a profile or to control what the client sees online.

## Where in Mindbody
- Enable at: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp (Settings > General > General Setup and Options > Client Management)
- Use at: client profile > **Documents** on the client management submenu

## Steps
### Enable
1. Settings > General Setup and Options > expand **Client Management**.
2. Tick **Client Document Uploads**; optionally the consumer options below; click **Update**. The Documents screen then appears on all client accounts.

### Upload / manage
1. Find the client > **Documents**.
2. **Choose File**, pick the file, click **Upload** (one at a time).
3. Click a file name to view it; use Delete to remove it.
4. Per document, tick **Client View** (client may see it in consumer site) and **Client Remove** (client may delete it). Saved automatically.

### Client forms block on this screen
- **Send Client Forms** sends forms set as Staff only or Generate Client Form Outside Booking a Service.
- Columns: Name, Status (Submitted / Not yet submitted), Date submitted, view log icon (which staff opened it, when).
- Actions menu: **Resend** (same link; absent for Staff-only forms), **Open** (permitted staff complete it in person or for the client), **Copy Link**, **Delete** (permitted staff), **View/Delete** (submitted forms).

## Settings & fields
- **Allow Clients to View Their Documents in Consumer Mode**: shows a Documents tab (My Info > Documents).
- **Allow Clients to Upload Documents**: only works when the view option is on. Client-uploaded files carry a note icon with upload time.
- **Default Permissions for Client Documents in Consumer Mode**: **View** and **Delete** defaults for staff-uploaded files; overridable per document.
- Uploads: max 4 MB; allowed types bmp, doc, docx, gif, jpg, jpeg, mov, pdf, png, ppt, pptx, rtf, tif, tiff, txt, xls, xlsx, zip.

## Gotchas
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: deleted documents cannot be recovered.
- Only PDF and Word files offer a download option; images and text do not.
- Avoid spaces and slashes in file names.
- Clients cannot see completed client forms online; staff must download/print and send them.
- If a form is generated on booking and the client cancels, pending forms vanish; forms already completed stay.
- Waivers signed in the Business app (or via WaiverKing) and contract agreement terms may land here depending on where they were completed; no contract data in the Mindbody app or web.
- Staff can toggle Client View/Remove, but only the site owner's login can disable the feature.
