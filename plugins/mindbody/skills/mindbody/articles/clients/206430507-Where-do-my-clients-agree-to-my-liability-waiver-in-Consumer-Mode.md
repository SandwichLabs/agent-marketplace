# Where clients agree to the liability waiver in Consumer Mode

Explains how and where clients accept your liability waiver on the Mindbody consumer site, and how staff see that acceptance in business mode.

## Where in Mindbody
- Waiver terms are set up under business policies (see the customize business policies article).
- Staff view of acceptance: client profile > **Client Info** > Additional Information section.

## Steps
1. Set up the waiver terms under your business policies.
2. A new client creating an account on the desktop consumer site must tick **I agree with the above terms** before the account is created.
3. For existing clients, or after waiver status has been reset, turn on the Liability Waiver client alert.
4. Those clients see a prompt at login, tick the checkbox and click **Continue**.
5. In business mode, open the client's Client Info and check Additional Information for the acceptance line.

## Settings & fields
- Client Info shows "Liability waived by Client on [date]" once accepted.
- **Print liability waiver** prints only the current waiver terms, not a signed copy.

## Gotchas
- No document is uploaded to the client's profile when they agree; the date line is the only record.
- The checkbox flow described here applies to the desktop consumer site.
- Existing clients are not prompted unless the alert is enabled or their waiver status is reset.
