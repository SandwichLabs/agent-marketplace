# How to manage active and inactive client profiles

Clients cannot be deleted from Mindbody; they can be deactivated (hidden) and reactivated. Covers deactivating, reactivating, finding inactive profiles, and the automatic deactivation setting.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_clt_lkup.asp (Client Directory); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_rpt_mailer.asp (Mailing Lists); https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp (General Setup & Options)
- Menu path: **Clients**; **Settings > General > General Setup and Options > Client Management**

## Steps
### Deactivate
1. Search for the client and open **Client Info**.
2. Click **More Actions > Deactivate**.

### Reactivate (staff)
1. Find the inactive profile (below), open **Client Info**.
2. Click **More Actions > Activate**.
3. For many profiles at once, contact Mindbody Customer Support to request mass reactivation.

### Find inactive profiles
- Client Directory: under **Clients**, change the Active filter to **All clients** (or **Inactive clients** for only inactive), enter the name, click **Search**. One match opens directly; multiple matches show inactive names with strikethrough.
- Mailing Lists report: **Insights > Reports > Clients > Mailing Lists**, set **List clients** to **Currently inactive**, keep **Client's Opt-in status** at **All clients**, click **Generate**.
- Include inactive in all searches: **Settings > General > General Setup and Options > Client Management**, scroll to **Lookup Clients - Search All Clients**, tick **Enable**, click **Update**.

### Change or disable automatic deactivation
1. Open General Setup and Options and expand **Client Management**.
2. Scroll to **Set up Automatic Deactivation of Clients** and click the link.
3. In the When Should We Deactivate Clients? window, pick a **Studio**.
4. Choose **Deactivate after** (weeks or months) or **Do not automatically deactivate clients**.
5. Click **Save**, then **Update**.

## Settings & fields
- Default auto-deactivation: 24 months after last visit; maximum 48 months; configurable per location.
- Runs nightly, works retroactively; mass deactivation can take up to 24 hours after a change.
- Does not apply to clients with no visit history (including imported history). Purchases (even for others) count as activity.
- Profiles stay inactive until staff or client reactivates. Client Info shows "Deactivated on: [date] [time]".

## Gotchas
- Needs: Business Information screen and General Setup & Options screen (Settings); View client info and Edit client info (Clients); Marketing Reports; Ability to view all clients. Availability depends on software package.
- Inactive profiles are hidden from searches and reports unless included as above.
- Selling a pricing option or product to an inactive client does not reactivate them.
- Terminate the client's contracts before deactivating; active contracts keep charging.
- Opt the client out of marketing emails and texts first, otherwise they still receive messages.
- Clients reactivate themselves by logging in with a Mindbody account (Consumer Identity sites), or by using the Mindbody or branded app (booking or buying). Branded web tools show a connection error for deactivated profiles.
- If a deactivated client's last visit and deactivation date are over a year apart, auto-deactivation was probably the cause.
- EU businesses: clients can request data deletion via the Data Options screen.
