# Family accounts FAQ

Family accounts let one primary Mindbody account holder book, buy and manage profiles for dependents. This page covers what staff can and cannot do, client-side rules, and platform limits. Needed when a parent asks about adding kids, duplicate profiles, or linking accounts.

## Where in Mindbody
- Identify a family account: client profile > **Client Info** > **Manage Family Account** (Mindbody account section). Lists the primary and dependent users; clicking a linked name switches profile.
- Optional setting: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_tlbx_ss_setup.asp (Settings > General > General Setup and Options > Client Management > **Family Sign Up Experience**)
- Client-side portal: account.mindbodyonline.com

## Steps
### Show the "Add Family" button in the Mindbody app sign-up
1. General Setup and Options > Client Management > tick **Family Sign Up Experience** > Update. Not required for family accounts to work (the feature is rolling out to all businesses); leave off if most clients sign up individually.

### Staff adding a child
1. Create the child's profile and enter the parent (primary user) email address.
2. A parent with no Mindbody account gets a welcome email to set a password and become primary; a parent with an account gets a confirmation email to add the business.

### Staff helping a client
- From Client Info, staff can verify the Mindbody account name/email, see family members, send a Reset Password email to the primary holder, or send an update-client-info email.

## Settings & fields
- **Primary user** vs **dependents**: one primary only; a second parent must also be a dependent. Up to 50 family members.
- Primary can **Edit** name, **Move to their own account** (needs a valid email for that member), **Make account owner**, or **Delete family member** (disconnects all that person's business profiles; cannot be undone).
- Clients manage accounts at account.mindbodyonline.com, or via consumer site (Client Profile > Edit), branded web tools (Account Info > Edit account info), branded app (Client Profile > Edit > Edit). Changes sync to business profiles.
- Existing profiles without a Mindbody account: client signs in with the old login (needs a real email not already linked) and is walked through creating a Mindbody account, then can add family.

## Gotchas
- Required client fields apply to both primary and dependent profiles.
- Staff and owners cannot modify family relationships; some Client Info fields are grayed out. Client must make the changes.
- Sharing pricing options is not supported with family accounts; sharing and paying still require standard client relationships (separate feature).
- Booking the parent and a dependent into the same class: book the dependent first, or the dependent's Book button is disabled. Intro offers likewise: buy for the dependent first or the dependent pays full price.
- Primary agrees to the liability waiver for dependents.
- A parent who moved a dependent off and changed their mind can still act until the dependent confirms the email link; after that the parent loses booking/purchase access.
- New profile with a first/last name that matches no family member triggers an email asking the client to add the business at account.mindbodyonline.com (Notifications) and name the family member. Manual duplicate clients with the same name and email are blocked.
- Clients cannot see all dependents' passes and memberships at once. Mindbody app and web books classes only (not appointments); web app shows passes for primary only and needs payment info per dependent.
- Not integrated: Marketing Suite, Messenger[ai] (it asks which profile when phone numbers are shared), progress tracking. Lead Management is integrated. Livestream and on-demand video recognize family accounts (family picker on login).
- Branded app cannot add dependents during sign-up; use the Mindbody account portal.
- If not enabled, clients can still manage family in their Mindbody account but cannot switch between members on that business's consumer site, branded web tools or branded app. New additions may lag; refresh the app and update it.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: Delete family member, Make account owner, Move to own account.
