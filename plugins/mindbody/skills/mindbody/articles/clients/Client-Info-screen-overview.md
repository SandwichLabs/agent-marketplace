# Client Info screen overview

The Client Info screen is the hub for one client's contact data, billing card, waiver, status, indexes, types and more. It is a two-column layout: a left summary column and right-hand collapsible modules (14 default, up to 25 depending on enabled features). Needed for almost any client edit.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Find a client** box at top > click the client > **Client Info** on the client management submenu.
- The submenu also links Contact Logs, Schedule, Visits, Purchases, Account Details, Documents. The **More** dropdown sets Client Info as the default landing screen and offers merging duplicate client accounts.
- Not yet rolled out to all sites.

## Steps
### Reorder modules
1. More Actions > **Re-order Layout**; drag with the six-dot handles, toggle default collapse/expand, click **Save Changes**. Saved per staff member.

### Other left-column actions
- **Message** (envelope) emails from the client's own address on file, not the General Setup default.
- **Switch account owner** (family account plus Mindbody account only): choose New account owner.
- **Deactivate**: deactivates the profile.
- **Mindbody account** block: **Connect Mindbody Account**, or **Manage Account** / **Manage Family Account** with Email Reset Password Link, Update Email, Email Mindbody Account Link, Disconnect Mindbody Account Link (affects only the profile you are on).

## Settings & fields
Left column summary: Account, Liability waiver (mark complete or email), Mindbody account, Visits (last/next), Membership summary (status, last status change, current membership, sessions remaining, locker number, suspensions to date), Sessions Remaining.

Default modules:
- **Alerts**: Client Alert (yellow, general), Staff Alert (red, urgent, shown on Class Sign In), Covid-19 vaccine verified (needs setting). Staff-only.
- **Contact & Subscriptions**: Contact email; Mindbody account email (cannot edit directly; use Update link and the client confirms from their current email); Setup login/Password only without consumer identity; Mobile/home/work phone; Subscriptions (Account management, Reminders and schedule changes, News and promos); Class Waitlist SMS (default opted in).
- **Notes**: up to 40,000 characters, no timestamps, not visible to client, not in reports or Excel.
- **Billing Information**: card/bank details; card may sync from Mindbody app, web app or branded app.
- **Edit name**: Third-party payer, Prospect, Company, RSSID (Assign ID, up to 50 chars, or key tag), name fields, Nickname (searchable but not on sign-in sheets), Company Name; prospect fields (close probability, expected close date/amount, first contact date, prospect stage).
- **Membership status**: dropdown of Active, Non-Member, Expired, Suspended, Terminated, Declined; Status Log; **Auto Pays limit** (cap per autopay on card/ACH, remainder goes to client account); **Suspend client booking** with start/end dates (needs Scheduling Suspensions setting).
- **Address** (plus Studio/location for multi-location), **Photo** (PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, TIFF under 4MB, max 200px high; second photo needs a setting), **Referred by**, **Relationships**.
- **Additional information**: birthday, age, gender, gender preference, liability waiver incomplete/expired checkboxes (waive on client's behalf), **Email liability waiver to client**, **Print liability waiver** (prints the current text, not what was signed), Liability waived by, Opt-in for rewards. Hidden for company profiles.
- **History** (needs Sales Team Management), **Client Indexes**, **Client Types**.

Optional modules (need settings): Emergency contact (Children's Program Features), Formula notes (Appointment formula notes), Progress tracking, Measurements (Costume Management + Client Measurements), Insurance (CAM feature enabled by Mindbody), Martial arts belts, Registration information, ICD codes, Custom date fields, Other custom fields, Rep (Sales Team Management), Class Tests.

## Gotchas
- A profile with no ID assigned does not appear in searches, even in the Client Directory.
- First/last name are locked when the client has a Mindbody account; the client edits them at account.mindbodyonline.com.
- If consumer identity is on and the contact and Mindbody account emails mismatch, an alert icon shows and neither can be edited by hand.
- "News and promos" text checkbox grayed out means the client must opt in from their own login. Email subscription defaults on for US clients only.
- Email subscription ties to Constant Contact / NamasteLight emails.
- Custom field values do not show in search results, only the matching names; custom fields are not on the consumer site or business-site new-profile form.
- ID cards, prospects and scheduling suspensions each need a General Setup & Options (Client Management) setting.
- ⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first: Deactivate, Disconnect Mindbody Account Link, and changing Auto Pays limit.
