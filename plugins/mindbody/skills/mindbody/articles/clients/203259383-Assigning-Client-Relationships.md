# How to assign or remove client relationships

Client relationships link clients (parent and child, shared pricing options or memberships, one paying for another). Use this to add or remove those links from Client Info or while creating a new client.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/asp/adm/adm_clt_lkup.asp (Client Directory)
- Menu path: **Clients**, open a client, **Client Info > Relationships**

## Steps
### Assign to existing clients
1. Search for and open the client, then their **Client Info**.
2. Expand **Relationships**; existing ones are listed.
3. Click **+Assign**.
4. Find the other client in the **Client** search field.
5. Choose the relationship type from the dropdown (e.g. Shares pricing option).
6. Click **Add**. The mirror relationship is added to the other client automatically.

### Assign while adding a new client
1. Click **Clients > Add New Client** and fill in details.
2. In the Relationships section, pick the existing related client in **Client Search**.
3. Choose the relationship and click **Add Relationship**; repeat for more relationships or other clients.
4. Optionally click **Add Family Member** (options limited to "is paid for", "Shares series with", "Shares memberships with"; more can be assigned later).
5. Click **Add New Client** to save.

### Remove a relationship
1. Open the client's **Client Info** and expand **Relationships**.
2. Click **Remove** beside the relationship. The matching one on the other client disappears too.

## Gotchas
- Only relationship types that exist and are active on the Relationship Types screen can be assigned.
- Permissions: View client info, Edit client info, Set up relationship types (for custom types), and Ability to view all clients. Availability depends on software package.
- Changes are mirrored on both profiles, so removing affects both clients (e.g. a shared pricing option arrangement ends).
