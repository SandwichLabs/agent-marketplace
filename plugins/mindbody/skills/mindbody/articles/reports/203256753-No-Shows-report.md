# No-Shows report

Lists clients who missed visits or late cancelled, with contact details, so staff can follow up. Use it to find repeat no-show clients or to build a tagged list.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_rpt_noshows.asp?category=Clients
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports > Clients > No-Shows**

## Steps
1. Open the report, set filters, click **Generate**.
2. Optionally **Export to Excel**, **Tag add** / **Tag new**, **Save this report**, or **Print this report**.

## Settings & fields
- **Find**: **No Shows** (neither Signed In nor Late Cancel checked); **Late Cancels**; **No Shows OR Late Cancels**; **No Shows - NOT Late Cancelled**.
- **Start date** / **End date** (only visits inside the range), **At** (location), **Teacher** (only staff with matching visits appear), **Type of Service** (all, appointments, classes; courses count as classes).
- Quick dates: **Calendar Months**, **To-Date**, **Rolling Averages**.
- **Sort By**: client name or number of no-shows/late cancels.
- **Tagged clients only**: limit to your tag list.
- Result columns: Client Name (links to Client Info), ID, Date Range, No Shows, Late Cancels, combined total, Home/Work/Mobile phone, Email.

## Gotchas
- Needs the **Analysis Reports** permission (Reports section); report availability depends on software package.
- A no-show appointment only appears if the Apply Payment button was used to charge the late cancel; confirmed appointments never appear.
- Teacher dropdown omits staff with no matching visits in the range; regenerate after changing filters.
- Tag new replaces the tag list but deletes no client records.
