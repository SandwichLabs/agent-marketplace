# Export client email or mailing addresses

How to use the Mailing Lists report to pull client email addresses or postal addresses, either copied from the screen or exported to Excel.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_rpt_mailer.asp
- Menu path: **Insights** > **Reports**, search "Mailing Lists", or **Clients** > **Mailing Lists**

## Steps
1. Open the Mailing Lists report.
2. Top-left filter: choose **Mailing List** (postal addresses) or **Email List**.
3. Pick the client group in the **List Clients** menu.
4. In **Client's Opt-in Status**, choose all clients or only those meeting certain opt-in criteria (reminders, notifications, newsletters, promotions).
5. Optionally choose **Tagged Clients Only** (for lists tagged from other reports or rosters).
6. Choose **Clients Only**, **Prospects Only**, or **Clients and Prospects**.
7. Set **Sort By**: Client Name, Postal Code, or Client Barcode.
8. Click **Generate** to preview.
9. Click **Export to Excel**, or copy emails from the text box at the top and paste into an email.

## Gotchas
- The copy text box caps at 10,000 email addresses (for browser performance). **Export to Excel** has no limit and includes all addresses.
- Respect opt-in status when choosing recipients.
- Package-dependent features. Frequently used reports can be saved as favorites.
