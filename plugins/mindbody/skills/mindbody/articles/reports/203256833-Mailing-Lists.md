# Mailing Lists report

Builds client contact lists in three flavors (Email, Mailing, Sales) for marketing emails, postal labels, sales follow-up, anniversaries and clients missing emails. Use it whenever a list of clients and their contact details is needed.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_rpt_mailer.asp
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports**, search "Mailing Lists" or go to **Clients > Mailing Lists**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Pick the list type (Mailing, Email or Sales) in the first dropdown. Default is Email, listing active clients.
3. Set the other filters.
4. Click **Generate**.
5. Email List: copy the addresses shown in the box beside the filters and paste them into the BCC field of your email; the on-screen link opens your default mail program.
6. Mailing List: click **Create Label** for a printable address sheet.

## Settings & fields
- **Mailing List type**: Mailing (postal address, phone), Email (name, ID, email, phone), Sales (name, contact log link, emergency contact name, phone, email, contact log details, creation date, age; works with Contact Logs to track prospect conversion).
- **List clients** options: All clients (default, active and inactive), Anniversary date between (month/day of account creation), By Home Studio Location (multi-location), Currently active, Currently inactive, With birthdays between (ignores birth year), With client creation date between (year matters, unlike anniversary), With pending contract confirmation, With first visit dates between, With incomplete address, With logins, Without logins, With No Contact Logs, With selected type(s) (Ctrl/Cmd for multiple client types).
- **Client's Opt-in status**: All Clients, or Subscribed / Not subscribed to News and promos, Account management, or Reminders and schedule changes, each for emails or texts (a contact method must be on file).
- **Group by household**: Mailing List only; merges same last name plus address into one label.
- **Tagged Clients** checkbox; **All clients / only Prospects / closed clients only** (default All; useful for Sales List).
- **Sort by**: Client Name, Postal code, Client barcode, Client email.
- **Change the delimiter**: Email List only; Semicolon default, or Comma or Space.
- **Export to Excel**, **Tag Add**, **Tag New** (replaces tagged list, clients not deleted), **Untag Clients**, **Save this report**, **Print this report**.

## Gotchas
- Needs Marketing Reports and Ability to view all clients permissions; availability depends on software package.
- Opt-in defaults: US clients default to the news-and-promos Email box checked; other countries default to unchecked. Sending marketing to non-opted-in clients can violate anti-spam law, so filter by opt-in status.
- Email List with opt-in "All Clients" still lists clients lacking an email address.
- Birthday filter does not display birth years; the Retention Management report may export birthdays for some businesses.
- Blank Sales List columns mean the data is missing from Client Info.
- If the email link does nothing, the browser has no default email program set.
