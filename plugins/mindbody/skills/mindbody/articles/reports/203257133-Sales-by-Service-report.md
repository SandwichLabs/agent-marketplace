# Sales by Service report

Breaks down sales of services (pricing options) by revenue category, in summary or per-sale detail, on cash or accrual basis. Use it to see totals for a given pricing option or what each employee sold.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/Report/Sales/SalesByService
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports > Sales > Sales by Service**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Set start and end dates (type them or use **Quick Dates**).
3. Set filters.
4. Choose **Summary** or **Detail**.
5. Choose the Accounting Basis (cash or accrual).
6. Click **Go!**. Click column headings to sort.

## Settings & fields
- **Sale Location**: multi-select; "Online Store" covers consumer site, Mindbody app, branded app and branded web tools. Default All Locations.
- **Revenue Category**, **Secondary Revenue Category**, **Service Category** (only those with sales in range are listed).
- **Show Discontinued Pricing Options**.
- **Pricing Option**: stays empty until a service category is chosen.
- **Entered By**: sales by employee.
- **Autopays**: include or exclude.
- **Pricing Option Type**: count, time, membership, or all.
- Summary columns: Pricing Option, Total Amount (after discounts and tax), Percent of Revenue Category, Cash Equivalent, Non-cash Equivalent, Quantity.
- Detail columns: Client (link to profile), Category, Home Phone, Sale Date, Activation Date, Activation Offset Days, Expiration Date, Total Amount, Cash/Non-cash Equivalent, Quantity.
- Buttons: **Go!**, **Export to Excel**, **Export to PDF**, **Save this report**, **Tag Add**, **Tag New** (replaces tagged list), **Untag clients**.

## Gotchas
- Needs Sales/Sales by Category/Sales by Services (Reports) and Ability to view all clients (Sales Team Management); availability depends on software package.
- First run defaults to Accrual Basis; afterward it remembers your last choice.
- Filtering by employee only works if each employee has their own login and uses it for every sale.
- If **Go!** does nothing, no sales match the filters.
- Deactivated pricing options and categories still appear under "All" filters.
- Online $0 transactions are recorded as cash and show under every accounting basis; classes set to "Clients can attend this class for free" are excluded.
- If the activation date is set to first visit, the report shows the sale date until the option is first used.
- The Constant Contact button was retired June 1, 2020.
