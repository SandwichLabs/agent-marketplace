# Sales by Category report

Shows revenue grouped by product category and service revenue category, with optional comparison to the previous month or year. Use it to see which categories earn what and how that is trending.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct links: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/Report/Sales/SalesByCategory and https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/appshell/shuttle/Report/Sales/SalesByCategory
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports > Sales > Sales by Category**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Set dates (type them or use Quick Dates).
3. Optionally choose **Compare to Previous**: Month or Year.
4. Set filters.
5. Pick **Accrual** or **Cash** in the Account Basis menu.
6. Click **Go!**. Click column headers to sort.

## Settings & fields
- **Compare to Previous**: same dates one month or year earlier; adds a previous-period column and percent change = (current - previous) / previous.
- **Show Tagged Clients Only**, **Sale Location** (multi-select, includes Online Store; default All Locations).
- **Client Home Studio**: filters by client home location, also splits Online Store sales.
- **Payment Method**, **Entered By** (needs unique logins), **Sales Rep**, **Autopays** (Include, Exclude, Autopays Only).
- **Accounting Basis**: Accrual, Cash, or Accrual & Cash combined (under **More**; adds sales paid with non-cash-equivalent methods such as trade).
- **Subcategories (products)**, **Secondary Categories (services)**, **Show Discounting Detail** (not produced with Compare to Previous).
- Buttons: **Export to Excel**, **Export to PDF**, **Save this report**, **Tag Add**, **Tag New**, **Untag clients**.
- Result groups: Services, Products, Prepaid Gift cards, Deposits (cash basis only; assignable gift cards and account payments), Other (tips, shipping and handling, fees such as suspension, returned payment and auto renew). Empty groups are hidden.
- Columns: Category, Subcategory (products only), Second Category (services only), Subtotal, Discounts, Tax, Sales Total, cash/non-cash split, Percent of Revenue, Percent Change.

## Gotchas
- Revenue categories must be set up first. Needs Sales/Sales by Category/Sales by Services (Reports) and Ability to view all clients (Sales Team Management); availability depends on software package.
- First run defaults to accrual; later runs remember the last choice.
- Accrual dates gift cards at redemption; gift card sales are full revenue on purchase date under cash. Remaining credit on assignable cards/account payments shows in Deposits.
- Accrual Excel export has no product secondary category or service subcategory; cash-basis export adds a "Grouping" column (Services, Products, Prepaid gift cards, Deposits, Other).
- The Constant Contact button has been gone since June 1, 2020.
