# Schedule at a Glance report

Lists every appointment, class, and course booking (past, present, future) with each client's visit status, in one place. Use it for attendance checks, unpaid-visit reconciliation, booking-trend and who-booked-what questions.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/Report/Staff/ScheduleAtAGlance/Generate?reportID=undefined
- Menu path: **Insights** > **Reports** > **Staff** > **Schedule at a Glance** (or search by name)

## Steps
1. Open the report, set a date range and filters.
2. Optional: with Visit Milestones enabled, tick **Show visit milestones** (only for a single-day range with the "Date of service" date filter).
3. Click **Go!**.
4. Click the blue Description link to jump to Class Sign In (classes/courses) or the Appointment schedule; click a client name to open their Info screen.

## Settings & fields
- **Start date / End Date**; **Date** dropdown: "Date of service" or "Date created" (sorts by booking date, good for booking trends).
- **Show Tagged Clients Only**, **Visit Location** (required choice for multi-location), **Staff**, **Service Category**, **Status**, **Scheduling Method** (staff-booked vs online).
- Optional columns, each gated by a permission: **Account Balances** and **Sessions Remaining** ("View client account/purchase history"), **Phone Numbers** ("View client info"), **Scheduled by** and **Rooms** ("View client schedule").
- Actions: **Print This Report**, **Export to Excel**, **Export to PDF** (can fix print formatting), **Save This Report**, tagging icons (**Tag Add**, **Tag New**, **Untag clients**), **Day** and **Week** arrow buttons shifting the range by 1 or 7 days. The Constant Contact option was discontinued June 2020.
- Result columns: Day, Date, Time, Description, Staff (hidden when filtering one staff member), Room, Location (hidden for single-location), Client (membership, first-visit, unpaid icons), Notes, Account balance, Remaining (click header to sort negatives to top = unpaid), Phone #, Icons, Status, Scheduled by. Weekly visit totals appear on the last row of each week.
- Appointment statuses: **Arrived** (Arrived? ticked, until checkout), **Booked** (unconfirmed future), **Completed** (checked out and paid), **Confirmed** (future, staff ticked Confirmed?), **Late Cancel** (cancelled inside the window; charged), **No-Show** (past appointment never checked out, cancelled, arrived, or confirmed; not yet charged).
- Class/course statuses: **Reserved**, **Signed-In**, **Absent** (never signed in), **Late Cancel**, **Made-Up** (absence later made up).

## Gotchas
- Needs **Schedule at a Glance/Attendance** permission: Everyone's schedule, or Own schedule ONLY.
- Checkbox/radio filter choices are sticky per login.
- No results after **Go!** means no appointments/reservations in that range.
- Appointments with identical start time and length are grouped; different ones are separate entries.
- Deactivated staff still appear in the Staff dropdown unless "Teacher (for classes)" and "Instructor (for appointments)" are unchecked on their profile.
- Milestones count completed visits only (not no-shows or cancellations); in Excel the milestone column becomes "First Visit" for multi-day ranges or other date filters.
- Some features depend on software package.
