# Retention Management report

Exports membership, contact and check-in data for clients with memberships, intended to be emailed to the Retention Management - TRP service for attrition analysis. Use it when the studio works with TRP.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/ASP/adm/adm_rpt_retention_mgmnt.asp?category=Clients
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports > Clients > Retention Management**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Tick **Active Clients only** if wanted.
3. Choose **Sort by**: **Client Name** or **Membership Type**.
4. Click **Generate**.
5. Click the **Export to Excel** icon and save the file.
6. Email the file to the address TRP provided.

## Settings & fields
- **Active Clients only**, **Sort by**, **Generate**, **Export to Excel** (same layout as on-screen), **Print this report**.
- Result columns: Name, Birthday, Gender, Member Since, Home phone, Email, Status (active/inactive), Freeze, Membership Type, Membership Expiration, Last (most recent check-in), YTD, 30, 90 and 365 (check-in counts for year to date and the past 30/90/365 days).

## Gotchas
- At least one membership must be set up first; the report only includes clients with memberships.
- Needs both the Marketing Report and Retention Management permissions (Reports section); availability depends on software package.
- Sending the export transmits client personal data (birthday, email, phone) outside Mindbody; confirm with the user first.
