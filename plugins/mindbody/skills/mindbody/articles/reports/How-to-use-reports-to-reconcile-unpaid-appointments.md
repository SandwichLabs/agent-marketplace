# How to use reports to reconcile unpaid appointments

Two reports (Unpaid Visits, Schedule at a Glance) can surface appointments that were never paid for, and from there you start a sale or checkout to clear them. Use when a client has appointments marked unpaid.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/mainappointments/index (Appointment Schedule)
- Menu path: **Appointments** in the left navigation; reports under the Reports area (Unpaid Visits report, Schedule at a Glance report).

## Steps
### Option A: Unpaid Visits report (good for many unpaid appointments)
1. Run the Unpaid Visits report; note each unpaid appointment type and date.
2. Click the client's name to open Point of Sale.
3. Under Add Item, click **Services** and find the pricing option (search, or filter by service category).
4. Set **Activates** to the unpaid appointment's date. To cover several appointments with one multi-session option, use the earliest date and lengthen **Duration** to span all dates.
5. Optionally edit session count or price, then click **Add Item**.
6. Repeat for each appointment, pick the **payment method** (e.g. Credit (Visa/MC), Cash), and finish the sale.
7. Go to the Appointment Schedule, click the unpaid appointment, and choose **Apply payment** from the action menu.
8. Click the appointment again and click **Retail** to finish. (If using Checkout instead, add the "PAID" item to the cart and complete checkout.)
9. Repeat for each paid appointment.

### Option B: Schedule at a Glance report (good for one appointment)
1. Run the report and click the service Description to jump to the Appointment Schedule for that date.
2. Click the unpaid appointment and choose **Checkout**.
3. Select the pricing option; the activation date automatically matches the appointment.
4. Click **Add To Cart**, then **Checkout**.
5. Choose the **Payment Method** and complete the sale.

## Gotchas
- If the client already owns a usable pricing option with sessions left, reconcile with that instead (see Appointment Checkout article).
- Staff permissions needed: Sales Team Management > Ability to view all clients; Reports > Account Balances & Invoices plus Sales/Sales by Category/Sales by Service (for Unpaid Visits); Reports > Schedule at a Glance/Attendance (Everyone's or Own schedule ONLY); Appointments > View entire appointment schedule, View appointment details, Modify appointments, Apply payment, Check out appointments.
- Some features depend on the software package.
- Completing a sale takes payment; confirm payment method with the user.
