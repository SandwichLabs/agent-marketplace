# Unpaid Visits report

Lists clients with classes, courses (enrollments) or appointments that have no payment attached, so staff can collect or reconcile. Use it for chasing owed money or building a tagged list of clients to contact.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/Unpaidvisitsreport?category=Clients
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports**, search "Unpaid Visits" or go to **Clients > Unpaid Visits**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Choose filters (dates, service category, view, etc.).
3. Click **Go!**.
4. Click any column heading to sort the results.
5. Optionally tag the listed clients, export, or save the report.

## Settings & fields
- **Start date** / **End date** / **Quick Dates**.
- **Select all dates**: past, present and future.
- **Show tagged clients only**.
- **Include future unpaids**: adds unpaid visits scheduled in the future within the range.
- **Show consolidated records**: one line per client per service category with a count; the columns become Date Of Last Visit and Status Of Last Visit.
- **Location** (visit location only shows in the Excel export), **Service category**.
- **Unpaid visits between**: only clients whose unpaid count in the chosen category falls in your range.
- **View**: Summary (client, visit type, unpaid count, last visit status/date) or Detail (adds payment reference number and contact info).
- **Go!**, **Print this report**, **Export to Excel**, **Export to PDF**, **Save this report**.
- **Tag add**: add listed clients to the existing tagged list. **Tag new**: replaces the tagged list (clients are not deleted). **Untag clients**: removes listed clients from the tagged list.
- Result columns: Client ID, Client (link opens the Point of Sale screen), Visit Type, Unpaid Visits, Date of Visit, Status, Payment Reference Number (appointments show "n/a"), Home phone, Mobile phone, Email Address.

## Gotchas
- Staff need these permissions: Account Balances & Invoices (Reports), Sales/Sales by Category/Sales by Services (Reports), and Ability to view all clients (Sales Team Management).
- A "Previous version" link at the top opens the older fixed-date version, which can look back up to two years.
- An unpaid appointment is one on the schedule not yet tied to a pricing option; requested and waitlisted bookings can count.
- **Tag new** discards the previous tagged list.
- The Constant Contact button was retired on June 1, 2020.
- Availability depends on software package.
