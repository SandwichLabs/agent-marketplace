# Last Visit report

Shows each client's most recent visit within a date range, with visit count, service, pricing option, booking method and contact info. Use it to find high-frequency clients, lapsed clients, intro-offer follow-up, or to see how clients book.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/LastVisitReport?category=Clients
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports**, search "Last Visit" or go to **Clients > Last Visit**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Enter dates in **Sessions between** or click a **Quick Dates** option.
3. Adjust filters and options.
4. Click **Go!**. Results are detail-only; click a client name to open their account.

### Find clients with zero visits (Scheduled report version)
1. Click **Scheduled report** at the top.
2. Enter 0 in **List clients who had** and change the dropdown to **or Fewer**.
3. Set a start date in **Last visit on or before**.
4. Tick **Hide clients with future visits**.
5. Click **Request Report**.
6. Under Requested Reports, click the **Excel download** icon to get the .xlsx.
7. Use **Return to previous report** to go back to the default version.

## Settings & fields
- **Sessions between** / **Quick Dates**.
- **Show tagged clients only**, **Include inactive clients**, **Hide clients with future visits**.
- **Only show clients with visits via intro offers**.
- **Visit location**, **Staff member**, **Service category** (All = actual last visit overall; a specific category = last visit in that category), **Pricing options**, **Visit type**.
- **Booking method**: Mindbody app, consumer mode, business mode; everything else (API, Fitmetrix, branded app) is "Other".
- **Referral type**.
- **List clients who had**: a visit-count threshold with more/fewer.
- **Last visit on or before**: latest date analyzed; use today for "up to now".
- **Print This Report**, **Export to Excel** (adds a Client ID column), **Export to PDF**, **Save This Report**, **Tag Add** (append to tagged list), **Tag New** (replaces tagged list), **Untag clients**.
- Result columns: Last Visit, # Visits (excludes future visits), Client, Visit Location, Service Category, Visit Type, Pricing Option, Expiration Date, Booking Method, Referral Type (Unassigned if none), Staff, Rep (needs the Sales Rep feature), Phone, Email.

## Gotchas
- Needs the Marketing Reports staff permission; availability depends on software package.
- Scheduled appointments, late cancels, no-shows and class absents are not counted.
- A client with several visits on the same last-visit day appears multiple times.
- Zero-visit searches only work through the Scheduled report version, which ignores the Sessions between range.
- Cross-regional sites: by default shows last visit at the running site; a "more info" link reveals the true last visit across regions.
- **Tag New** discards the previous tagged list. The Constant Contact button was retired June 1, 2020.
