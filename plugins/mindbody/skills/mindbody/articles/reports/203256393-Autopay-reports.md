# Autopay reports

Overview of the four autopay reports and their default time windows. Use it to choose which report answers an autopay question, then follow to the detailed article.

## Where in Mindbody
- Menu path: **Insights** > **Reports** (Payment Processing / autopay reports); individual URLs are in the linked detail articles.

## Settings & fields
- **Autopay Summary**: per-day totals and status (scheduled, processed, declined) across the previous two and next two months by default.
- **Autopay Detail**: today's scheduled autopays by default; lists date, client, location, item, card info, amount, status; combined autopay sales appear as one sale with line items. Daily scheduled autopays are also run through this report.
- **Autopay Expirations**: autopay schedules that ended in the last two months or end in the next two.
- **Autopay CC Expirations**: autopays on cards that expired in the last month or expire in the next month.

## Gotchas
- Staff need the **Autopay Reports** permission (Reports section) to manage autopays from these screens.
- Declined autopays are automatically resubmitted for up to seven calendar days after the scheduled date by default; the number is changeable on General Setup and Options, and they can be resubmitted manually.
- Items showing as Submitted (declined but not successful) should be sent to Merchant Support to rule out a processing error.
- Report availability depends on software package.
