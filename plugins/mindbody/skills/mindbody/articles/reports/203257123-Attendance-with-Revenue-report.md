# Attendance with Revenue report

Shows gross revenue per attended visit (class or appointment), the pricing option that paid for it, and visits remaining. Use it to analyze limited-visit packs, find comps, or debug a staff payroll shortfall.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/AttendanceReport
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports**, search "Attendance with Revenue" or go to **Sales > Attendance with Revenue**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Pick a date range in **Dates**.
3. Set filters and a **View**.
4. Click **Go!**. Afterward, print, export or tag clients from the options under the Generate area.

## Settings & fields
- **Dates**, **Show tagged clients only**.
- **Exclude unlimited sessions**: drops unlimited-type pricing options (single and multi-visit ones stay).
- **Exclude no-shows and late cancels**.
- **Visit location** (defaults to main location) and **Sale location** (defaults to All locations).
- **Staff member** (only staff who worked in the range are listed), **Start Time**, **Day of week** (all checked by default).
- **Visit service category**, **Payment service category** (category of the payment used), **Payment method**.
- **Cross-regional visit**: Yes/No column.
- **View**: default is by service category; others are summary, staff member, date, visit type, client, no-shows/late cancels, roll sheet.
- Standard buttons: **Go!**, **Print This Report**, **Export to Excel**, **Export to PDF**, **Save This Report**, **Tag Add**, **Tag New** (replaces tagged list), **Untag Clients**.
- Key columns: Client, Day/Date/Time, Visit Service Category, Visit Type, Pricing Option, Exp. Date, Visits Rem., Visit Location, Staff, Staff paid, Late Cancel, No-show, Booking Method, Payment Method, Rev per Visit; totals for Paid, Comp, Late Cancel, No-Shows, online booking and revenue.
- Summary view adds Type, Paid Clients, Comp Clients, Online Bookings, Members, Members Revenue, Total.
- Roll Sheet view adds Class Name, Room, Assistant, Enrolled and Head Count (manual entry spaces), Paid, Comps.

## Gotchas
- Needs: Schedule at a Glance/Attendance (everyone's, or own schedule only), Attendance with Revenue, Analysis reports (Reports section), plus Ability to view all clients (Sales Team Management). Availability depends on software package.
- Revenue per visit is price divided by visits; example: a $45 5-visit card gives $9 per visit.
- Poor fit for unlimited pricing options: per-visit revenue shifts as more visits are used.
- Taxes are excluded.
- "Comp" here means staff was not paid for the visit (pricing option set not to pay staff), not a free Point of Sale sale. Useful to check why payroll looks low.
- If **Go!** does nothing, there was no registration activity in the range.
- The Constant Contact button was retired June 1, 2020.
