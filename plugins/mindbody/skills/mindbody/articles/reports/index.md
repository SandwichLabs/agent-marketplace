# Reports

Sales, attendance, payroll, autopay, retention, mailing lists, unpaid visits, exporting.

24 pages. Each links its Mindbody support source.

- [Attendance with Revenue report](203257123-Attendance-with-Revenue-report.md) — Shows gross revenue per attended visit (class or appointment), the pricing option that paid for it, and visits remaining. Use it to analyze limited-visit packs, find comps, or debug a staff payroll shortfall.
- [Autopay Detail report](203256413-Autopay-Detail-report.md) — Per-transaction list of scheduled and processed autopays (default: today's), plus the place to manually run or delete scheduled autopays. Use for expected income snapshots and autopay status checks.
- [Autopay reports](203256393-Autopay-reports.md) — Overview of the four autopay reports and their default time windows. Use it to choose which report answers an autopay question, then follow to the detailed article.
- [Autopay Summary report](203256403-Autopay-Summary-report.md) — Day-by-day totals of scheduled and processed autopays with projected value, used for budget forecasting and reviewing autopay revenue. Default window is the last two months plus the next two.
- [Earned Revenue report](203257223-Earned-Revenue-report.md) — Shows earned versus deferred revenue from pricing options sold, for limited, unlimited and membership options. Use it for deferred-liability and accrual-style revenue analysis; like the Outstanding Series report but not limited to limited-visit options.
- [Export client email or mailing addresses](205027717-Export-client-email-or-mailing-addresses.md) — How to use the Mailing Lists report to pull client email addresses or postal addresses, either copied from the screen or exported to Excel.
- [How to create a mailing list of all active clients](How-to-create-a-mailing-list-of-all-active-clients.md) — Two ways to produce a list of clients with an active profile: the Mailing Lists report (for a mailing list) or the Client Directory (for a plain list or tagging). The article only gives the filter values and defers the steps to other articles.
- [How to create and export a list of clients using the Client Directory](203259313-All-client-search.md) — Shows how to list all (or tagged) clients in the Client Directory and get that list into Excel. Use it for quick client lists under 1,000 rows.
- [How to export reports to Microsoft Excel](203256023-Exporting-reports-to-Excel.md) — Nearly all Mindbody reports can be downloaded as Excel files. Use this when a user wants report data in a spreadsheet.
- [How to use reports to reconcile unpaid appointments](How-to-use-reports-to-reconcile-unpaid-appointments.md) — Two reports (Unpaid Visits, Schedule at a Glance) can surface appointments that were never paid for, and from there you start a sale or checkout to clear them. Use when a client has appointments marked unpaid.
- [Last Visit report](203256823-Last-Visit-report.md) — Shows each client's most recent visit within a date range, with visit count, service, pricing option, booking method and contact info. Use it to find high-frequency clients, lapsed clients, intro-offer follow-up, or to see how clients book.
- [Mailing Lists report](203256833-Mailing-Lists.md) — Builds client contact lists in three flavors (Email, Mailing, Sales) for marketing emails, postal labels, sales follow-up, anniversaries and clients missing emails. Use it whenever a list of clients and their contact details is needed.
- [Most frequently used Mindbody reports](203814246-Top-Reports-requested-by-MINDBODY-clients.md) — Digest of eight popular reports: what each is for, where it lives, and its filters. Use to pick the right report or find its URL.
- [No-Shows report](203256753-No-Shows-report.md) — Lists clients who missed visits or late cancelled, with contact details, so staff can follow up. Use it to find repeat no-show clients or to build a tagged list.
- [Payroll report](203256603-Payroll-report.md) — Shows what each staff member earned from classes, courses, appointments, hourly pay, tips and commissions, minus assistant and professional-product deductions. Use it when preparing staff pay or auditing a paycheck.
- [Reports screen overview](203256053-Reports-explained.md) — Directory of Mindbody reports grouped by tab, with what each answers and the staff permission gating it. Use it to pick the right report for a question.
- [Retention Management report](203256853-Retention-Management-report.md) — Exports membership, contact and check-in data for clients with memberships, intended to be emailed to the Retention Management - TRP service for attrition analysis. Use it when the studio works with TRP.
- [Retention report](203256863-Retention-report.md) — Counts clients who had an initial visit (class, course or appointment) in a date window and then came back within N days, split into new, repeat and total. Use it to judge new-client retention or the payoff of a promotion.
- [Sales by Category report](203257233-Sales-by-Category-report.md) — Shows revenue grouped by product category and service revenue category, with optional comparison to the previous month or year. Use it to see which categories earn what and how that is trending.
- [Sales by Product report](203256993-Sales-by-Product-report.md) — Lists retail product sales for a date range with quantities, totals and a cash vs non-cash split, in summary or per-sale detail. Use it to see what sold, from which supplier or register, and who bought it.
- [Sales by Service report](203257133-Sales-by-Service-report.md) — Breaks down sales of services (pricing options) by revenue category, in summary or per-sale detail, on cash or accrual basis. Use it to see totals for a given pricing option or what each employee sold.
- [Sales report](203257193-Sales-Report.md) — Lists every sale by employee, service category, location and payment method, in summary (daily totals) or detail (each transaction). Use it to see what was sold, who rang it, and how it was paid.
- [Schedule at a Glance report](203256543-Schedule-at-a-Glance-report.md) — Lists every appointment, class, and course booking (past, present, future) with each client's visit status, in one place. Use it for attendance checks, unpaid-visit reconciliation, booking-trend and who-booked-what questions.
- [Unpaid Visits report](203256703-Unpaid-Visits-report.md) — Lists clients with classes, courses (enrollments) or appointments that have no payment attached, so staff can collect or reconcile. Use it for chasing owed money or building a tagged list of clients to contact.
