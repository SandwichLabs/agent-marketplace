# Sales by Product report

Lists retail product sales for a date range with quantities, totals and a cash vs non-cash split, in summary or per-sale detail. Use it to see what sold, from which supplier or register, and who bought it.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/Report/Sales/SalesByItem
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports > Sales** (or **Inventory**) **> Sales by Product**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Set dates (type, calendar, or **Quick Dates**) and other filters.
3. Click **Go!**. Click column headings to sort.

## Settings & fields
- **Start date and End date**, **Quick Dates**, **Show tagged clients only**.
- **Sale location**: multi-select, includes the online store (consumer mode, apps, branded web tools); default All locations.
- **Payment Method**, **Supplier**, **Product** (only products sold in the range are listed; default All products), **Color**, **Size**, **Tax** (All, Non-Tax, Tax).
- **Cash register**: only shown when multiple cash registers are set up.
- **View**: Summary (grouped by product revenue category: name, color, size, barcode, quantity, total, cash equivalent, non-cash equivalent, plus category and grand totals) or Detail (per sale: client, category, sale date, unit price before tax, quantity, total, cash and non-cash amounts).
- **Show credits and gift cards**: adds gift card and account credit purchases.
- **Show subcategories**: adds product revenue subcategory column.
- **Go!**, **Print this report**, **Export to Excel**, **Export to PDF**, **Save this report**, **Tag add**, **Tag new** (replaces tagged list), **Untag clients**.
- Total = after discounts and tax. Cash equivalent = cash, check, credit and similar; non-cash equivalent = account, trade, other.

## Gotchas
- Needs the Sales by Supplier/Sales by Product permission; availability depends on software package.
- Accrual basis only.
- Multiple cash drawers require the Cash Drawers (Multiple) setting.
- If **Go!** seems to do nothing, no product sales exist in that range.
- Returns appear in parentheses even if the item was not restocked; if one is missing, check the date it was processed.
- To see only Contract Deposits: tick **Show credits and gift cards**, set the dates, then pick **Contract Deposit** in the Product dropdown.
- Sales by Category gives gross totals of product revenue categories.
- The Constant Contact button was retired June 1, 2020.
