# Reports screen overview

Directory of Mindbody reports grouped by tab, with what each answers and the staff permission gating it. Use it to pick the right report for a question.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/reportslandingpage/FavoriteReports
- Menu path: **Insights** > **Reports**. Landing page is **Favorite reports**; tabs on the left by category (each with filters, e.g. Clients tab > **Client Accounts**); report search box top right; **Save This Report** stores your filter choices under the **Saved** column (different from favoriting, which does not store filters).

## Gotchas
- Report availability depends on software package.
- Very large historical ranges can throw "So sorry, something's not working right"; run smaller date segments.
- Payment Processing reports need a merchant account.
- Permission shorthand used below (all under staff permissions, Reports section unless noted): **Sales** = Sales/Sales by Category/Sales by Service + Ability to view all clients (Sales Team Mgmt); **Analysis** = Analysis reports + Schedule at a Glance/Attendance (Everyone's Schedule); **Marketing** = Marketing reports; **Merchant** = View Merchant Account Transactions; **Autopay** = View autopay schedule and history; **Payroll** = Payroll Report/Payroll Export/Tips/Assistant/Commission Reports (All Staff, or own only); **Inventory** = Inventory reports; **Membership** = Membership reports.
- Reports needing set-up features: Sales by Rep (Sales Team Management), Contact Logs (Sales Team Management), Class Tests ("Use Class Testing" in Class & Course Options), Locker, Martial Arts Belt Promotion, Rewards, Promotions, Assistants, Tips/Commission (features enabled).

## Reports by tab
### Sales
- **Sales**, **Sales by Service**, **Sales by Category**, **Best Sellers** (with margins), **Sales Tax**, **Voided Sales** (shows who processed/authorized), **Returns**, **Contract Sales**, **Promotions**, **Rewards**, **Prospects**, **Sales Forecast** (Sales Team Management perm): cash or accrual basis for the sales ones. Permission: Sales.
- **Daily Closeout**: compare software totals with money collected, day or range (perm: Daily Closeout).
- **Cash Drawer**: end-of-shift balance by payment method, cash equivalents only, multiple drawers (perm: Cash Drawer today or date range).
- **Attendance with Revenue**: revenue per visit and visits remaining (Analysis + view all clients).
- **Sales by Rep** (Sales); **Mindbody Network Fees** (Mindbody Promote Fees perm); **Invoice** (Account Balances & Invoices + Sales); **Gift Cards** and **Gift Card Analysis** (Gift Card perm); **Sales by Supplier**, **Sales by Product** (Sales by Supplier/Product perm); **Manage Online Orders** (Pending/Shipped/Back Ordered; Online Store Reports perm).
- **Earned Revenue** (deferred revenue, all visit types) and **Outstanding Series** (presold pricing options; limited visits only) (Analysis); **Revenue by Class** needs a data set closed out first via **Average Revenue Analysis**, which can only be run once per data set, even after export (Analysis).
### Payment Processing (merchant account required)
- **Transactions** (processed/declined with codes; use for reconciliation with Payouts or auto-capture), **Approved Transactions** (approved, unbatched; not used with auto-capture), **Settled Transactions** (approved and batched, ACH included), **Pending Transactions** (ACH in progress), **Voided/Rejected Transactions** (not instant POS declines or failed autopays; use client Autopay History for those). Perm: Merchant (+ Settle credit card transactions for some).
- **Autopay Detail** (today's scheduled autopays with status; not a sales report), **Autopay Summary** (two months back and forward), **Autopay Expirations**, **Autopay Credit Card Expirations**. Month-to-month queued payments are excluded; perm: Autopay.
- **Card Updater**: cards auto-updated by the service, opt in/out (needs "Allow editing of card updater subscription settings").
### Virtual Wellness
- **Live Stream Classes** (Marketing perm); **Viewership** (add-on; no permission needed).
### Clients
- **Schedule at a Glance**: everything scheduled in a date range, filter by location, teacher, service category, status, method (perm: Schedule at a Glance/Attendance, everyone's or own).
- **Mailing Lists** (Marketing; Sales List filter adds contact logs), **Account Balances** (balances in 30-day buckets, generate statements), **Unpaid Visits** (links to reconcile), **Big Spenders** (top 10/100), **Membership**, **New Members** (Membership perm), **Ratings and Reviews** (Mindbody app feedback).
- **Contact Logs**, **Entry Logs** (all staff/client logins), **Cancellations** (can restore bookings; shows who cancelled), **First Visit**, **Last Visit**, **No Return**, **Retention**, **Referral Types**, **Referrers**, **Pricing Option Expirations**, **Visits Remaining** (limited only), **Retention Management** (for Fitronics; members only) (Marketing).
- **Attendance Analysis** (arrivals only counted when filtering one service category), **Attendance Without Revenue** (check against Payroll), **Clients Per Teacher**, **No-Shows**, **Client Arrivals**, **Online Metrics**, **Martial Arts Belt Promotion**, **Client Health Check** (six-month history vs last 30 days) (Analysis).
- **Client Indexes**, **Event Payments** (course payment plans; Event Invoices perm), **Locker**, **Class Tests**.
### Staff
- **Payroll**, **Tips**, **Commission** (POS-sold items only; percentage-of-revenue pay is on Payroll only), **Assistants** (only place assistants-only pay appears), **Payroll Export Setup** (ADP, Exact, Paychex).
- **Schedule at a Glance**, **Staff Schedule**, **Time Clock** (View/Edit clock-ins), **Cancellations**, **Phone Book** (Staff Phone Book), **Staff Performance**, **Appointment Metrics**, **Retail Sales Performance** (Staff Performance perm), **Pay Rates** (Staff Pay Rates), **Staff Activity** (who opened sensitive reports, IP), **Tasks**, **Trainer Conversions**, **Ratings and Reviews**, **Clients Per Teacher**.
### Inventory
- **Inventory on Hand** (adjust counts), **Inventory Change Log**, **Inventory Age** (Inventory perm); **Cost of Goods Sold**, **Sales by Product**, **Sales by Supplier**; **Manage Online Orders**; **Retail Sales Performance**.
### Other (not on Reports screen)
- **Client Directory** (search active/inactive clients), **Modify Tagged Clients** (bulk change indexes/types, add contact logs; Marketing + Tag clients), **Merge Duplicate Clients** (⚠️ Irreversible / financial — confirm with the user first; combines series, visit and payment history, contact logs, relationships, billing; you choose which contact info to keep) and **Locate Duplicate Clients**.
### Enterprise (Corporate)
- Need an enterprise login (via Technical Account Management): Sales, Clients, Gift Cards, Cross Regional Visits, Year-Over-Year Overview, Custom reports (run only; cannot create).
