# Sales report

Lists every sale by employee, service category, location and payment method, in summary (daily totals) or detail (each transaction). Use it to see what was sold, who rang it, and how it was paid.

## Where in Mindbody
- Direct link: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/app/business/Report/Sales/Sales?category=Sales
- Menu path: **Insights > Reports**, search "Sales" or go to **Sales > Sales**

## Steps
1. Open the report.
2. Set dates, filters, **View** and **Accounting Basis**.
3. Click **Go!**.
4. Optional: **Scheduled Report** (top right) opens the newer scheduled version; **Return to previous report** (top left) returns.

## Settings & fields
- **Dates**: type, calendar, or Quick Dates (calendar months, to-date, rolling averages).
- **Show tagged clients only**.
- **Sale location**: multi-select including online store; default All Locations.
- **Client home studio**: for multi-location/datashare sites; also splits online-store sales by client home.
- **Payment Method**, **Revenue Category**, **Entered by** (needs each employee's own login), **Sales rep** (needs a rep chosen at each sale).
- **Autopays**: Include, Exclude, or Autopays only (a contract's first payment is not an autopay).
- **View**: detail by default (client and item); summary gives totals for cash, checks, credit cards and Misc.
- **Accounting Basis**: accrual (first-run default, then remembers last used), cash, or accrual & cash combined (under **More**; shows non-cash-equivalent sales, account payments, $0 online sales, prepaid gift cards; tips only show in this mode).
- **Show supplier name**.
- Buttons: **Go!**, **Print this Report**, **Export to Excel**, **Export to PDF**, **Save this report**, **Tag add**, **Tag new**, **Untag clients**.
- Summary columns: Sale Date, Tickets Sold, Cash, Check, Credit Card, Misc., Total (with tax), Deposits section.
- Detail columns: Sale Date, Client, Sale ID (links to Manage Sales), Item Name (note icon for sale notes), Studio, Supplier, Notes, Color, Size, Item Price, Quantity (negative = return), Subtotal, Discount %, Discount Amount, Tax, Item Total, Total paid w/ payment method. Excel adds Client ID, Sale Notes, Payment Method.

## Gotchas
- Needs Sales/Sales by Category/Sales by Services (Reports) and View client account/purchase history (Clients); Detail view also needs Ability to view all clients. Availability depends on software package.
- Does not show how a card was processed (keyed, swiped, dipped).
- Split payments list the item once per payment method used.
- Values in parentheses are returns/refunds and reduce the daily total.
- Accrual basis dates a gift card at redemption; cash basis counts full gift card value at purchase and does not show what it was spent on. Assignable gift cards with remaining balance show in Deposits.
- Deposits section: an account payment shows there until the credit is spent; the later purchase is attributed to the original payment method regardless of date filters.
- Sales made with inactive payment methods only appear when "All payment methods" is selected.
- "Perkville" shows in the Payment Method list even if not set up.
- If **Go!** does nothing, there were no sales in the range.
- The Constant Contact button was retired June 1, 2020.
