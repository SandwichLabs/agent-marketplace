# The PR Method: brand sheet

## Look

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Near-black | `#0C0D0F` | Backgrounds, text pills |
| Bone | `#F2F0ED` | Main text on footage |
| PR orange | `#EA8E3E` | The one accent: the last word of a line, strike-throughs, the offer pill |
| Warm grey | `#B4ADA7` | Small secondary text |

- **Headline font:** Space Grotesk, Bold. ALL CAPS, tight letter spacing.
- **Body font:** DM Sans, Regular or Light, for quiet lines ("Just training that helps you").
- Both are free on Google Fonts. Install them on the computer before opening CapCut so they show up in its font
  list.
- The neon triangle logo goes at the end, never at the start.

## Voice

The studio's own line is the brief: **"Most gyms chase intensity. We focus on quality."** The edit can be intense; the
words sell intention, control and coaching. "One more rep, y'all!" works because it's encouragement, not punishment.

Lines straight from the studio (safe to use as is):

- Fitness that works with your body, not against it.
- Most gyms chase intensity. We focus on quality.
- No ego. No pressure. Just training that helps you live better.
- Move pain-free. Real, lasting strength.
- Small class sizes. Personalized cues and form coaching, every single session.
- Pain-free, capable, and confident at any age.
- ONE REP AT A TIME. (on the studio wall)
- PROGRESS OVER PERFECTION. (on the studio wall)
